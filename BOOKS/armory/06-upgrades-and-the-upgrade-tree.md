# Chapter Six — Upgrades and the Upgrade Tree

**Two systems live in this chapter: 164 armour components and lightsaber crystals, and a
further 143 weapon-specific upgrades.** Both were taken straight from the games' own item
files.

**An item marked *"Fully Upgradeable"* anywhere in this book** — every weapon and most
armour in Chapters Two through Five — **has slots that these components fill.**

## ⚠ The personal crystal — one item, forty-five rows

**Forty-five of the 104 crystal rows are a single crystal.** They are `qcrystal_1_0`
through `qcrystal_9_4`, and every one of them carries the same description:

> *“This crystal is bound to ‹your character›. **Its power reflects that of its master.**”*

**It is KOTOR 2's personal crystal**, and it exists in **nine power tiers across five
alignment bands** — very dark, dark, neutral, light, very light. **All forty-five rows cost
nothing and all are marked unique**, because it is not bought and there is only ever one.

**The three alignments are genuinely different items, not the same bonuses relabelled:**

| Band | What it favours |
|---|---|
| **Very dark · dark** | Strength and Charisma, Dark Side damage, and Massive Criticals |
| **Neutral** | Constitution and a spread across every score, plus improved resistance |
| **Light · very light** | Wisdom and Dexterity, Light Side damage |

**And it scales steeply.** At tier 1 the very-dark form gives Strength `+2`, Dexterity `+1`,
Charisma `+1`, Dark Side damage `1` and Massive Criticals `1d4`. **At tier 9 the same form
gives Strength `+7`, Dexterity `+5`, Charisma `+6`, an attack bonus of `+3`, and Dark Side
damage `5`.** The very-light form ends at Wisdom `+7`, Strength `+5`, Dexterity `+6` and the
same `+3` to attack.

> **A player never chooses between forty-five crystals. They carry one, and it becomes
> whichever of the forty-five they have earned.**

**⚠ One caveat about the table above.** All forty-five rows carry **Tier 1** in the data,
including the tier-9 forms. **That is plainly wrong for the upper tiers** — a crystal giving
`+7` to an ability is not tier-1 equipment — **but the tier column is what the source
records, and correcting it is not this catalogue's call.** The power tier is read from the
resref, not from that column.

## ⚠ Two rows in the crystal list are not crystals

**Both are named here rather than quietly dropped**, on the same principle Chapter Three
applies to the fifty rows it excludes.

**`g1_w_sbrcrstl20` — *"Door Cutting"***, whose entire description is *"Door Sabering"*. It
is the utility that lets a lightsaber open a door in a scripted scene. **It has no
properties and is not equipment.**

**`g1_w_sbrcrstl21` — *"+ dual strike"*, and it is a feat, not an item.** Its description
reads: *"Prerequisites: None. The character has advanced training using teamwork to defeat
enemies. When attacking an enemy that another party member is also attacking, the character
gets a +2 bonus to hit."* **That is a feat's text sitting on a crystal's resref**, priced at
25,000 credits. **A mis-filing in the game data, not a component anyone installs.**
