# Chapter Nine — Usable Items

**Things a character carries and spends.** Adrenals, medpacs, trap kits and crafting
materials — **active consumables rather than anything worn or wielded.**

**Taken straight from the games' own item files**, on the same footing as Chapters Five
through Eight.

---

## The six categories

**58 items**, 30 appearing in both games — the highest cross-game overlap of any category
in this book, consistent with these being everyday consumables rather than
setting-specific unique gear.

| Category | Count | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Adrenal** | 10 | Timed combat buffs |
| **Components** | 2 | Crafting materials — `Chemicals` and one more. These feed the crafting rules in Chapter Twelve |
| **Light-source** | 3 | — |
| **Medical** | 8 | Healing and status-curing consumables |
| **Spike** | 6 | Also crafting materials (`Parts`) |
| **Trap-kit** | 29 | Placed area-effect devices — the largest category here |

## ⚠ Medical items — where this game knowingly differs from the games

**This is the second place in the Armory where a value is deliberately not the source's
own**, the first being Chapter One's cap on Massive Criticals. **You will find different
numbers in the games, and the difference is on purpose.**

| Item | What the games do | **What this game does** |
|---|---|---|
| **Medpac** | 10 + Wisdom modifier + Treat Injury skill | **`2d8`** |
| **Advanced Medpac** | 20 + Wisdom + (2 × Treat Injury) | **`4d8`** |
| **Life Support Pack** | 30 + Wisdom + (3 × Treat Injury) | **`6d8`** |

**The change drops the Wisdom and skill dependency entirely — a medpac heals the same
amount no matter who uses it.**

**But it keeps the source's own proportions exactly.** The games triple both the base
healing and the skill multiplier from Medpac to Life Support Pack — 10 to 30, `×1` to
`×3`. **This game triples the average healing across the same three items**: `2d8` averages
9, `6d8` averages 27. **Different mechanic, same shape.**

> **A medpac is a medpac. In this game, who hands it to you does not change what it
> does.**

**Two medical items sit outside those three tiers.** **`Antidote Kit`** ends a poison
effect and restores attribute scores the poison had lowered. **`Squad Recovery Stim`** is
party-wide — **it heals everyone present for 10 vitality on activation**, which makes it a
different kind of thing from a medpac rather than a stronger one.

# The catalogue

**All 58 items, by category**, with resref, which game each comes from, tier, price, effect
and the item's own description.

## ⚠⚠ Read this before using the Effect column

**Four things about it are not obvious, and all four matter at a table.**

**⚠ 1 — Most of these effects are still the games' own numbers, not converted.** A stun
lasting *"9sec"*, a blast radius of *"3.3m"*, a mine dealing *"Piercing, 18pts"*, a save at
*"DC35"* — **those are video-game values, printed here as the games state them.** This game
runs on rounds, squares, dice and its own DC scale, and **the adrenals and the trap kits
have not been converted to any of them.**

> **They are recorded rather than converted, and saying so is the honest thing.** A
> Gamemaster can read a Deadly Frag Mine's 54 points and its DC 25 and judge the shape of
> it. **What they cannot do is treat those figures as this game's rules, because they are
> not.**

**⚠ 2 — The medpacs are the exception, and they show the converted value.** The three
medpac tiers carry **`2d8` / `4d8` / `6d8`**, per the table above — **not the games'
Wisdom-and-skill formula.** That conversion exists, so the catalogue uses it. **Where a
conversion does not exist, nothing was invented to fill the gap.**

**⚠ Five of those descriptions restate the games' formula in their own text**, because the
formula is written into the item's description as well as its properties. **The quote is
left intact and marked**, so that the live value and the superseded one are never confused.

**⚠ 3 — Where a description opens by repeating the same figures, that repetition is
trimmed.** Twenty-three of the game's descriptions begin with the identical stat block that
already sits in the Effect column, then continue into real description. **Only the repeated
opening was cut; everything the description actually says is quoted.**

**⚠ 4 — Three rows have no name in the source.** `ptar_rakghoulser`, `g_i_progspike003` and
`g_i_progspike02` are real rows with real resrefs whose names did not resolve. **The last of
the three carries a full description and a working effect**; the other two carry nothing but
a resref and a price of zero. **They are listed rather than quietly dropped, and no name has
been invented for any of them.**
