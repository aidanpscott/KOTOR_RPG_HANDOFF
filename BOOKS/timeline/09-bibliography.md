# Galactic Timeline — Sources and Credits

**Status: per `PT-1813`.** Scoped to what **this book** actually drew on. Other books in
this set used other sources and carry their own lists.

**⚠ Every entry is marked for verification status.** A credit page is the worst place in a
book to blur the line between what was checked and what was assumed, so entries verified
against a source held on this machine are marked **✔ verified**, and entries taken from
relay or general knowledge are marked **⚠ unverified** and should be checked against a
physical copy before this book is published anywhere.

## Primary source

### *Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide* (2008)

**Wizards of the Coast.** **✔ Verified** — read directly from the credits page, f. 3.

**Seven of this book's eight chapters rest on this volume**, and Chapters Three through
Seven are built almost entirely from its historical sections (ff. 5–6), its dated timeline
(ff. 112–113), and its character entries (ff. 139–210).

## Underlying works this book narrates without citing directly

### *Tales of the Jedi* — **Dark Horse Comics**

Rank 3 in the hierarchy. **⚠ Unverified — no creative credits for this series appear in
any source held on this machine.**

**An honest note about scope.** Chapter Three is titled *The Tales of the Jedi Era* and
tells that series' story — Exar Kun, Ulic Qel-Droma, the Krath, Nomi Sunrider, the Cron
Cluster, Yavin 4. **But every citation in that chapter is to the Campaign Guide.** The
comics themselves were not consulted, because they are not held here.

**They are credited anyway, and deliberately.** The events of one of this book's eight
chapters are that series' invention, and reaching them through a licensed summary does not
make them less so. **The creative team deserves the credit even where the citation went
through someone else's book** — and this entry is marked unverified so that whoever
finishes this page can name them properly rather than leaving it at the publisher.

## Consulted by relay only

### *The New Essential Chronology* (2005) — **Daniel Wallace**

Rank 6. **⚠ Unverified — the book is not staged on this machine and its credits page has
not been read.** The attribution above is as relayed by MAIN. **⚠ If the volume has a
co-author, this entry is incomplete**, and it should be checked against a physical copy
before publication.

**Three claims in this entire book rest on it**, all in Chapter Two and all marked in that
chapter's text: the Force-negating plague that ended the Infinite Empire and its
approximate date, the Jedi Order's establishment on Tython, and the Battle of Corbos. The
book was **not staged**; those three facts were researched and relayed.
