# THE FORCE CLASSES

**⚠ `PT-997`. ⚠⚠ THE PLAYER-FACING CHAPTER FOR THE SIX FORCE BASE CLASSES — ⚠ THE COMPANION TO `CLASSES-STANDARD-PHB`, WHICH CARRIES THE THIRTEEN STANDARD ONES.**

> **⚠ `CLASS-ROSTER-01` FLAGGED ⚠⚠ **TWO** CLASSES AS LACKING A PLAYER-FACING ENTRY. ⚠ `PT-996` FOUND IT WAS ⚠⚠ **SIX** — ⚠ THE WHOLE FORCE TIER HAD NO CHAPTER.**

**⚠ NOTHING HERE IS NEW DESIGN. ⚠⚠ EVERY VALUE IS ASSEMBLED FROM `CLASS-TABLES-JEDI`, `CLASS-TABLES-AUTHORED`, `SKILLS-01 §9.2`, `CLASS-ATTACKS-01` AND `FEATS-LIBRARY-01`.**

## How to read a class

**⚠ SAME SHAPE AS `CLASSES-STANDARD-PHB`: ⚠ A LINE ON WHAT THE CLASS **IS**, ⚠ THE RECORD, ⚠ THE CLASS SKILLS, ⚠⚠ AND THE CLASS FEATURE.**

**⚠ AND ONE THING THE STANDARD CLASSES DO NOT HAVE: ⚠⚠ A **FORCE DIE**. ⚠ IT IS THE SECOND NUMBER THAT DEFINES A FORCE CLASS, AND IT RUNS **OPPOSITE** TO THE HIT DIE.**

    ⚠ `Jedi Guardian`  ⚠ HIT `d10` · ⚠⚠ FORCE `d4`
    ⚠ `Jedi Sentinel`  ⚠ HIT `d8`  · ⚠⚠ FORCE `d6`
    ⚠ `Jedi Consular`  ⚠ HIT `d6`  · ⚠⚠ FORCE `d8`

> **⚠ ⚠⚠ THE BLADE AND THE FORCE TRADE AGAINST EACH OTHER. ⚠ THAT IS THE WHOLE FORCE TIER IN ONE LINE.**

### *The blade first.*

You went to the practice floor and stayed there. The Force is real and you can feel it, but you learned it the way you learned everything else — **by doing it until it worked**. Others in the Order will out-think you and out-sense you. None of them will out-fight you.

> **Puts himself between the enemy and the person who cannot fight**
