## 0a · ⚠ BACKTRACKING — a later model may become an earlier one. `PT-589`

**Owner ruling, AMENDING `PT-574`'s exclusions.**

> **⚠ A SWTOR OR PREQUEL MODEL MAY BE BACKTRACKED INTO AN ERA-APPROPRIATE ONE.** **The DESIGN travels; ⚠ THE NAME MUST CHANGE.**

### ⚠ THE SETTING ALREADY DOES THIS, AND THAT IS WHY IT IS SAFE

    ⚠ C-3PO      Cybot Galactica, 32 BBY     the LATER one
    ⚠ A-3DO      Duwani, Tales of the Jedi   ⚠ THE SAME DROID, 4,000 YEARS EARLIER

**⚠ WOOKIEEPEDIA CARRIES BOTH. The `3DO-series` IS the backtrack, and Lucasfilm made it — a protocol droid of the Old Republic with a designation one letter off the famous one.**

### The rules

    ⚠ THE NAME CHANGES.       A backtracked model is NOT called what the later
                              one was called.
    ⚠ THE MAKER CHANGES       to an era company — Duwani, Czerka, Aratech.
      where the later one's does not exist yet.
    ⚠ THE SOURCE IS RECORDED AS INSPIRATION, NOT AS WARRANT.
      *"Backtracked from X"* — never *"attested as X."*
    ⚠ NO IMPERIAL MODELS. Owner ruling. The prequel era is permitted;
      the Empire is not.

> **⚠⚠ THE AMENDMENT — `PT-590`. FOR DROID MODELS, BACKTRACKING IS THE **ONLY** PERMITTED FORM OF INVENTION.**

**⚠ `PT-574` said *"the pass should NOT invent mechanical differences the sources do not give."*** **`PT-589` opened a door and `PT-590` states its exact width:**

    ⚠ a droid model MAY be BACKTRACKED from a later attested one
    ⚠ a droid model MAY NOT be invented from NOTHING

**⚠ EVERY MODEL ON THIS ROSTER IS THEREFORE EITHER ATTESTED IN OUR ERA OR TRACEABLE TO A REAL LATER MODEL.** **There is NO THIRD CATEGORY, and a name with no line back to a source does not belong here.**

**⚠ IT IS NARROWER THAN IT LOOKS.** **A backtrack needs a DONOR — a real model, a movable designation, and a maker that either exists in our era or has an era counterpart.** **`ID9` and `ID10` failed that test and were left alone.**

**⚠ AND IT IS MARKED. Every backtracked model carries `⚠ BACKTRACKED` and names what it came from, so nobody later mistakes it for an attested one.**

## 0b · ⚠ A KIOSK STOCKS THE CURRENT MARK ONLY — `PT-592`

> **⚠ A DROID MERCHANT SELLS THE HIGHEST MARK THAT EXISTS IN THE CAMPAIGN'S ERA. NOTHING BELOW IT IS STOCK.**

**⚠ IT APPLIES TO MARKS OF ONE LINE — `War Droid Mark I–V`, `KM1 Mark I–II`, `Assault Droid Mark I–IV`.**

### ⚠⚠ THE HK LINE IS THE EXCEPTION — `PT-593`, owner ruling

> **⚠ HK MODELS ARE DISTINGUISHED BY MODEL, NOT BY MARK.**

**⚠ `HK-24`, `HK-48` and `HK-49` are NOT Mark I, II and III of one product.** **They are SEPARATE MODELS, each with its own window, and `PT-589`'s era locks are what govern them — NOT this section.**

**⚠ WHERE TWO HK MODELS' WINDOWS OVERLAP, BOTH ARE STOCK.** **A superseded MARK is salvage; a DIFFERENT MODEL is not superseded at all.**

**⚠ `PT-592` said this rule was *"the HK rule generalised."* IT IS NOT. The HK line is where it does NOT apply.**

### ⚠ AN OLDER MARK IS SALVAGE, NOT STOCK

    ⚠ NOT FOR SALE NEW    a superseded mark is not on a kiosk at any price
    ⚠ STILL IN THE WORLD  found, looted, inherited, bought secondhand,
                          or already owned when the campaign starts
    ⚠ STILL PRICED        the frame prices in this document stand — they are
                          what one is WORTH, not what a shop asks

> **⚠ THE LADDER SURVIVES AS A SECONDHAND MARKET.** **A `War Droid Mark I` at 1,900 credits still exists; you cannot ORDER one.**

**⚠ AND A STARTING DROID MASTER IS NOT PRICED OUT.** **`PT-566` gives the FIRST DROID FREE — chassis of your choice — so the current mark being dear does not gate entry to the class.**

**⚠ THE CAMPAIGN PACKAGE DECIDES WHICH MARK IS CURRENT.** **`DEATH-AND-DIFFICULTY-01 §3` already lets a package set exceptions; this is one.**

## 8 · ⚠ Two anomalies in the source data

## 9 · ⚠ What a model IS and IS NOT

> **⚠ A MODEL IS A NAME, A PRICE AND A SOURCE. IT IS NOT A NEW STAT BLOCK.**

**⚠ `PT-574` required this: *"the pass should NOT invent mechanical differences the sources do not give."***

**A droid's mechanics come from its CHASSIS — `PT-567` for attacks and combat limits, `PT-568` for credits, `PT-577` for the upgrade cap.** **The MODEL sets the frame price and tells you what you are looking at.**

**⚠ THE ONE EXCEPTION IS THE FRAME PRICE, which varies within a chassis** — a `War Droid Mark I` at 1,900 and a `Mark V` at 4,200 are the same chassis at different points of a ladder the source itself built.
