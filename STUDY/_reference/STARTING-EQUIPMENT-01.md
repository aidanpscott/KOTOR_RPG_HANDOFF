## 1 · ⚠⚠ THREE ROUTES, AND TWO ARE THE PLAYER'S CHOICE

    ⚠ 1  THE ASSORTMENT   ⚠ the recommended array for your class, plus
                          whatever credits are left over
    ⚠ 2  THE PURSE        ⚠ the array's credit value, spent freely.
                          ⚠ WHATEVER IS LEFT, YOU KEEP.
    ⚠ 3  ⚠ THE GM DECIDES ⚠ and a campaign package may impose it

**⚠ ROUTES 1 AND 2 ARE THE PLAYER'S TO PICK. ⚠ ROUTE 3 OVERRIDES BOTH.**

### ⚠⚠ THE ARRAY IS WORTH MORE THAN THE PURSE — `PT-728`, owner ruling

> **⚠ `PT-724` SAID THE TWO ROUTES WERE *"exactly equal in worth."* ⚠ THE OWNER CHANGED THAT, AND IT IS THE BETTER DESIGN.**

**⚠ THE ARRAY MAY CONTAIN ITEMS THE PURSE COULD NOT AFFORD. ⚠ THAT IS THE REASON TO TAKE IT.**

    ⚠ ENGINEER   ⚠ 2 × Computer Spike = 500cr OF CONSUMABLES ALONE
    ⚠ MACHINIST  ⚠ a frag grenade at 100cr that no purse would prioritise

**⚠ A PLAYER WHO TAKES THE PURSE GETS **FREEDOM**. ⚠ A PLAYER WHO TAKES THE ARRAY GETS **VALUE**. ⚠ NEITHER IS STRICTLY BETTER, AND THAT IS WHAT MAKES IT A CHOICE RATHER THAN A CALCULATION.**

**⚠ IF THE TWO WERE EQUAL, ⚠ ROUTE 1 WOULD BE A CONVENIENCE AND NOTHING ELSE.**

**⚠ A PLAYER WHO WANTS A **Stealth Field Generator** AT 75cr BUYS ONE AND EATS THE DIFFERENCE ELSEWHERE. ⚠ THAT IS THE POINT.**

### ⚠⚠ AND KOTOR 2 IS A ROUTE-3 PACKAGE

**⚠ THE EXILE BEGINS STRIPPED ON PERAGUS. ⚠ A PACKAGE THAT OPENS THAT WAY CANNOT OFFER A PURSE, AND `LOOT-01 §5`'s EXPLICIT OVERRIDE IS THE MECHANISM.**

### ⚠ Three conversion rules, applied to every row

    ⚠ 1  ⚠ MELEE IS DROPPED — `PT-567`. ⚠ SIX CLASSES LOSE A HALF;
         ⚠ `Machinist` AND `Duelist` LOSE THEIR ONLY WEAPON AND
         SUBSTITUTE A **Blaster Pistol**.
    ⚠ 2  ⚠⚠ ADRENALS ARE DROPPED — ⚠ A DROID HAS NO BLOODSTREAM.
         ⚠ WHERE THE ADRENAL WAS THE **WHOLE** CONSUMABLE, A
         **Sensor Probe** REPLACES IT.
    ⚠ 3  ⚠ MEDPACS BECOME **Repair Kit**s AT THE SAME COUNT —
         ⚠ INCLUDING THE `Medic`'s FOUR.

### ⚠⚠ THE DROID `Medic` IS THE ONE EXCEPTION TO RULE 3 — `PT-771`

> **⚠ IT KEEPS ⚠ ALL FOUR MEDPACS **AND** GETS ⚠ 2 **Repair Kit**s ON TOP.**

**⚠ OWNER RULING: ⚠ A DROID `Medic` IS A ⚠ MEDICAL DROID — ⚠ IT SPECIALISES IN HEALING **ORGANICS**, AND ITS ORGANIC KIT IS THE POINT OF THE CLASS RATHER THAN A CASUALTY OF THE CHASSIS.**

**⚠ `PT-770` CONVERTED ITS FOUR MEDPACS INTO FOUR REPAIR KITS BY RULE 3. ⚠ THAT WAS MECHANICAL AND WRONG — ⚠ IT DELETED THE ENTIRE CLASS.**

    ⚠ 4 MEDPACS · 1 ADVANCED MEDPAC · 1 ANTIDOTE KIT
      ⚠⚠ FOR THE PARTY — the droid cannot use one of them
    ⚠ 2 REPAIR KITS
      ⚠ FOR ITSELF, AND FOR ANY OTHER DROID IN THE PARTY

**⚠⚠ IT IS THE ONLY DROID ARRAY THAT CARRIES BOTH KINDS OF MEDICINE, AND ⚠ THE RICHEST ARRAY IN THE GAME AT 628cr OF CONSUMABLES.**

> **⚠ `PT-746` SAID THE `Medic` CARRIES MEDPACS **FOR OTHERS**. ⚠ THE DROID VERSION IS THAT SENTENCE WITH NOTHING LEFT OVER.**

### ⚠ Why the **Sensor Probe**

**⚠ `g_i_drdtrgcom004` · ⚠ 500cr · ⚠ GATED BEHIND `Droid Upgrade 1`, WHICH ⚠ EVERY DROID HAS AT LEVEL 1 — `PT-616`.**

> **⚠⚠ 500cr IS THE MOST EXPENSIVE CONSUMABLE IN ANY ARRAY EXCEPT THE `Engineer`'s SPIKES. ⚠ THAT IS DELIBERATE: ⚠ IT IS THE ONLY THING IN THE `sensor` CATEGORY A DROID CAN ACTUALLY USE THAT IS NOT A TARGETING COMPUTER.**

**⚠ AND IT MAKES THE THREE ADRENAL-REPLACED ARRAYS THE **RICHEST** DROID ARRAYS, WHICH OFFSETS `PT-724`'s FINDING THAT DROIDS RUN 20–65cr LIGHT.**

**⚠ THREE CLASSES LOST THEIR ENTIRE CONSUMABLE WHEN THE ADRENAL WENT. ⚠ A PROBE IS WHAT A DROID WOULD CARRY INSTEAD OF A STIM.**

### ⚠⚠ NO STARTING SHIELD, AND THE SLOT ANALYSIS IS WHY

**⚠ `PT-712` CLAIMED A DROID HAS **MORE SLOTS** THAN AN ORGANIC. ⚠ IT DOES NOT — `PT-724` CHECKED THE BIT MASKS.**

    ⚠ bit 0   HEAD    Mask · ⚠ Droid sensors and scopes
    ⚠ bit 1   ARMOUR  Robes · ⚠ Droid plating
    ⚠ bit 3   HANDS   Gauntlets · ⚠ Droid spike mounts
    ⚠⚠ 7 · 8 ARMS    Forearm Bands · ⚠⚠ DROID UTILITY DEVICES
    ⚠ bit 9   IMPLANT shared
    ⚠⚠ bit 10 BELT    Belt · Stealth Unit · ⚠⚠ DROID SHIELD

> **⚠ THERE ARE ⚠ ZERO DROID-ONLY SLOTS. ⚠ A DROID USES A SUBSET.**

**⚠ BUT IT CONVERTS ITS NON-COMBAT SLOTS INTO COMBAT ONES: ⚠ AN ORGANIC'S ARMS HOLD PASSIVE BANDS; ⚠ A DROID'S HOLD WEAPONS.**

**⚠⚠ SO THE 65cr GAP IS NOT A DISADVANTAGE — ⚠ IT IS THE DISCOUNT FOR GEAR THAT DOES MORE. ⚠ A FREE SHIELD ON TOP WOULD OVER-CORRECT A GAP THAT IS NOT ONE.**

**⚠ THE BELT SLOT STAYS OPEN, AND THE DROID BUYS ITS OWN SHIELD WHEN IT WANTS ONE.**
