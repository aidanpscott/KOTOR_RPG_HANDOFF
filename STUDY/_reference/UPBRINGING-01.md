## 1 · ⚠⚠ IT GRANTS NOTHING. THAT IS THE DESIGN.

> **⚠ NO SKILL. NO ITEM. NO CREDITS. ⚠ AN UPBRINGING IS WHO YOU WERE BEFORE THE GAME AND IT PAYS NOTHING FOR IT.**

**⚠ `PT-694` MADE IT APTITUDE #2. ⚠ `PT-705` TAKES THAT BACK — ⚠ APTITUDE #2 BELONGS TO `PROFESSIONS-01`, WHICH IS WHAT `SKILLS-01 §11.3` SAID BEFORE I MOVED IT.**

    ⚠ HOMEWORLD    aptitude #1 · ⚠ PLUS a `+2` skill bonus
    ⚠ PROFESSION   ⚠ APTITUDE #2 — or the grant instead
    ⚠⚠ UPBRINGING  ⚠ NOTHING

### ⚠ Why nothing is the right answer

**⚠ A CREDIT LADDER WAS PROPOSED AND THE OWNER KILLED IT IN ONE LINE: *"I'd imagine everyone would just take Noble."***

> **⚠⚠ AN ALL-UPSIDE LIST HAS NO TEXTURE. ⚠ THE ONLY FIX WAS TO MAKE EVERY OPTION A TRADE, AND ⚠ THAT IS A THIRD SYSTEM TO MAINTAIN FOR EIGHT WORDS OF COLOUR.**

**⚠ AND AS A SKILL SOURCE IT WAS HOMEWORLD'S WEAKER TWIN — SAME CURRENCY, LESS OF IT.**

**⚠ WITH NO GRANT THERE IS NOTHING TO OPTIMISE, ⚠ WHICH MEANS A PLAYER PICKS THE ONE THEY WANT TO BE.**

## 3 · ⚠⚠ DROIDS DO NOT TAKE AN UPBRINGING — `PT-707`, owner ruling

> **⚠ NOT SOME. NOT SEVEN OF NINE. ⚠ NONE.**

**⚠ `PT-694` AND `PT-706` BOTH ARGUED DROIDS COULD TAKE ONE, ON THE GROUND THAT *"a production run IS a condition of manufacture."* ⚠ THE OWNER OVERRULED IT TWICE OVER.**

### ⚠ And the consistent reading is his

**⚠ `PT-692` CLOSED PROFESSIONS TO DROIDS: *"a droid has no childhood — IT HAS A PRODUCTION RUN."***

**⚠ I THEN CARVED AN EXCEPTION FOR UPBRINGING BY REDEFINING THE PRODUCTION RUN AS A CHILDHOOD. ⚠ THAT IS THE SAME ARGUMENT `PT-692` REJECTED, RUN BACKWARDS.**

> **⚠⚠ A DROID IS NOT RAISED. ⚠ `Noble`, `Bastard`, `Orphan`, `Slave`, `Peasant` ALL DESCRIBE A CHILDHOOD, AND A DROID DOES NOT HAVE ONE TO DESCRIBE.**

**⚠ THE METAPHORS WERE GOOD — A RESTRAINING BOLT FOR `Slave`, AN UNLICENSED COPY FOR `Bastard`. ⚠ BEING ABLE TO WRITE A METAPHOR IS NOT THE SAME AS THE CATEGORY APPLYING.**

### ⚠ What a droid has instead

    ⚠ ITS CHASSIS   one of four playable — `Astromech`, `Assassin`,
                    `Battle`, `Remote`
    ⚠ ITS MODEL     ⚠ 29 MODEL FEATS — `PT-610`
    ⚠ `junk droid`  ⚠ *"pieced together from other droids' parts"* — `PT-609`

**⚠⚠ THREE INPUTS THAT SAY WHO A DROID IS, AND NONE OF THEM BORROWS A HUMAN WORD.**

### ⚠ So the creation sheets diverge completely

    ⚠ ORGANIC   species · homeworld · ⚠ UPBRINGING · ⚠ PROFESSION · class
    ⚠ DROID     chassis · model · ⚠ NEITHER · class

**⚠ A DROID CHARACTER IS BUILT FROM WHAT IT **IS**. ⚠ AN ORGANIC IS BUILT FROM WHAT HAPPENED TO IT.**
