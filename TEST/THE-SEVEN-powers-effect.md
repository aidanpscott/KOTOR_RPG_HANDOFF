## 1. Beast Control

`marks: citation` · `tier 1` · `cost 6` · `targets ['beast']`

```
Beast Control distracts a creature or beast, making it easier to sneak by them. If the target fails a Will save of DC 5 + the attacking character's level + Wisdom modifier + Charisma modifier they are distracted for 10 rounds. Distracted targets will not notice the player unless the player gets too close or interacts with them. ⚠ Renamed from Beast Control, and the DC corrected: our entry dropped the two ability modifiers the source states. PT-461 recorded that omission as deliberate; it was a transcription loss.
```

## 2. Death Field

`marks: citation` · `tier 2` · `cost 20` · `targets None`

```
Death Field targets all enemies within a 10-metre radius — 5 squares, draining life to heal the attacking Jedi. The damage is 1d4 per Force level to 20, then 1d4 per two levels — maximum 20d4, cap lowered at PT-472. A Fortitude save made at a DC of 5 + the attacking character's level + the attacking character's Wisdom and Charisma modifiers reduces the damage by half. The character is healed the equivalent of the most damage dealt to any single enemy. This power does not affect droids.
```

## 3. Drain Life

`marks: citation` · `tier 1` · `cost 10` · `targets None`

```
This power allows the Jedi to drain the life of others to heal him or herself. 1d4 per Force level to 20, then 1d4 per two levels — maximum 15d4, cap lowered at PT-471 and again at PT-472. ⚠ The source gives Drain Life and Death Field the SAME damage line; ours no longer does. Owner ruling. A successful Fortitude save at a DC of 5 + the attacking character's level + the attacking character's Wisdom and Charisma modifiers reduces damage by half. This power does not affect droids.
```

## 4. Force Choke

`marks: citation` · `tier 2` · `cost 14` · `targets None`

```
This power constricts the throat of a target at range, stunning and inflicting 1d6 per Force level to 20, then 1d6 per two levels — maximum 25d6, each round — PT-301 for the duration (2 rounds). The target also suffers a temporary (8 rounds) -4 penalty to Constitution, Dexterity and Strength. A successful Fortitude save at DC 5 + the attacking character's level + the attacking character's Wisdom and Charisma modifiers results in no effect. This power does not affect droids.
```

Heat builds inside the victim until it erupts. Damage equal to the attacking character's level. A Fortitude save at DC 5 + the attacking character's level + Wisdom and Charisma modifiers negates. ⚠ The source adds "the victim may also suffer a temporary bout of insanity" — DROPPED. ## 6. Force Strangle

```
This power triggers spasms in the victim's lungs, causing great pain and inflicting 1d6 per Force level to 20, then 1d6 per two levels — maximum 25d6, each round — PT-301 for the duration (2 rounds), and the target is stunned for that duration — 2 rounds. ⚠ AUTHORED. This power does not affect droids.
```

An ancient Dark Side power. The caster draws dark side energy from within, concentrates it into near-physical form and hurls it like a spear. One target. 1d10 per Force level, maximum 30d10. A Fortitude save at DC 5 + the attacking character's level + Wisdom and Charisma modifiers halves the damage. ⚠ SINGLE TARGET ONLY — that is the whole caveat.
