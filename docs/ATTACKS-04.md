**`Shoot` has no level and no gate. Every character has it.**

### The reaction pool

> **One pool. Every reaction chain draws on it.**

**Uses per encounter = the lower of your highest reaction tier and your ability allowance.**

| Allowance | Uses |
|---|---|
| **Dexterity modifier** | +1 -> **1** * +3 -> **2** * +5 -> **3** |
| **Base attack bonus** | +1 -> **1** * +6 -> **2** * +11 -> **3** |

**Take the better of the two.** **A character with neither has no reactions at all, whatever they hold.**

> **Three reaction chains exist across the rosters** -- **Snap Shot** and **Overwatch** in ranged, **Parry** in melee. **Holding all three buys options, not volume.**

### Stealth damage is no longer here

***Stealthy Shot* and the melee stealth chain were attack declarations and are now the **`Sneak Attack`** feat chain in `FEATS-LIBRARY-01`.**

> **A stealth opener was one attack competing against Velocity's three, and lost to `Shoot`.** **As a rider it attaches to whatever you declare.** **`PLAYTEST-RULINGS-01 §C29`.**

### Why the disarm chain gates latest

**5 / 8 / 14, and Dexterity 13.**

> **Sunder destroys a weapon permanently** — the only ranged attack that removes equipment from the game. **Level 14 prices that, and level 5 on the root stops a low-level character disarming everything they meet.**

**Each tier adds an *option* rather than replacing the outcome.** **At Sunder you may still simply disarm.**
