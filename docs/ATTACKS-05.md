| **Precision** | Critical Strike | Same |
| **Power** | Power Attack | Same |
| **Position** | Quick Attack | ⚠ **`PT-333`. Melee closes RANKS where ranged closes DISTANCE.** Both ask *where do I stand*. |

| **Support** | Guarding Stance | **Bodyguarding replaces suppression.** You cannot pin a corridor with a blade; **you can stand in front of someone.** |
| **Reaction** | Parry | **Parry-riposte replaces opportunity fire.** The most natural reaction in the game. |

### The reaction pool

> **One pool. Every reaction chain draws on it.**

**Uses per encounter = the lower of your highest reaction tier and your ability allowance.**

| Allowance | Uses |
|---|---|
| **Dexterity modifier** | +1 -> **1** * +3 -> **2** * +5 -> **3** |
| **Base attack bonus** | +1 -> **1** * +6 -> **2** * +11 -> **3** |

**Take the better of the two.** **A character with neither has no reactions at all, whatever they hold.**

> **Three reaction chains exist across the rosters** -- **Snap Shot** and **Overwatch** in ranged, **Parry** in melee. **Holding all three buys options, not volume.**

### The four ability gates

**Strength 12 on Power Attack** — **d20's own prerequisite.** 3.5 requires Strength 12 for Power Attack.

**Strength 12 on Sweep Attack** — **its opposed roll does not inherently use Strength**, so without the gate a Dexterity build sweeps as well as a Wookiee.

**Dexterity 13 on Flurry** — speed, and the mirror of Power Attack.

**Both on Disarming Strike.** **The only chain requiring two abilities** — you need the leverage to break their grip and the hands to catch what falls.

> **Thirteen is d20's standard threshold**, the first score giving a +1 modifier. **Low enough that any melee character clears it if they care.**

**Four gates against eleven chains leaves seven open to anyone.** **A Guardian at Strength 16 / Dexterity 10 loses Flurry and Disarming Strike; a Smuggler at Dexterity 16 / Strength 10 loses Power Attack, Sweep Attack, and Disarming Strike.**
