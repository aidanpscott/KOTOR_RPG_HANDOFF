# ATTACKS-06 — The Lightsaber Roster

**Seven forms, two chains each. 14 chains, 42 entries.**

> **Every lightsaber attack requires the matching form's feat.** **A Jedi holding one form has six of these entries available, not forty-two.**

**⚠ CORRECTED by `PT-189`.** **This read *"you hold one form at a time"* and that stopped being true when the stance was cut.**

    forms held    entries available
    1              6
    3             18     every Force base class by level 12
    6             36     a form-master at 30

**⚠ The gate still exists — you cannot use a form's entries without its feat — it is no longer *one*.**

**Which is what the ruling intends: a form is an unlock, so holding more unlocks more.** **The roster's size is justified by the acquisition schedule rather than by exclusivity** — **three forms by level 12, `PT-185`, and the rest taught.**

**Lore from Wookieepedia, Legends continuity.** `source_rank: 3`. **Mechanics authored.** **Every tier replaces the one below it** — see `ATTACKS-01 §3`.

---

## How forms are acquired — deferred to the class workstream

**Forms are feats *and* stances — `PT-185`, `FORMS-01 §2.2`.** **The feat unlocks the form's attacks permanently; the stance gives its bonuses and you are in one at a time.** **⚠ This sentence previously read *"Forms are feats"* and contradicted `FORMS-01 §2` in the act of citing it.**

> **A Jedi acquires a form two ways — by levelling, and by being taught.** A master, a holocron, an enemy who teaches by defeating you. **Which classes grant which forms, on what schedule, and what training in play looks like, belongs to the class workstream.**

**`FORMS-01 §2` records that forms have no prerequisites and no tier marking in the source** — **the numbering is nominal, not a chain.** Whatever granted them in KOTOR is in a table we do not hold.

---

## Why the form gate is the whole design

**A lightsaber user already has every melee chain.** Flurry, Critical Strike, Power Attack, Sneak Attack, Parry — **all of it, with a lightsaber in hand.**

> **This is an eighth axis on top, not a parallel set.**

**Forty-two entries looks large against melee's thirty-one — until you count what a character can reach.** **Six.** **Switching forms is the cost of switching attacks**, which makes the form choice a decision every encounter rather than a one-time pick.

# The forms answer each other

**Two chains encode a relationship the lore states directly.**

> **Makashi exists to counter Shii-Cho.** *"Form II's primary purpose was to serve as a counter to the first form… a heavy focus on protecting one's weapon to avoid being disarmed, the primary goal of Form I."*

**So Form I's second chain is disarming, and Form II's first makes you immune to it.** **A Determination duellist meeting a Contention duellist loses their opening move.**

**And Niman's capstone is the form's own definition.** **Unity of Form lets you declare any Form I–V attack you know without holding that form** — which is literally what *"combining Forms I through V"* means.

# Three things this roster does that nothing else does

**Ataru spends Force points.** *"Intensive use of the Force… extremely exhausting."* **Hawk-Bat Swoop costs 1 per use; Saber Swarm costs 2.** **The only attacks in three rosters that draw on the Force pool.**

> **Force points spent on an attack do not degrade the ceiling.** **Degradation in `FORCE-POOL-01` is defined per power tier, and an attack has no tier** — the rule would name no number. **An Ataru duellist runs dry; they do not shrink their maximum.**

**And Saber Swarm at 2 points a round is a real constraint.** A Velocity chain is declared most rounds, **so an Ataru duellist is spending from the pool for an entire fight.**

**Niman breaks the declaration rule.** `ATTACKS-01 §2` establishes one declaration per round, **and that a Force power is an attack.** **Guided Strike is the only entry permitted two.**

### The Ferocity drift rule

**Juyo runs on anger. Whether that costs you depends on where you already are.**

**`ALIGNMENT-01` has seven bands. The rule maps to them exactly:**

| Band | Drift per encounter in which a Ferocity attack is declared |
|---|---|
| **Deep Light** · **Committed Light** · **Leaning Light** | **2 points** |
| **Neutral** | **1 point** |
| **Leaning Dark** · **Committed Dark** · **Deep Dark** | **None** |

*Darth Maul "was so immersed in the dark side, yet so much in control of his anger that he could employ his own deadly variant of Juyo without fear."*

> **The rule is self-accelerating and that is deliberate — but it needs a floor.** A light-band Jedi using Ferocity drifts toward the bands where it costs less, and eventually to where it costs nothing. **`ALIGNMENT-01` already carried a defect of this shape — a permanent zero-drift hole. This is its mirror: a permanent drift slope with an absorbing state at the bottom.**
>
> **The floor: drift from this rule alone cannot carry a character past the Leaning Dark threshold.** **Reaching Committed Dark or beyond requires choices made in play, not a form used in combat.** *A Jedi who fights with Juyo becomes someone who is slipping. Becoming Sith is still something you do, not something a lightsaber form does to you.*

> **The lore is explicit that the form requires the emotion, not merely permits it.** *"Juyo not only employed a fiercely aggressive offense but also necessitated that practitioners actively harness their anger and negative emotions to fuel the constant attack."*
>
> **And that drawing on it is what costs a Jedi.** *"Because Form VII drew on a negative emotional state, drawing so heavily and continuously on the Force brought the practitioner perilously close to the dark side."*

**A Sith pays nothing. A Jedi pays most.** **The form is equally deadly for both** — **what differs is what it does to you.**

**This is the only place in three rosters where alignment changes what an attack costs**, and it applies at the chain level rather than per tier.

**And both Ferocity chains may be declared at any intensity you have unlocked.** **Under `ATTACKS-01 §3` a tier replaces the one below it — which for a chain whose cost rises would leave a level-12 master unable to fight at low intensity.** **The Defence penalty is therefore flat across all three tiers, and the escalation carries the difference.**

# Corrections made against the original sketch

**Vaapad is cut.** **Mace Windu created it roughly 3,900 years after the campaign date.** **Form VII is Juyo.** *Same contamination discipline the project applies to SWTOR-era material, running the other direction — not a source published too late, but a technique invented too late.*

**Returning blaster bolts moved from Soresu to Shien.** An earlier draft gave Form III a Mirror Attack. **The lore is explicit that returning bolts to their origin is Form V's.** **Soresu blocks; Shien returns.** **Form III's chains are now attrition and raw deflection.**

**Juyo faces one opponent, not many.** An earlier draft had its capstone taunting every enemy in sight. **Form VII is *"notable for its primary focus on facing a single opponent."*** **Both chains now reward staying on one target.**

**Djem So is not here.** **Shien is the classical Form V variant and the one KOTOR names.** **Djem So was developed later**, and nothing establishes it exists by 3956 BBY.

# Open

**Five attacks in three rosters deal no damage** — **Covering Fire, Disarming Shot, Disarming Slash, Disarming Strike, Guarding Stance.** *Search scope: every entry in `ATTACKS-04`, `05`, and `06` whose effect text states no damage. Interpose and Bulwark inherit the property from Guarding Stance rather than restating it.*

**Worth watching against the class-grant schedule**, since a class granted only non-damaging attacks would be strange.

**And Form I's Disarming Slash has no ability prerequisite** where melee's Disarming Strike requires Strength 12 *and* Dexterity 13 at level 5. **Deliberate — the form supplies the training** — but **the lightsaber route to disarming is cheaper and earlier than the mundane one.**

**Multi-target is no longer lightsaber-only.** **`Cleave` in melee and `Spray` in ranged** close a gap the lightsaber roster revealed rather than created: **before them, no non-Force character could strike two enemies with one declaration at any level.** *Flurry, Rapid Fire, Dual Strike, and Saber Swarm all give more attacks — which is the same target more often, not more targets.*
