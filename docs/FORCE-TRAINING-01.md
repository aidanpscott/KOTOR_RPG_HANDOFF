# FORCE-TRAINING-01 — Who will teach you

**Settled except two authoring jobs, both named in `§6`.**

**Depends on:** `FORCE-AWAKENING-01` (confirmation must have happened), `SKILL-RESOLUTION-01` (the DC ladder), `ALIGNMENT-01 v2` (the dark-side cost in `§7`).
**Unblocks:** the three Jedi base classes — Guardian, Sentinel, Consular — as playable.
**Candidate warrant for:** `MULTICLASS-01 §3.3`'s Force-class gate.

---

## 1. Knowing is not training

**`FORCE-AWAKENING-01` answers *what am I*. This document answers *who will teach me*.**

> **A character can walk out of a temple knowing exactly what they are, with nobody willing to teach them.**

**That is Jolee. That is half of KOTOR 2.** **The Jedi classes are gated here, not there.**

---

## 2. Asking a Jedi

**After confirmation, a character may ask the Force-user who confirmed them for training.**

**One `Persuade` roll. Three bands, on `SKILL-RESOLUTION-01`'s ladder.**

| Result | DC | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Fail** | under 25 | **No. Find someone else** |
| **Partial** | **25 — Formidable** | **No — but the Council might permit it** |
| **Reluctant yes** | **35 — Legendary** | **They will teach you** |

> **⚠ 35 is *"built never to open"* on that ladder. That is the correct anchor for asking the Jedi Order to break its own custom.**

**A level 10 character with `Persuade` maxed and good Charisma sits near +18.** **They need a 17 for partial and cannot reach 35 unaided.**

**That is intended. The partial is the normal outcome, and the Council is the real route.**

## 2.1 The age penalty

**"Young for a Jedi" means an infant. The Order takes children; a human of twenty is already too old.**

**⚠ The penalty scales on age bracket *within the species*, never on character level.** **A level 1 Wookiee can be sixty.**

### The ladder

**Six tiers, RCR p.120, `Table 6-1` and `Table 6-2`.** **One ladder, every species** — *only the year ranges differ.*

| Tier | Penalty to `Persuade` |
|---|---|
| **Child** | **0** |
| **Young Adult** | **−5** |
| **Adult** | **−10** |
| **Middle Age** | **−15** |
| **Old** | **−20** |
| **Venerable** | **⚠ No roll. The answer is no** |

### What that produces

**A character with `Persuade` maxed and good Charisma sits near `+18` at 10th level.**

| Tier | Partial, DC 25 | Reluctant yes, DC 35 |
|---|---|---|
| **Child** | 7+ | 17+ |
| **Young Adult** | 12+ | **impossible** |
| **Adult** | 17+ | **impossible** |
| **Middle Age** | **impossible** | **impossible** |
| **Old** | **impossible** | **impossible** |

> **⚠ Only a child can earn a reluctant yes on their own.** **That is the Order's custom stated as arithmetic.**

**An Adult with a silver tongue can reach the partial and go to the Council. A Middle Age character cannot, and the Council is their only route at all.**

**And a non-social character at `+5`:**

    Young Adult   partial needs 25+   impossible
    Adult         partial needs 30+   impossible

**⚠ A Soldier cannot talk a Jedi into anything.** **Correct — that is what the Council is for, and what the Sith are for.**

### Venerable has no ceiling, so it gets its own rule

**⚠ Every species' venerable band is `N+`, open-ended.** **A penalty that scales past it has nothing to scale against.**

> **So it does not scale. A venerable character is refused without a roll.**

**⚠ This does not touch already-trained characters.** **Jolee is old and was trained young.** **The gate is on *beginning*, never on *being*.**

### The conversion tables are in three places

**`Table 6-2` covers seventeen species. The roster is thirty-one.**

| Where the bands come from | Species |
|---|---|
| **RCR p.120 `Table 6-2`** | 17 |
| **UAA or the Campaign Guide** | 14 |
| **⚠ Nowhere** | **Kaleesh, Echani** |

**⚠ The tier names are universal. The conversions are not all in one place, and two must be authored.** **`§6`.**

## 2.2 The rule-breaker

**The GM or AI may permit one Jedi per campaign who does not care what the Order thinks.**

**Kreia. Qui-Gon. The Exile.**

**⚠ At most once per campaign, if at all, and the GM has to actually build the character.**

> **A rule that says *"sometimes an exception happens"* is not a rule.** **A rule that says *"one, and you have to earn meeting them"* is.**

## 2.3 The Council

**A session, not a roll. The `Persuade` already happened.**

**What the Council does is decide, weighted by what the party has actually done.** *A character who has saved a Jedi gets a different answer than one who has not.*

> **⚠ Rolling twice for the same question produces a player who fails at the temple, fails again at the Council, and has nothing to show for either.**

## 3. Asking a Sith

**Age is no obstacle. The Sith take anyone.**

**⚠ And that is exactly why people fall.** **The Order's custom is the reason the Sith have a recruiting pitch.**

**But the Sith do not make an offer you can decline for free.**

---

## 4. `Marked by the Academy`

**Applied when a Sith confirms your sensitivity and you decline to go with them.**

> **You are not forced. You are *known*.**

**They have a name, a face, and the knowledge that a Force-sensitive is walking around untrained and unclaimed. They consider that theirs.**

### 4.1 Hunt severity, by the marked character's level

| Levels | Who comes | Group | Encounter level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1–4** | Bounty hunter, alone | 1 | character level **+1** |
| **5–9** | Sith acolyte and hired guns | 3 | character level **+1** |
| **10–14** | Sith apprentice with a team | 4 | character level **+2** |
| **15–19** | A Sith Lord's agent | 5 | character level **+2** |
| **20+** | **A Sith Lord, in person** | 6 | character level **+3** |

**⚠ Always above the character's level.** **Being hunted should be losable. That is what makes capture live.**

### 4.2 Frequency

| Package setting | Hunt fires |
|---|---|
| **Off** | Never. **The mark is flavour only** |
| **Low** | Once per major story beat |
| **Standard** | **Once per major beat, plus `d6` 5–6 between beats** |
| **High** | Every major beat, and once between |

**Default: Standard. Campaign-package tunable, as the Echo cap is.**

**⚠ This tag can make a campaign about the Sith when the campaign was not about the Sith.** **`Off` exists for that reason.**

### 4.3 Escalation

**Each hunt the party survives raises the next by one band, to a maximum of two bands above the character's level.**

> **⚠ Winning makes it worse.** **You cannot fight your way clear — which is what makes removal the only exit.**

### 4.4 Capture

**They want you alive.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Character drops to 0** | **Captured, not killed** — if the hunt group is still standing |
| **Where they wake** | **A Sith academy. Korriban unless the package says otherwise** |
| **What that is** | **A place a character continues from** |

> **A hunt that kills you is combat with a story attached.** **A hunt that wants you taken means losing does not end the character — it starts a different one.**

**⚠ That is Malak's route, and it is Bastila's.** **A KOTOR 1 package would script her capture rather than roll it** — the `Closed` posture, as with the Echo list.

### 4.5 Removal

**One route.**

> **The Sith who marked you stops being able to report it.**

**Find them and kill them, or something worse.**

**⚠ Dark-side cost: treat as a tier-3 act regardless of circumstance.** **Hunting a person to silence them is murder, whatever the reason.**

> **The way out of Sith attention is to do something the Sith would approve of. That is the trap, and it should be a trap.**

### 4.6 What it is not

**Not a countdown.** *No "you have N sessions." The pressure is open-ended.*

**⚠ Not removable by joining the Jedi.** **The Order can protect you. They cannot un-know you.** **A marked character who becomes a Jedi is a marked Jedi.**

## 5. What training unlocks

**Once anyone agrees to teach you — Jedi, Sith, rule-breaker, or the Council's permission — the three Jedi base classes open: Jedi Guardian, Jedi Sentinel and Jedi Consular.**

**⚠ Whether Sith training opens the same three classes or different ones is not settled.** **Open item.**
