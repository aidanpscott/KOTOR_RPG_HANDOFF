# K2-PARITY-CHARGEN-PT2752 — character generation against K2, screen by screen

**Status: first pass complete (2026-10-16).** One row per screen and control. Each row cites the K2 picture it is checked against (`MAIN_WORK/media/study/pt2752/k2-live/`, private) and our own (`ours/`), and says whether ours **matches**, **differs on purpose** (with the ruling), or is **still to build**. Rulings: `PT-2752` (MAIN's rulings 1–8, the owner's chargen-text ruling, the class screen, Powers and the class gates, all in PLAYTEST-RULINGS-01), `PT-2751` (portraits), `PT-1697` (powers at level 1), `PT-215` (Jedi Master and Sith Lord cut as classes). App hashes are where each was built.

**Every deliberate difference, in one place:**

1. **Species is a step of its own, before Class**, and a droid chooses a chassis and model (`CHARACTER-CREATION-01 §3`; `PT-1222`). K2 has one human species.
2. **Gender is its own hub step**, before Portrait (owner's R-b, `PT-2751`: "Gender and species before portrait"). K2 bakes gender into the six class figures. So our class screen shows **one face**, a stand-in until Portrait is chosen.
3. **Our extra steps: Origin, Background and Equipment**, which K2 does not have; our Name popup has a second stage, the Story panel.
4. **Powers is a creation step** after Feats, 2 powers at level 1 (`PT-1697`, ruling 6). K2 offers no powers at creation; ours is built from K2's level-up `pwrlvlup_p` parts.
5. *(Retired 2026-10-17.)* The plus removed at the cap is **K2's own behaviour**, not a difference: the level-up capture shows K2 removes it (Demolitions at 2 cross-class, Stealth at 5; `k2-levelup/09`, `12`, `manual/L3-04`).
6. **No home-made sentence on a chargen screen** outside the description pane and the help box (owner's chargen-text ruling); labels are K2's StrRefs or approved names, and a guard test enforces it.
7. **Recommended is dimmed everywhere, and so is Quick Character**, until recommended builds are written (checkpoint 1, the Quick correction). K2's are live.
8. **The Powers grid is OUR chains, from `powers.toml` alone** (MAIN's powers ruling 5): one row per chain in the catalogue's order, not K2's row order; a chain longer than K2's three tiers (`Crush Opposition` I–VI, `Inspire Followers` I–V) carries on in the next row; a power heading two chains (`Stun Droid`, `Force Stun`) is drawn in both rows. *(Force Distraction was a third until 2026-10-19, through Beast Confusion's unruled link; PT-462's Beast Control link is restored, owner via MAIN, `PT-2752`.)* K2 has no fork. *(Corrected 2026-10-19: this read "no chain over three tiers", but K2's `spells.2da` chains Crush Opposition I–VI and Inspire Followers I–VI; the level-up captures cannot show their layout, since K2 leaves a power the class can never learn off its grid. POWER-GRID-ORDER-AND-ICONS-PT2752 §2.)*
9. **Restricted By Armor is printed on every power**: our rule is that medium and heavy armour block every power (`EQUIPMENT-01 §5.4`, enforced at the cast); K2 flags power by power (Energy Resistance has none).
10. **No pool comes back above its maximum after a level, and nothing refills** (MAIN's powers ruling 6; Lodestar 7d11db4): K2 leaves Force above it (48/47, 22/61 in `k2-levelup/manual/A-*`), a K2 bug.
11. **Attributes' score box shows the FINAL score**, the species adjustment applied, and its modifier box reads from it (`PT-2325`); the species line itself sits verbatim in the pane. K2 has no species, so its score is the bought one. Our old separate adjustment column (`-` for an untouched ability, `PT-1226`) has no K2 box and is gone with the K2 layout. **Ruled 2026-10-18 (MAIN, batch 1): accepted — it shows the player the number that counts.**
12. **Gold marks ANY aptitude, not only a class skill**, and CLASSSKL_LBL reads Class Skill (38152) for it: an aptitude from a homeworld, species or profession costs what K2's class skill costs. The pane names the sources (`Aptitude: class, homeworld`, `PT-1201`). The class's two priority abilities are gold on Attributes too; K2 marks none there. **Ruled 2026-10-18 (MAIN, batch 1): accepted — UI-STYLE-VALUES-01 (amber marks the privileged member of a set: aptitude, priority ability) and the owner's Aptitude ruling; Class Skill / Cross-class Skill (38152/38153) are K2's words for aptitude or not.**
13. **Our skills scroll inside K2's eight-row extent**, with K2's own list scroll bar in the gutter between the rows and the pane; K2 has exactly eight skills and no bar there. **Ruled 2026-10-18 (MAIN, batch 1): accepted — we have 25 skills to K2's eight, and the bar is K2's own part.**
14. **Portrait is a grid of faces, not K2's 3D head** (owner, 2026-10-18: *"There should not be3d models, but rather just portraits they can choose upon based on the race/subrace/chassis they go for"*; then *"Grid of every face"* and *"Same set per species for now"*). K2's `portcust_p` frame is kept; its head-view parts (LBL_HEAD, the bevels, pills and arrows) are left out.
15. **The hub is our own page, built from K2's parts** (owner, 2026-10-18: variant B approved, *"It looks amazing."*; Basic Info locked; the Character tab in round 6's table form; app 6c51c50). K2's custom panel (`custpnl_p`) holds K2's six steps beside a 3D figure; ours has twelve rows (eleven steps and Play) and a summary, so it is laid out afresh on one grid. Every control is a copy of one of K2's, at our own rectangle (`hubPanel` in `lib/chargen/hub_page.dart`):
    - **Head and foot:** `abchrgen_p` MAIN_TITLE_LBL, SUB_TITLE_LBL ("Custom Character", 240), LBL_BAR1 and LBL_BAR2; `custpnl_p` BTN_CANCEL and BTN_BACK.
    - **Left column, the steps:** `custpnl_p` BTN_STEPNAME1, one per step, at a 30-unit pitch from y 142 (x 76, 317 × 26). Only the current step is live; a finished step keeps its look but does not open (as K2, reone `custom.cpp` 85–90).
    - **Right column, the tabs:** `abilities_p`'s tab strip (LBL_BAR1, its tab words as type-4 labels, LBL_BAR2) over `ftchrgen_p` LB_DESC as the list box. The tabs are Basic Info, Character (1062), Skills, Feats, Attacks and Powers.
    - **Basic Info:** `maincg_p`'s portrait frame (`uibit_brdr_portp`) and name box, then `character_p`'s stats table control by control. The score and the modifier sit in their own cells (LBL_STR, LBL_STR_MOD …); Vitality and Force are current/maximum; then Defense and the saves, on LBL_STATSBORDER (`uibit_stf_chr3_p`) inside LBL_MORE_BACK.
    - **Skills:** `abilities_p`'s row (the icon square, the dark name box) with K2's list scroll bar, and the rank table on LBL_INFOBG.
    - **Feats, Attacks, Powers:** the same rows, holding what the character has; no icon means K2's empty slot.
    - **Character:** the Basic Info table's form under K2 section bars: Origin (species, subrace, chassis, gender or voice, homeworld, upbringing), Background (background, aptitudes), Languages. A long value wraps and its row grows: the shelf's languages are a sentence, not a list (agenda J).

    The Attacks step is shown locked (its picks are a later design). K2 has no Basic Info, Character or Attacks tab and no summary beside its steps.
16. **A finished step can be opened again** (owner, 2026-10-18, going back). K2 enables only the current step's button (reone `custom.cpp` 85–90); ours also opens any finished step, and locked steps stay locked. Left with the same choices, or cancelled, nothing changes. Changed, a K2 confirm box warns first (its line is with the owner: K2-WORDS-PENDING-PT2752 §4); OK clears every finished step after it, to be redone in order, and Cancel keeps everything. Back still undoes one step at a time.
