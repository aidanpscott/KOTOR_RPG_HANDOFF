# K2-PARITY-CHARGEN-PT2752 — character generation against K2, screen by screen

**Status: first pass complete (2026-10-16).** One row per screen and control. Each row cites the K2 picture it is checked against (`MAIN_WORK/media/study/pt2752/k2-live/`, private) and our own (`ours/`), and says whether ours **matches**, **differs on purpose** (with the ruling), or is **still to build**. Rulings: `PT-2752` (MAIN's rulings 1–8, the owner's chargen-text ruling, the class screen, Powers and the class gates, all in PLAYTEST-RULINGS-01), `PT-2751` (portraits), `PT-1697` (powers at level 1), `PT-215` (Jedi Master and Sith Lord cut as classes). App hashes are where each was built.

**Every deliberate difference, in one place:**

1. **Species is a step of its own, before Class**, and a droid chooses a chassis and model (`CHARACTER-CREATION-01 §3`; `PT-1222`). K2 has one human species.
2. **Gender is its own hub step**, before Portrait (owner's R-b, `PT-2751`: "Gender and species before portrait"). K2 bakes gender into the six class figures. So our class screen shows **one face**, a stand-in until Portrait is chosen.
3. **Our extra steps: Origin, Background and Equipment**, which K2 does not have; our Name popup has a second stage, the Story panel.
4. **Powers is a creation step** after Feats, 2 powers at level 1 (`PT-1697`, ruling 6). K2 offers no powers at creation; ours is built from K2's level-up `pwrlvlup_p` parts.
5. *(Retired 2026-10-17.)* The plus removed at the cap is **K2's own behaviour**, not a difference: the level-up capture shows K2 removes it (Demolitions at 2 cross-class, Stealth at 5; `k2-levelup/09`, `12`, `manual/L3-04`).
6. **No home-made sentence on a chargen screen** outside the description pane and the help box (owner's chargen-text ruling); labels are K2's StrRefs or approved names, and a guard test enforces it.
7. **Recommended is dimmed everywhere, and so is Quick Character**, until recommended builds are written (checkpoint 1, the Quick correction). K2's are live.
8. **The Powers grid is OUR chains, from `powers.toml` alone** (MAIN's powers ruling 5): one row per chain in the catalogue's order, not K2's row order; a chain longer than K2's three tiers (`Crush Opposition` I–VI, `Inspire Followers` I–V) carries on in the next row; a power heading two chains (`Stun Droid`, `Force Distraction`, `Force Stun`) is drawn in both rows. K2 has no chain over three tiers and no fork.
9. **Restricted By Armor is printed on every power**: our rule is that medium and heavy armour block every power (`EQUIPMENT-01 §5.4`, enforced at the cast); K2 flags power by power (Energy Resistance has none).
10. **No pool comes back above its maximum after a level, and nothing refills** (MAIN's powers ruling 6; Lodestar 7d11db4): K2 leaves Force above it (48/47, 22/61 in `k2-levelup/manual/A-*`), a K2 bug.
11. **Attributes' score box shows the FINAL score**, the species adjustment applied, and its modifier box reads from it (`PT-2325`); the species line itself sits verbatim in the pane. K2 has no species, so its score is the bought one. Our old separate adjustment column (`-` for an untouched ability, `PT-1226`) has no K2 box and is gone with the K2 layout.
12. **Gold marks ANY aptitude, not only a class skill**, and CLASSSKL_LBL reads Class Skill (38152) for it: an aptitude from a homeworld, species or profession costs what K2's class skill costs. The pane names the sources (`Aptitude: class, homeworld`, `PT-1201`). The class's two priority abilities are gold on Attributes too; K2 marks none there.
13. **Our skills scroll inside K2's eight-row extent**, with K2's own list scroll bar in the gutter between the rows and the pane; K2 has exactly eight skills and no bar there.
