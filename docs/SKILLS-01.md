## 2. The three new skills

**All Int or Wis, and none is a skill you guess at.** You do not stumble onto Sith history or Hutt protocol.

**Archaeology** — the dead. Ruins, artifacts, vanished civilisations, deep history. Rakatan technology, Infinite Empire sites, reading a wall nobody has read in twenty thousand years.

> **This is the setting's most-used knowledge skill and it barely needed arguing for.** KOTOR 1's entire plot is an archaeology expedition — Star Maps in Rakatan ruins, Sith tombs on Korriban, Massassi temples on Yavin 4.

**Xenology** — the living. Species, biology, customs, current factions and politics. Recognising a people and knowing how not to insult them.

**Mysticism** — the Force and its traditions. Recognising a power as it is used, Jedi and Sith doctrine, holocrons, what a Korriban tomb is for and what is likely inside it.

### 2.1 The line between Xenology and Archaeology is living versus dead

**Recent history sits in Xenology** — the Mandalorian Wars, the Great Sith War — because what matters is who is still angry with whom.

**The Rakata sit in Archaeology** even though a few survive, because what matters is what they *built*.

### 2.2 Mysticism does not require Force-sensitivity

> **A scholar who studies the Force without being able to touch it is one of the better character concepts this setting offers.** A Sith archaeologist. A historian who can read a holocron's provenance.

**And it matters mechanically.** `GAP-002` gates Force *powers* behind Jedi and Sith class levels. **Mysticism is one of the few routes by which a Smuggler or a Soldier can be meaningfully involved in the Force plot at all.** Restricting it would close that.

## 3. Why each merge

**Awareness — Spot + Search.** Both sight. Bith and Rodian already had both at equal values; only Bothan changes, gaining Search — and for the species running the Spynet, noticing and searching are one job.

**Alertness — Listen + Sense Motive.** Hearing and reading intent. **Five species affected**, and the traits it flattens are moving to racial feats where they are better served — §5.

> **Listen had to stay out of Awareness, and the species data is unusually clear about why.** Selkath and Sullustan hear and do not see; Bith and Bothan see and do not hear. **Four of five perception species are specialists.** Folding hearing into sight would have erased that on all four.

**Stealth — Hide + Move Silently.** Rolled together in practice.

**Security — Disable Device + Open Lock.** Locks, traps, and sealed doors are one job.

> **And Security holds a line that was blurring.** **Security gets you in; Slicing does things once you are in.** Electronic locks, alarms, and encryption on a system you have no access to are Security. Searching records, altering data, and running unfamiliar interfaces are Slicing. **Without that split, Slicing becomes the skill that does everything with a computer, which in this galaxy is close to everything.**

**Persuade — Bluff + Diplomacy.** Both are working on the person in front of you. **Intimidate stays out** — threatening someone is a different act with different consequences, and Wookiees carry +4 in it.

**Athletics — Climb + Jump. Swim stays separate**, because three species carry Swim bonuses and two are amphibious. **It is doing identity work Climb and Jump are not.**

**Acrobatics — Balance + Tumble + Escape Artist.** No species has a bonus in any of the three.

**Pilot — absorbs Astrogate.** Plotting the jump and flying the ship are the same person's job.

**Beast Handling — Ride + Handle Animal.** Riding, calming, driving off, training. **Two narrow skills into one that earns its slot.**

> **It also gives non-Force characters parity with a Force option.** Beast Trick, Beast Confusion, and Dominate Beast already do this job for Jedi. **Beast Handling is what everyone else reaches for — and a Force user with both can do things neither alone allows.**

### 3.1 Forgery split rather than merged

**RCR's Forgery is a 3.5 skill built for parchment and seals** — handwriting, wax, signatures. **Almost none of that survives translation to a galaxy of datacards.**

**What is left splits cleanly in two:**

- **Making a fake** — editing a record, faking credentials. **Slicing.**
- **Sourcing a fake** — knowing which Nar Shaddaa forger does clean work. **Streetwise.**

**Two different acts that the parchment-era skill had bundled.**

## 4. Why each cut

**Read/Write Language and Speak Language.** No key ability, bought with Intelligence-bonus languages at creation, **and nobody rolls them.** Character-sheet bookkeeping wearing a skill's clothes.

**Craft.** **KOTOR 2's crafting system is being incorporated**, which supersedes a generic Craft skill. Mon Calamari's +4 Expert Artisans moves to **Appraise** — which is why Appraise survives at all.

**Handle Animal.** Absorbed into Beast Handling.

**Gamble.** **Its work distributes across three skills that already exist.** Reading an opponent is Alertness; bluffing them is Persuade; palming a card is Sleight of Hand. **What remained was knowing the rules and odds of one pastime** — and nothing else on the list is a single-activity skill. A pazaak hand resolves as a Persuade or Alertness contest depending on whether you are bluffing or reading.

**Profession, Knowledge, Entertain.** All three were category skills — the last of them. **Ithorian's Knowledge (wilderness lore) +2 folds into their existing Scavenging +4**, which already covers it; the Knowledge bonus was the narrower half of a redundant pair.

## 5. Five species traits — CLOSED, and not the way this section predicted

**⚠ This section proposed moving five traits to racial feats on the grounds that *"a skill bonus cannot carry a condition."*** **It was written against a schema that has since changed.**

**Derived from `SPECIES-CHAPTER-v2`:**

| Species | Then | Now |
|---|---|---|
| **Nautolan** | Pheromonal sensing, graded +4/+1 | **`Pheromonal Sensor`** — a trait line carrying its own condition |
| **Bith** | Micro-vision, +4 near / penalty far | **⚠ Cut.** The `Skill Bonuses` line already carries it: *+4 Awareness within 4 metres, −2 Alertness beyond 10* |
| **Selkath** | Acute hearing, paired with a vulnerability | **`Acute Hearing`** — a trait line carrying both halves |
| **Mon Calamari** | Expert artisan, a per-individual choice | **⚠ Cut.** Replaced by `Waterbreathing` and `Moist Environment Affinity` |
| **Echani** | Kinetic reading | **⚠ Renamed.** `Familial Near-Identity` on the Eshan, and `Echani Combat Training` grants an attack chain |

### What actually resolved it

> **Trait lines carry conditions, ranges, choices and paired drawbacks.** **They were never restricted to flat skill bonuses.**

**⚠ The premise was wrong, not the analysis.** **The five were not schema problems; they were being written in the wrong shape and the shape was available all along.**

**Two did become racial feats — `Silver Reflex` and `Heavy Discipline` on the Echani subraces — but for a different reason: they are learned on level-up within a level cap.**

**⚠ And the four open species schema questions this section cited are closed.** **`§1.1` of the agenda records the species chapter as settled on every axis.**

**Sixteen of thirty-one species carry one, up from twelve.**

| Species | Bonuses |
|---|---|
> **Racial skill bonuses live in `SPECIES-CHAPTER-v2`, not here.**

**Each species entry carries a `Skill Bonuses:` field, written in the same shape as its ability adjustments.** **That is the single owner; this section previously held a second copy and the two had already diverged.**

**⚠ The divergence was real.** **This table gave Arkanian *Archaeology +2, Xenology +2*; the chapter now gives it *Medicine +2, Science +2* against its genetics and medicine identity.** **The chapter governs.**

**Every new bonus is drawn from the species' own chapter entry:**

**Miraluka** perceive entirely through the Force and have no other way of seeing.

**Kissai** are the Sith **priest** caste — *"the priests, scholars, and spiritual leaders of the ancient Sith Empire,"* who *"indoctrinated their comrades in the teachings of the dark side."* **A Mysticism bonus described in prose**, and it gives them a mechanical identity distinct from the Massassi, who are the soldiers.

**Rakata** carry *"retained knowledge from ancestors who built great technological works."* **They are the subject of the discipline and remember it from the inside.**

**Arkanian** are geneticists who study other species' genomes and are infamous for treating them as research subjects — **that is xenology** — with an ancient sophisticated civilisation and a Jedi academy founded on their world.

### 6.1 Two things worth watching

**Rakata are availability-restricted.** Barred until after the Jedi Civil War, so their Archaeology bonus will rarely be in play in a KOTOR 1-era package.

**Arkanian carry two knowledge bonuses and nothing else.** Both come up less often than Awareness or Stealth. **Thematically exact; mechanically narrow.** Worth checking in play that it feels like enough.

## 8. What this replaces

**KOTOR's eight — Computer Use, Demolitions, Stealth, Awareness, Persuade, Repair, Security, Treat Injury — were the starting point and are not a subset of RCR's list.**

**Four map straight across. Security was a rename. Three were consolidations of nine RCR skills** — Stealth from two, Awareness from three, Persuade from four.

> **So the work was never adding to KOTOR's eight.** It was deciding, cluster by cluster, whether KOTOR's consolidation or RCR's granularity was right — **and the answer differed by cluster.** Stealth merged. Awareness split, because species hear and see differently. Persuade kept Intimidate out.

**The three additions came last and from a different direction** — not from either list, but from what a tabletop campaign in this era actually needs and neither source provides.

### 9.2a Where the three orphan skills went

**Botany, Science and Swim were granted by no class at all.** **All three were added after `§9.2` was first written.**

**`Botany` → Scout and Engineer.** *Field naturalism, and the astromech that runs soil samples.*
**`Science` → Jedi Consular and Machinist.** *The scholar and the technician.*
**`Swim` → Soldier, Scout and Smuggler.** *It was separated from Athletics precisely because it does identity work; three classes that cross terrain now carry it.*

> **Every one of the twenty-four skills is now a class skill for at least one class, and a racial skill for at least one species.**

### 9.4 Three judgement calls in the rebuild

**Mysticism goes to all three Jedi classes.** Every Jedi studies the Force; it is not a Consular specialty.

**The Sentinel finally has an identity beyond skill points.** **Security, Stealth, and Streetwise make them the one who hunts dark siders rather than duels them** — which is what the class is for and what the source never expressed mechanically. It is also why the "broadest list" error was so easy to write: the class *should* have had one.

**Demolitions dropped from the Consular**, which the source gave them. Explosives do not fit the scholar-diplomat; it read as leftover from KOTOR treating the Consular as its general-skills Jedi.

### 9.5 One open balance item

**The Machinist maxes their entire class list at 1st level if Intelligence is 16.**

**Six of their nine class skills are Intelligence-keyed**, so Intelligence 16 is their obvious array — and **Intelligence is also the skill-point stat, so the ×4 compounds.** At Int 12 they max seven of nine, which is correct; at Int 16 they max all nine and have nothing left to grow into.

**The fix is widening the list rather than lowering the base.** Nine is thin for a technical generalist; **Streetwise, Sleight of Hand, or Astrogate-adjacent work would all fit**, and any two solve it.

## 10. Origin world and homeworld are different things

**A distinction the corpus had been eliding, and it matters now that a world grants a skill bonus.**

| Term | Belongs to | Changes? | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Origin world** | **The species** | Never | Selkath come from Manaan, Kissai from Korriban, Rodians from Rodia. **Availability restrictions act on this.** |
| **Homeworld** | **The character** | Chosen at creation | Where this individual was actually raised. **Grants a +2 skill bonus.** |

> **A Selkath raised on Taris has origin world Manaan and homeworld Taris.** They carry Selkath species traits and Taris's skill bonus. **Neither field constrains the other by default.**

**The canonical species chapter uses "homeworld" fifteen times, all in prose, all meaning origin world.**

> **This was true of the canonical chapter and false of the corpus when written.** A superseded parallel chapter — `SPECIES-CHAPTER-v2`, a complete 31-record draft predating D-Z — **carried `**Homeworld**` as a structured field on thirty records.** It was retired to `_dead/` under ruling A-20, **which is what made this claim true rather than accidentally correct.**
>
> **Recorded because the claim was right for the wrong reason.** A check that reads the canonical layer and reports on the corpus is a target error, not a warrant error — different tell, different guard.

**So the rename is clean against the canonical layer** — every current use becomes *origin world*, and *homeworld* becomes a new character-level field.

### 10.1 Three consequences

**Availability restrictions are origin-world facts and stay with the species.** Rakata remain on Lehon until after the Jedi Civil War; Kaleesh come from Wild Space; Dashade's origin world was destroyed. **None of that is about where a given character grew up.**

**But some species cannot freely choose a homeworld.** **A Rakata raised on Coruscant is not plausible** — Lehon is unreachable and its population is tribal and isolated. **Where a species' origin world is closed, the character's homeworld is effectively fixed to it.** That interaction needs stating, not assuming.

**And the homeworld bonus is a much larger content surface than the species list.** Thirty-one species have origin worlds; **a character can be raised anywhere.** Whether homeworld bonuses are assigned to a curated list of worlds or to every world the atlas produces is an open decision.

## 11. Aptitude

> **Aptitude replaces "trained."** In d20 that word means *has ranks in it*, which is not what this column holds. **Aptitude means the skill comes cheaply to you** — and it says nothing about how good you are.

### 11.1 The cost rule

| | Cost per rank |
|---|---|
| **With aptitude** | **1 skill point** |
| **Without** | **2 skill points** |

**Maximum rank in a skill with aptitude is character level + 3.** Without, half that.

### 11.2 Five sources, and the sheet shows which

**A skill is marked with an icon for each source granting it. A character may hold aptitude from several at once — there is no benefit to duplication, and no penalty.**

| Source | How many | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Class skills** | Most of them | 6 to 11 per class. See §9.2. |
| **Racial skills** | **1**, chosen | **Aptitude in one of the species' own bonus skills, chosen at creation.** See §11.4. |
| **Background skills** | 2 | **One from homeworld, one from profession.** |
| **Skill Focus** | 1 per feat taken | **A feat. 23 exist, one per skill.** §12. |
| **Trained skills** | Rare | **Campaign-granted, at special moments.** Not available at character creation. |

> **The first three are who you are. Skill Focus is what you chose. Trained is what happened to you.**

### 11.3 Profession is not yet designed

**Background grants two aptitudes: homeworld and profession.** **Homeworld also grants a +2 skill bonus** — see §10. **Profession has no design at all** and is on the agenda.

---

### 11.4 The racial skill is a choice, not a lookup

> **A species grants aptitude in **one** of the skills it already gives a bonus to. The player picks which, at character creation.**

**A Wookiee has `+4 Intimidate, +2 Beast Handling`, so a Wookiee player chooses aptitude in Intimidate or in Beast Handling.** **Both are on the sheet; only one comes cheap.**

**Where a species grants only one bonus skill there is no choice** — **Duros take Pilot, Gand take Awareness, Nautolan take Swim, Flesh Raiders take Scavenging.** *That is not a loss; those species are specialists and the single `+4` is why.*

**Three exceptions.**

**`Droid, Astromech` gets two — Slicing and Repair, both.** **It is the only entry with two racial skills.** > **The chassis is a diagnostic computer with tool arms; splitting the two would make it good at one half of its own job.**

**`Human` chooses freely.** **Their two `+2`s are already player-chosen, and the racial skill is one of those two.** **And a Human may trade their `Bonus Feat` for a *second* racial skill** — **one feat and one racial skill, or two racial skills and no bonus feat.**

**`Bith` take Awareness, and it is strange on purpose.** **Their bonus is conditional — `+4` within 4 metres, `−2` beyond 10 — but aptitude is not conditional.** **A Bith is therefore *apt* at Awareness and *penalised* at range.** *Which is exactly what a species built for the workbench should look like.*

**⚠ The bonus list is the whole of it.** **A racial skill must be one the species grants a bonus to.** **For droids, `DROID-SKILLS-01` gates which skills the chassis may take at all, and the bonus list is already drawn from that — so no droid clause is needed.**

**The choice is locked once play begins.** **A player may change it freely while building the character; after the first session it is a fact about them, not a loadout.**

> **`Trained skills` is the only route to aptitude after creation** — **campaign-granted, at special moments, and not something a player selects.**

**⚠ Duplication remains harmless.** **A Scout Trandoshan who picks Scavenging has aptitude from two sources and gains nothing extra, per §11.2.**

**Trained skills are the only route that arrives mid-campaign**, which makes them a package-authoring tool rather than a chargen option — the same shape as `INFLUENCE-01`'s opportunity frequency.

> **⚠ Two changes reached the feat library and not this table, and were corrected after an audit.** **Streetwise moved from `Freetrader` to `Hustler`; `Naturalist` gained `Science`.** **`scripts/audit_skillfeats.py` now verifies both directions and that all 24 skills are covered exactly once.**

### 12.1 Three are ported, seven are new

**Cautious, Gear Head, and Empathy are KOTOR's own** — and **two of the three changed.**

**Cautious survives unaltered.** Demolitions and Stealth both came through consolidation intact.

**Gear Head keeps its three skills but they are broader now** — Security absorbed Open Lock, Slicing absorbed half of Forgery. **The same +3 buys more than it did.**

> **Empathy lost a skill and the reason is our own consolidation.** It covered Persuade, **Awareness**, and Medicine — where KOTOR's Awareness was Listen + Spot + Search, *everything perceptual*.
>
> **Ours splits that**: Awareness is Spot + Search, Listen went to Alertness. **Awareness moves to Perceptive**, which covers both halves, and **Empathy is left with the social and medical pair.**

### 12.2 Loremaster uses a different tier convention

**Loremaster → Advanced Loremaster → Grand Loremaster.** The name already contains *master*, so the usual **Master** prefix is unusable.

**Not a departure.** The corpus already runs several conventions — **Gear Head uses *Adept*, Dueling uses *Advanced*, Sneak Attack uses dice, Targeting uses numbers.**

### 12.4 Duros receive Freetrader free at 1st level

**The species chapter reads:** *"Duros receive the Spacer feat as a bonus feat at 1st level"* (RCR p.25). **The record stands unchanged — the feat behind it changes.**

> **RCR's Spacer is +2 to Astrogate and Pilot.** *(An RPGnet review of the Core Rulebook lists it among the skill-bonus feats and names Han Solo's Astrogate and Pilot as the skills. **Secondary source — confirm with the extractor.**)*
>
> **In our system that feat is nearly worthless. We folded Astrogate into Pilot**, so RCR's Spacer would grant +2 to a single skill where every other skill feat covers two or three.

**So Duros receive our Freetrader instead** — **Appraise, Pilot, and Streetwise.** Flying, trading, and knowing who to ask.

**Better suited than RCR's version ever was.** Duros charted the hyperlanes and then made a living running them; **the trader-navigator kit is the species.**

**And two renames fall out of it.** Our skill feat was called *Spacer*, which collided with the Duros grant — **it is now Freetrader.** Our Acrobatics-and-Swim feat was called *Evasion*, which collided with the Scout's damage-avoidance feat — **it is now Nimble.**

**Duros receive the base feat only, not the chain.** They buy Improved and Master like everyone else.

> **Why the limit.** A three-skill chain granted free would make Duros the strongest species in the roster for a skill build — **every other species grant is a flat +2 to one or two skills, or a single narrow feat.** The base feat alone keeps them distinctive without making them dominant.

### 12.5 Open — do skill feats stack with Skill Focus?

**Gear Head Master gives +3 to Security. Skill Focus (Security) makes it 1 point per rank. A character may hold both.**

> **They are different mechanics — a flat bonus against a cost discount — so stacking is coherent.** But nothing has ruled it, **and it is the difference between a specialist and a broken one.**

## ⚠ What that means in practice

**⚠ A skill in `SKILLS-01` needs no RCR counterpart.** **`Medicine`, `Science` and `Slicing` are ours and answer to this document alone.**

**⚠ Where a skill DOES share a name with RCR's — `Repair`, `Demolitions` — the name is shared and the content is still ours.**

**⚠ And a rule written about an RCR skill does not automatically reach ours.** **f.72's take-10 conditions apply because they are GENERAL, not because `Repair` appears in both books.**

## ⚠ The one thing this costs

**⚠ An RCR mechanic that references a skill by name may reference one we do not have.**

**Report it as a gap rather than mapping it silently.** **⚠ `Treat Injury` is not `Medicine` and `Knowledge` is not `Science`; they are different skills that happen to overlap.**
