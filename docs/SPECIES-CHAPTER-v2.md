# Chapter One: Species of the Old Republic

*Knights of the Old Republic — 3956 BBY*

---

The galaxy of the Old Republic is home to hundreds of sentient species. Thirty-one of them are available to player characters in this game — ranging from the ubiquitous Humans who dominate galactic politics to the reclusive Miraluka who see the world through the Force alone, from the ferocious Massassi who once served the Sith Empire to the scholarly Bith whose homeworld has known no war for generations.

This chapter tells you what you need to know to play each of them.

---

## How Species Work

When you create a character, you choose a species before anything else. Your species determines several things about your character from the start, and those things never change.

**Ability Adjustments.** Most species modify your starting ability scores — a bonus here, a penalty there. Wookiees are extraordinarily strong; Duros are quick and clever but not physically robust. A few species, including Humans and Zabrak, carry no adjustments at all. Adjustments are applied after you generate your scores and can push them above 18 or below 3.

**Size.** Every species in this chapter is Medium-size, which means no special modifiers for being particularly large or small.

**Speed.** All species have a base speed of 10 metres. A few amphibious species also have a swim speed, noted in their entry.

**Languages.** Every character understands Basic, the common tongue of the Republic. Most species speak it as well as their own language. A character with a high Intelligence score gets additional languages — one per point of bonus — which can be spent on either speaking a new language or gaining literacy in one already spoken. A few species have unusual language situations: Wookiees understand Basic but cannot physically speak it; Gamorreans lack the vocal apparatus for anything but their own tongue; most Gand cannot produce Basic phonemes at all.

**Species Traits.** Beyond adjustments and languages, many species carry unique abilities — natural armour, darkvision, a rage, a sensitivity to the Force. These are listed in each entry's traits block. They are permanent and not something you choose; they come with the species.

**Age.** Each species has age bands — child, young adult, adult, middle age, old, venerable — with mechanical effects. Player characters typically begin as adults. Physical ability scores decline from middle age onward while mental scores rise; no score can be reduced below 1 by aging.

**Canon boundary.** **Legends only, nothing published after April 2014** — and **`The Clone Wars` counts as Disney canon and is excluded with it**, whatever its publication dates. **That exclusion is load-bearing here:** the Dathomirian Zabrak, the retcon of Asajj Ventress's species, and the Nightbrother framing all originate there. **The Zabrak subraces below are Iridonian and Clanhold, both drawn from Iridonia.**

**The Force.** Any species can be Force-sensitive. One species — the Miraluka — receives Force-Sensitive as a species trait. One — the Rakata — is barred from it entirely.

**Mind-influencing Force powers, defined.** **Several species resist them and nothing said what they were.** **`PARTITION-01 §4` names the set and `§6.1` cuts part of it; what survives is:**

> **`Mind Trick` · `Advanced Mind Trick` · `Force Distraction` · `Force Confusion` · `Fear` · `Horror` · `Insanity`**

**⚠ `Beast Trick`, `Beast Confusion`, `Droid Trick` and `Droid Confusion` are cut or held for the KOTOR 2 package — `PARTITION-01 §6.1`.** **They are not in the set.**

**⚠ And the name `Mind Trick` moved.** **It was row 181 and is now row 6.** **Any reference written before `D-AK` that says *Mind Trick* meant the costed combat power, now `Force Distraction`.** **Every reference in this chapter is post-ruling and means row 6.**

**How a species communicates is not a feat.** **Scent, radio, lekku, signal fires, hive consensus — all of it belongs in the species prose, where a player reads it, rather than on the mechanical block.**

> **It is important information and it is not a mechanic.** **A Weequay clan talking by smell changes how a scene plays; it does not change a die roll.**

**⚠ The age tables are gone, and this states which.**

**`Ages by Species`, `Aging Effects`, `Age and Aging` and `Languages at a Glance` are not in this chapter.**

> **Derived, not recalled: they are absent from the file this chapter descends from.** **`git show 35d7538:rules/CHAPTER-SPECIES.md` — the 2026-08-22 import — contains zero occurrences of any of the four headings.**

**So they were not dropped by this work. They were never in the copy this work started from.**

**⚠ Which makes this a *report*, not a reversal.** **Where they went is not answerable from here** — the canonical chapter may hold them, or they may have been lost before the import. **The Library should trace it; I can only say the ancestor did not have them.**

**And the dependency is real.** **`E-10`'s chargen ordinal and `E-11`'s age-category finding both need species age bands, and two `AUTHORED_PENDING` age fields are recorded on Kaleesh and Echani.** **Neither appears anywhere in this tree.**

**⚠ Droids are species-chapter entries, and this reverses a prior exclusion.**

**The governance line in the canonical chapter read: *"Droids are a separate subsystem per RCR Chapter 15."*** **That is no longer true here.**

> **Four droid chassis are full species records — `Droid, Astromech`, `Droid, Assassin`, `Droid, Battle`, `Droid, Remote` — with a shared `Droid` block above them.** **Chassis is species; specialisation is class.**

**Stated as a reversal rather than arriving as an edit.** **`DROID-SKILLS-01` governs which skills each chassis may take; this chapter governs everything else about them.**

**⚠ And it gives the droid-language ruling a home.** **That ruling has been unfiled because `C06` excluded droids by its own statement.** **The exclusion is gone; the ruling belongs here.**

**Racial skills.** **Every species grants aptitude in one of the skills it already gives a bonus to, chosen by the player at creation.** **`SKILLS-01 §11.4`.**

> **Aptitude is not a bonus.** **It halves what ranks cost and raises the cap from half your level to level + 3** — at level 8, rank 11 instead of 5. **A species can *master* its racial skill; without aptitude nobody masters anything.**

**Where a species grants only one bonus skill, there is no choice.** **`Droid, Astromech` is the only entry with two.** **`Human` chooses freely and may trade its bonus feat for a second.**

**The choice is locked once play begins** — free to change while building, fixed after the first session.

**Subraces.** **Seven species are recorded as multiple entries each, and choosing the species means choosing which.** **There is no way to play the parent without picking a side.**

| Species | Subraces |
|---|---|
| **Arkanian** | **Pureblood** · **Offshoot** |
| **Rakata** | **Builder** · **Flesh Raider** |
| **Sith** | **Kissai** · **Massassi** |
| **Zabrak** | **Iridonian** · **Clanhold** |
| **Nikto** | **Red** · **Green** · **Mountain** · **Pale** · **Southern** |
| **Echani** | **Eshan** · **Thyrsian** |
| **Droid** | **Astromech** · **Assassin** · **Battle** · **Remote** |

> **A subrace is a full record, not a modifier.** **Each carries its own ability line, skills, traits and languages, and they can differ freely.**

**⚠ Nikto are the exception and worth reading before you build one.** **All five subspecies are genetically compatible and share one ability line and one set of base traits** — **they differ only in what the radiation of M'dweshuu did to each population.** **The `Nikto` entry carries the shared half; the five subrace entries carry only what is theirs.**

**Hybrids.** You choose one species. Mixed parentage exists in the setting and some of its members are famous — Arca Jeth was Arkanian and Sephi; Krynda Draay was Human and Miraluka, Force-sensitive, and did not inherit the Miraluka second sight. But no source this game draws on gives a rule for combining two ability lines, or for which traits pass to a child and which do not, and the Draay case shows the answer is not simply *both*. Hybrid characters are not a character-creation option here. They remain entirely available as people in the galaxy — as allies, patrons, rivals, and family.

## The Entries

Species are listed alphabetically. Each entry gives a brief description of who these people are in the Old Republic era, followed by a traits block with every mechanical value you need at character creation.

Where a species is drawn from a source other than the *Revised Core Rulebook*, a note at the end of the entry says so. Most players will never care; it is there for the curious and for anyone checking values against a book.

---

### Arkanian, Pureblood

The Arkanians are a proud and brilliant people from the icy world of Arkania in the Colonies region. Their civilisation is ancient, sophisticated, and more than a little arrogant — famous across the Republic for their research in genetics and medicine, and infamous for the reckless experiments that sometimes follow. Arkanian corporations bring advanced medical treatments to market at opportune times, and Arkanian scientists have been known to treat other species as research subjects without excessive concern for consent.

At the campaign date, Arkania is a thriving corporate meritocracy. Off-world contractors harvest its famous diamond deposits alongside the natives, and a Jedi training academy founded by Master Arca Jeth operates on the planet. The cities are built enclosed to spare visitors from the bitter cold.

Arkanians tend toward intellectual pride and social aloofness. They are not shy about profiting from their research, and they carry a reputation — earned — for looking down on species they consider less advanced.

**Arkanian Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** −2 Dexterity, +2 Constitution, +2 Intelligence, −2 Charisma.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Perfect Recall:** An Arkanian remembers any text, face, or conversation exactly. **+4 on Archaeology and Xenology checks concerning anything previously studied.**
- **Cold Calculation:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 6.* **+2 initiative, and immune to fear.** *An Arkanian does not believe anything in the room outranks them.*
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Science, +2 Xenology.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Arkanian and Basic.

*Source: Ultimate Alien Anthology, p.16.*

### Arkanian, Offshoot

The Arkanians' enthusiasm for genetic experimentation eventually turned inward. Over generations, they engineered a sub-population modified for labour, environmental tolerance, or simple curiosity — the Offshoots. Regarded by many baseline Arkanians as tools or second-class citizens, Offshoots are shorter-lived, less physically hardy, and carry visible differences from their creators: five-fingered hands where baseline Arkanians have three clawed digits, and distinctly different eyes.

Those who leave Arkania scatter across the galaxy, typically into the lower reaches of society. A significant Offshoot population lives on Telerath, a corporate meritocracy in the Inner Rim.

Offshoots are stubborn, resourceful, and accustomed to being underestimated. When they fail, they try again.

**Arkanian Offshoot Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Strength *or* +2 Dexterity (player's choice at character creation), −2 Constitution.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Engineered Physiology:** Offshoots are the product of the same centuries of Arkanian genework. **Immune to disease and to natural poison; +2 on Fortitude saves.**
- **Determination:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 4.* **Once per encounter, when an Offshoot fails a skill check in which it has aptitude, it may reroll.** The new result stands, and it may be declared after the outcome is known.
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Athletics, +2 Repair.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Basic and an older Arkanian dialect distinct from the parent species' tongue.

*Source: KOTOR Campaign Guide, p.11. Converted from Saga Edition.*

### Bith

The Bith are an intellectually advanced humanoid species whose oversized craniums house complex brain regions devoted to language, mathematics, music, and abstract reasoning. The trade-off is real: their basic instincts and biological drives have atrophied. Warfare and strife have been unknown on the Bith homeworld for generations, and in the days of the Republic their senators were among the loudest voices for galaxy-wide disarmament.

Bith are grey-skinned and large-eyed, standing 1.6 to 1.8 metres tall. Their vision is extraordinary at close range — they can focus on microscopic objects with the naked eye — but poor at distance. Their acute sense of smell partially compensates. On Taris in 3956 BBY, Bith musicians and traders can be found in the cantinas and marketplaces of the Lower City.

Bith do not sleep. Instead, they enter a meditative trance for four to six hours each day, remaining somewhat aware of their surroundings throughout.

**Bith Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** −2 Constitution, +2 Intelligence, +2 Charisma.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Heightened Smell:** A Bith can identify an individual by scent at a range of 10 metres with a successful Wisdom check (DC 15).
- **Skill Bonuses:** +4 Awareness when examining anything within 4 metres; −2 Alertness beyond 10 metres.
- **Meditative Trance:** Instead of sleeping for 8 hours, a Bith can enter a trance lasting 4 hours that provides the same benefits. During the trance, the Bith remains somewhat aware and can make Listen and Spot checks to avoid being surprised. This can be done once per standard day.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Bith. Bith who travel the galaxy commonly speak Basic as well.

*Source: Ultimate Alien Anthology, pp.23–24.*

### Bothan

Bothans are short, fur-covered humanoids from the industrial world of Bothawui in the Mid Rim. They deal in information — they collect it, trade it, weaponize it when necessary — and the Bothan Spynet serves as one of the largest intelligence agencies in the galaxy. In the Old Republic era, Bothawui is one of the most heavily trafficked planets in the Republic, its Senate influence considerable, with Republic bureaucratic committees meeting on the world itself.

Bothans are manipulative, crafty, suspicious, curious, and a little paranoid. They are also loyal and brave, though they prefer to work behind the scenes rather than stand in the line of fire.

Their fur ripples in response to their emotional state, making Bothans notoriously difficult poker players.

**Bothan Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Dexterity, −2 Constitution.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Political Instinct:** **On entering any social situation, a Bothan identifies who holds real authority regardless of who appears to.** **+2 on Persuade and Streetwise made against that person.**
- **Fur Bristle:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 3.* **A Bothan cannot be surprised by anyone it has met before.** *The fur reacts before the Bothan does.*
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Persuade, +2 Alertness.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Bothese and Basic.

*Source: Revised Core Rulebook, p.23.*

### Cathar

The Cathar are a proud species of bipedal feline humanoids, well known for their fierce loyalty and their ferocity in battle. They dwell in great tree-cities on their homeworld, a wild planet rich with ecological niches and enormous insect predators. Cathar have razor-sharp retractable claws and a passionate, emotional nature — they hold family and tradition above almost everything else.

In 3973 BBY — seventeen years before the campaign begins — the Mandalorian commander Cassus Fett led a devastating assault on Cathar. The world was ravaged. Most Cathar perished. Survivors fled to Republic worlds across the galaxy, and the homeworld at the campaign date is still rebuilding: tribal, with no central authority, no industrial base, and no capital city.

The extent of the massacre remained unknown to the Republic until an expedition led by Revan brought the atrocity to light, winning the Cathar the sympathy of many Jedi.

**Cathar Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Dexterity, −2 Intelligence.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Natural Weapons:** **Claws. Unarmed damage is 1d6 and cannot be disarmed.**
- **Racial Attacks:** Cathar may take the *Pounce* and *Claw* unarmed attacks — `ATTACKS-07`. **Both are attacks, not feats, and cost an attack pick.**
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Awareness, +2 Athletics.
- **Languages:** Speak and read Catharese and Basic.

*Source: KOTOR Campaign Guide, p.12. Converted from Saga Edition.*

### Dashade

The Dashade are a rare and feared species — towering, massively built reptilians once prized by both Sith Lords and Jedi as retainers and bodyguards, and for good reason: the Dashade combine raw physical power with an extraordinary natural resistance to Force-based mental manipulation. A Dashade shrugging off a Jedi mind trick is not an exception. It is the normal case.

Their homeworld of Urkupp was destroyed approximately forty years before the campaign date, consumed in the Cron Cluster supernova during the Great Sith War of 3996 BBY. The species is widely believed extinct. In truth, a handful survive: some preserved in cryogenic suspension by Falleen royal families, others possibly in scattered colonies beyond the Cron Cluster. And at least one never learned any of it happened — a Dashade sealed in stasis in a Korriban tomb thirteen centuries before Urkupp burned, by a Sith Lord who valued him, and still sealed there now. A Dashade character in this era is a survivor of a dead world, and knows it.

**Dashade Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Strength, +2 Dexterity, −2 Wisdom, −2 Charisma.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Force-Deadened:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 6.* **+10 on Will saves against any mind-influencing Force power.** *A functional immunity expressed as a number. Not a KOTOR mechanic — it comes from the tabletop line, which is why it costs a pick.*
- **Heat Dissipation:** **Immune to heat and to fire damage from natural sources.**
- **Shadow Killer:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 4.* **+2 damage against any target unaware of you.**
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Stealth, +2 Intimidate.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Dashadi; speak Basic.

**Availability:** Dashade are available only in campaigns set before 3996 BBY. After the destruction of Urkupp, surviving Dashade are vanishingly rare.

*Adapted from the Ultimate Alien Anthology, pp.43–44.*

### Devaronian

Devaronians are among the oldest spacefaring species in the galaxy, with males common in spaceports for thousands of years. Their horned appearance — and their reputation for wanderlust, unreliability, and a cheerful willingness to bend the truth — has made them a familiar sight on smuggler's runs and in frontier cantinas from the Core to the Rim.

What makes Devaronians mechanically unusual is that males and females are built differently. Male Devaronians are quick and socially brash; females are contemplative, intelligent, and perceptive. The book gives two adjustment arrays, keyed by sex — the only species on this roster that works this way.

**Devaronian Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments (Male):** +2 Dexterity, −2 Wisdom, −2 Charisma.
- **Ability Adjustments (Female):** −2 Dexterity, +2 Intelligence, +2 Wisdom.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Devil's Bargain:** **Once per encounter, a Devaronian may reroll a failed Appraise check.** *They know what a thing is worth and will argue about it.*
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Streetwise, +2 Appraise.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Devaronese and Basic.

Devaronians carry no special traits beyond their adjustments — one of only a few species on the roster defined entirely by their ability line.

*Source: Ultimate Alien Anthology, pp.45–46.*

### Droid

**Chassis is species. Specialisation is class.** **A protocol droid is a humanoid chassis running the Engineer class; HK-47 is the same chassis running Marksman.** **The four entries below are bodies, not jobs.**

Droids are built rather than generated. **They do not use the 30-point buy** — their abilities come off the assembly line and vary by manufacturer, so the values below are the standard production spread for each chassis. **A GM may vary them; a player may not.**

**Shared Droid Traits** — every chassis carries these.

- **Constructed:** **A droid is built, not born.** **Immune to poison, disease, suffocation, radiation, and anything requiring a living metabolism.** **It does not eat, sleep, or breathe.** **It has no Force points, cannot take `Force-Sensitive`, and Force powers that target the mind do not affect it.**
- **Force Blind:** **A droid cannot take `Force-Sensitive` and cannot take any Force class.** **`PT-92`.** *Built, not born. There is nothing to awaken.*
- **Fixed Armature:** **A droid cannot take a `Combat`-rate class.** **`PT-103`.** *A chassis reaches eleven ranged attack chains and no melee at all — thirty-three tiers against a Combat class's forty. The frame cannot absorb the training.*
- **Ion Vulnerability:** **Ion damage drains 1 Constitution per hit, 2 on a critical**, recovered at 1 per day. *`PLAYTEST-RULINGS-01 B4`.*
- **Languages:** **Every droid understands five languages at creation.** **Basic and Binary are two of them; the other three are chosen freely.** *A droid's language bank is storage, and storage is cheap.*

> **Binary is the droid language** — beeps, trills and whistles, what droids use with each other. **`Droidspeak` is the same language under another name.**

**⚠ Two prior notes here were wrong and the correction is now from a direct read of the article, not a snippet.**

**`Binary/Legends`, fetched:** ***"Binary, also known as Droidspeak"*** **is one entry, and the Revan and Meetra Surik fluency line is in the article itself** — not only in aggregators quoting it.

**But the same article names the era problem in its own Behind the Scenes section:** ***"Different sources demonstrate a discrepancy on when droidspeak was invented: droids of Revan's era, like the T3-series utility droid, were using droidspeak more than 3000 years before Yperio Baobab's alleged invention of the language in 200 BBY."***

> **Wookieepedia states the discrepancy and comes down on the side of KOTOR-era use.** **We follow it. `Binary` stands at 3956 BBY.**

**⚠ Recorded with two caveats.** **The article carries a sourcing-quality tag.** **And the Bab-Prime and Industrial Automaton material genuinely is Rise of the Empire era — what a KOTOR droid speaks may be the archaic CBell-1 under the same name.** **Nothing in reach resolves that, and the name is what we need.**

**Understanding is not speaking.** **Whether a droid can *talk* depends on its chassis** — see each entry. **A chassis that ships mute takes `Synthesised Voice` to change that**, either as a feat pick or as an installed part.

### Droid, Astromech

Wheeled or treaded, a metre tall, built to plug into a starship and fix what is wrong with it. **T3-M4.**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Intelligence, −2 Strength, −2 Charisma.
- **Size:** Small.
- **Speed:** 8 metres.
- **Blaster Integration:** **Integrates a blaster into the frame as a self-contained weapon system.** *The feat KOTOR grants T3-M4 by name.*
- **Portable Workbench:** **An astromech is a workbench.** **Any character may perform item construction or upgrade work anywhere the droid is**, without needing a facility.
- **Spike Fabrication:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 5.* **Once per session, fabricate a set of programming spikes or a security tunneler from available material.** *T3-M4 does this as a matter of course.*
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Slicing, +2 Repair.
- **Racial Skills:** **Both — Slicing *and* Repair.** **The only entry in the chapter with two.** *The chassis is a diagnostic computer with tool arms; splitting them would make it good at one half of its own job.*
- **Languages:** **Five, as every droid.** **Speaks Binary only** — an astromech that holds a conversation has taken `Synthesised Voice`, or had one fitted.

### Droid, Assassin

Humanoid combat frame with precision servos. **The chassis a protocol droid and a killer share** — the difference is the class, not the body. **HK-47.**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Dexterity, −2 Wisdom, −2 Charisma.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Assassin Protocols:** **+2 damage against any target that has not acted this encounter.**
- **Heavy Weapon Mounts:** **An Assassin chassis may equip heavy weapons and assault cannons**, which no other droid chassis and no Medium organic can carry unbraced. **It may not equip melee weapons** — `ATTACKS-05` closes melee to every droid.
- **Targeting Computer:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 6.* **+1 attack with blasters.** *The feat KOTOR grants through the Superior Targeting Computer, which cannot be obtained by levelling in the source.*
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Repair, +2 Demolitions.
- **Languages:** **Five, as every droid, and it speaks all of them.** **This chassis ships with a vocabulator** — which is why protocol droids use it, and why an Assassin droid can talk to you before it kills you.

### Droid, Battle

Mass-produced infantry. Cheap, identical, and never manufactured in ones.

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Strength, +2 Constitution, −2 Intelligence, −2 Charisma.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Mass-Produced:** **Repair on a Battle droid takes half the normal time and cost.**
- **Hardened Frame:** **+1 natural armour bonus to Defence.**
- **Standardised Parts:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 3.* **Repair parts cost one fewer, minimum one**, and **you may salvage parts from any destroyed droid without a check.** *Already a three-tier chain in `FEATS-LIBRARY-01`.*
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Scavenging, +2 Repair.
- **Languages:** **Five, as every droid**, and **Basic and Binary are always two of them** — a Battle droid has to take orders. **It speaks both.** *Cheap production still needs a unit that can answer.*

### Droid, Remote

Repulsorlift spheroid, no limbs, no footfalls. **G0-T0 at Medium; Bao-Dur's Remote at Tiny.** **Size is a property of the individual, not a separate chassis.**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Dexterity, +2 Intelligence, −4 Strength.
- **Size:** **Tiny or Medium**, chosen at creation. *`creaturesize.2da` gives Tiny +2 to attack and Defence.*
- **Speed:** 12 metres, hovering. **Ignores difficult terrain.**
- **Repulsorlift Frame:** **Hovers. Ignores difficult terrain, and makes no footfalls** — **+4 Stealth.**
- **Shield Projector:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 6.* **Once per encounter, project a shield giving one adjacent ally damage reduction 5 for a round.** *G0-T0 carries one.*
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Stealth, +2 Slicing.
- **Languages:** **Five, as every droid.** **Speaks Binary only.** **A Remote that talks has taken `Synthesised Voice`** — G0-T0's is fitted, and it is the loudest thing about him.

> **⚠ `Spheroid` was cut and Remote absorbed it** — `DROID-SKILLS-01 §5`. **They are one chassis; Wookieepedia calls both *spheroid droids* in the same sentence.**

### Duros

The Duros come from the Duro star system at the extreme edge of the Core, and they were among the first species to develop interstellar travel. They have an almost innate affinity for space — most Duros learn to pilot a ship or rebuild a hyperdrive shortly after they begin to walk and talk. Their home system is governed by vast starship engineering corporations, and in 3963 BBY, seven years before the campaign date, Duro was among the Republic worlds overrun by the Mandalorian invasion.

Blue-skinned, hairless, and large-eyed, Duros are intense, adventurous, self-sufficient, and fun-loving. They tend toward gregariousness and wanderlust.

**Duros Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Dexterity, +2 Intelligence, −2 Strength, −2 Constitution.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Navigator's Memory:** **A Duros never miscalculates a hyperspace jump.** Any calculation they make is correct; the question is only whether the route exists.
- **Steady Hands:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 4.* **+1 attack with blasters beyond half your weapon's range.**
- **Skill Bonuses:** +4 Pilot.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Durese and Basic.

*Source: Revised Core Rulebook, p.25.*

### Echani, Eshan

The Echani are a near-Human species from Eshan in the Inner Rim, the heart of a confederacy of six worlds known as the Six Sisters. They are lean, pale-skinned, silver-haired, and strikingly uniform in appearance — siblings are nearly indistinguishable to outsiders, and family resemblance extends to facial features across generations. Echani identify one another through subtle shifts in body language, a necessary adaptation when faces cannot do the work.

Echani culture revolves around a martial tradition in which combat is treated as a form of communication — but that tradition is cultural, not biological. A child raised off Eshan would not inherit it. What *is* heritable is the perceptive acuity that makes the tradition possible: an instinct for reading body language that other species cannot match.

Multiple sources report the Echani as a product of ancient Arkanian genetic experimentation with the Human genome. Neither species has confirmed it, and the claim is recorded here as narrative rather than as something that influences any value.

At the campaign date, the Echani take the Republic's side during the Mandalorian Wars.

**Echani Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Dexterity, −2 Constitution.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Echani Combat Training:** **Grants access to the `Echani Strike` attack chain from character level 5** — `ATTACKS-07`. **Other species may learn the chain from an Echani teacher; the tradition is trained, not heritable.**
- **Familial Near-Identity:** **Echani siblings are near-indistinguishable to outsiders.** **+4 on any attempt to pass as another Echani; −4 on any attempt by an outsider to tell two apart.**
- **Silver Reflex:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 4.* **+2 on Reflex saves and on initiative.**
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Alertness, +2 Acrobatics.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Basic. An Echani may select one additional language at character creation. Few records exist of an original Echani language; Basic became their tongue of choice on entering the galactic community — the only species on this roster whose own language is effectively lost.

### Echani, Thyrsian

Thyrsus is the sun-baked world of the Six Sisters, and its people are the Echani's opposite in almost every respect that shows.

Where the Echani of Eshan are silver-haired and chalk-pale, the Thyrsians are dark of hair and skin. Where Eshan's people are so uniform that outsiders cannot tell siblings apart, **Thyrsians are individually distinguishable at a glance** — the near-identity that defines the parent species simply did not carry.

The cultural inversion is as complete. **Echani society answers to a female council; Thyrsus is patriarchal and always has been.** **Echani warriors fight light — light armour, light blades, speed and reading.** **Thyrsians fight heavy: plate and Force pikes, and they will stand where an Echani would move.**

What they kept is the thing that matters. **A Thyrsian reads body language as well as any Echani**, and the martial tradition is the same tradition. Their elite special-missions division became the Sun Guard.

**Thyrsian Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Constitution, −2 Dexterity.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Echani Combat Training:** **As the parent species. Grants access to the `Echani Strike` attack chain from character level 5.**
- **Sun-Forged:** **Immune to heat and to arid conditions, and +2 on Fortitude saves against exhaustion.**
- **Heavy Discipline:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 6.* **No Dexterity penalty from heavy armour.** **Requires `Armour Proficiency: Heavy`.** *A Thyrsian trains in plate from childhood and moves in it as others move in cloth.*
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Alertness, +2 Athletics.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Echani and Basic.

> **⚠ Thyrsians do not carry `Familial Near-Identity`.** **The sources are explicit that they did not inherit the uniform appearance, and it is the one Echani trait that does not cross.**

*Source: Legends. The Thyrsian subspecies, its inverted culture, and the Sun Guard are attested; the mechanical values are ours.*

### Gamorrean

Brutish, violent, and enormously strong, the porcine Gamorreans come from the pre-industrial world of Gamorr in the Outer Rim. Males train for war from birth while the females farm, hunt, and handle trade. Gamorreans respect physical prowess above all else and have no problem facing death against a foe they consider at least equal. What they lack in social grace — and they lack it profoundly — they make up for in raw physical power.

Since their world was discovered — the entry does not say when — Gamorreans have entered galactic society as labourers, bodyguards, mercenaries, and professional soldiers. They prefer primitive melee weapons but have no difficulty learning to use powered ones. To date, there are no known Gamorrean Force-users.

**Gamorrean Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +4 Strength, −2 Dexterity, −2 Intelligence, −2 Charisma.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Bred to the Axe:** **A Gamorrean receives the `Power Attack` chain's tier 1 free at 1st level, in addition to the four attack credits every class receives.** *`PT-90`. It is the only species granted an attack chain.*
- **Brute Force:** **+3 damage with any one-handed weapon.** *A Gamorrean puts a shoulder behind a blow others swing from the wrist.*
- **Thick Skull:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 3.* **+4 on saves against stun, and cannot be knocked prone by anything that allows a save.**
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Intimidate, +2 Athletics.
- **Languages:** Speak Gamorrean. Gamorreans have no written form of their language. They can learn to understand other languages but lack the vocal apparatus to speak any tongue but their own.

*Adapted from the Revised Core Rulebook, p.26.*

### Gand

The Gand are insectoid humanoids from the ammonia-atmosphere world of Gand in the Outer Rim. Off-world Gand are common enough that breathing apparatus is a standard part of their kit — without it, a standard oxygen atmosphere is as lethal to a Gand as ammonia would be to a Human.

Their exoskeletal structure gives them natural armour, and their compound eyes function in total darkness. The most famous Gand tradition is the findsman — a tracker who uses mystical rituals to divine the location of quarry — and the findsman prestige class implies a well-established galactic role for the species.

Most Gand cannot speak Basic. They lack the vocal apparatus. A few individuals possess appropriate vocal cords, but the default for the species is reliance on translators or other means.

**Gand Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Wisdom, −2 Charisma.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Natural Armour:** **+1 natural armour bonus to Defence.**
- **Atmosphere Dependent:** **A Gand breathes ammonia.** **In an oxygen atmosphere without a rebreather, a Gand may act for `Constitution` rounds, then falls unconscious and begins dying.** *The rebreather is standard kit and losing it is a real emergency.*
- **Skill Bonuses:** +4 Awareness.
- **Languages:** Speak Gand. Most Gand are incapable of speaking Basic; a few have appropriate vocal cords. Most rely on translators.

*Source: Ultimate Alien Anthology, pp.62–63.*

### Kaleesh

### Kel Dor

The Kel Dor come from the planet Dorin, where they evolved in an atmosphere of helium and a gas unique to their world. Standard oxygen-nitrogen atmospheres are deadly to them. Off-world Kel Dor wear breath masks and protective goggles at all times — without the goggles they are effectively blind, and without the mask they begin to suffocate.

Despite this, the Kel Dor have a long and proud Force tradition, having adapted it to the Jedi tradition since joining the Republic over six thousand years ago. They are kind-hearted, even-tempered, and believe in quick, simple justice. Silver irises mark those some believe are strong in the Force.

### Sith, Massassi

The Massassi are the warrior caste of the original Sith race — ferocious, selfless soldiers bred for war. ### Miraluka

The Miraluka are remarkably similar to Humans — similar enough to blend in on most worlds — with two important and distinctive differences. They have no eyes, and they perceive the world entirely through the Force. Every Miraluka is, in a meaningful sense, Force-sensitive from birth.

They cover their featureless eye sockets with cloth or visors, especially when travelling. Most outsiders never notice the difference. The Miraluka homeworld of Alpheridies, in the Expansion Region, is a largely isolationist and self-sufficient world whose inhabitants rarely venture far. Their colony of Katarr exists at the campaign date but will be consumed by Darth Nihilus after the Jedi Civil War.

It is highly unusual for an entire sapient species to be Force-sensitive. Given their connection, it is common for Miraluka to join the Jedi Order, and several have become central figures in the Jedi tradition during this era.

**Miraluka Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** −2 Dexterity, +2 Intelligence.

- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Second Sight:** **A Miraluka cannot be blinded, and `Stealth` gains no benefit against them from darkness or cover.**
- **Force-Sensitive:** **Miraluka receive `Force-Sensitive` as a bonus feat at 1st level.** **They perceive entirely through the Force and know `Force Sight` from creation** — it is how they see, not a power they learned.

### Nautolan

Nautolans are amphibious humanoids with mottled green skin and a crown of long, tentacle-like head-tendrils in place of hair. These tendrils are sensory organs: in water, they function as pheromone detectors, allowing a Nautolan to read another creature's emotional state with uncanny precision. Out of water, this ability is diminished but not lost.

Their skeletal structure is reinforced with extra cartilage, making them exceptionally resilient. Nautolans feel more at home in the water than on land, but they are adaptable — nobles, scoundrels, soldiers, Jedi consulars and guardians all appear among their adventurers.

Their language, Nautila, is pronounceable only underwater. On land, it loses so much nuance that most Nautolans find it more practical to speak Basic or the Anselmian tongue of their land-dwelling neighbours.

**Nautolan Species Traits**

- **Waterbreathing:** **Cannot drown.**

### Nikto

Kintan sits in the Si'Klaata Cluster inside Hutt Space, close enough to the dying star M'dweshuu that its radiation permeates the whole world. It is not immediately lethal. What it did instead was accelerate mutation, so that changes which should have taken millions of years took thousands — and the Nikto diverged into five subspecies, each adapted to a different corner of a violently varied planet.

They are reptilian humanoids, about 1.8 metres, with thick leathery skin often carrying spikes or horns, and a protective membrane over the eye. **They lack the facial musculature for expression**, which other species consistently misread as stupidity. The Nikto are aware of this and most have stopped correcting anyone.

**They have belonged to the Hutts since the Treaty of Vontor**, signed during the Hutts' wars against Xim the Despot, twenty thousand years before this campaign. Many Nikto serve as guards and enforcers. Some are slaves outright. A Nikto who has left Hutt Space has usually done something to get there.

**All five subspecies are genetically compatible.** **They share every base trait and the −2 Charisma; each carries a different +2**, because the radiation of M'dweshuu pushed each population somewhere different.

**Shared Nikto Traits** — every subspecies carries these.

- **Ability Adjustments:** **−2 Charisma**, plus a +2 set by subspecies. *See each entry.*
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Leathery Hide:** +1 natural armour bonus to Defence.
- **Unreadable:** A Nikto face gives nothing away, and everyone assumes the worst. **+4 on Will saves against Persuade and against any attempt to read intent** — and **−2 on Persuade against anyone who has not dealt with Nikto before.**
- **Nictitating Membrane:** Immune to being blinded by light, sand, smoke, or spray.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write **Nikto and Basic**. **⚠ Huttese is not automatic** — a Nikto has heard it all their life and is not owed it. **A Nikto who wants it takes it as a language, or takes the Bounty Hunter class, which grants it.** **Mountain Nikto also speak Cracek**, which no other subspecies uses.

### Rakata, Builder

The Rakata are an ancient species whose civilisation once stretched from the Unknown Regions to distant parts of the galaxy. At the height of their power, they built the Star Forge — a massive space station factory that used the dark side of the Force to create the ships and materiel of an interstellar empire. They called it the Infinite Empire, and it was.

Then, for reasons unknown, the Rakata lost their connection to the Force. Their technology failed. Their subjugated worlds threw off their masters. Rakata civilisation collapsed into primitiveness over thousands of years. By the campaign date, the Rakata are rediscovered by Darth Revan on their homeworld of Lehon. They are small in number, tribal, and profoundly aware of what they once were.

The Rakata are the roster's counterexample for the principle that capability does not run backward through time. They are intelligent and can learn advanced technology despite a primitive upbringing — but they are also living proof that a spacefaring civilisation can become a tribal remnant within recorded history.

**Rakata Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Intelligence, −2 Wisdom.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Force Blind:** **A Rakata cannot take `Force-Sensitive`.** **They still accrue Dark Side Points and are still affected by Force powers — they simply cannot wield the Force.**
- **Rakata Rage:** **Once per day as a Bonus action, rage for `5 + Constitution modifier` rounds.** **+2 attack and +2 damage; −2 Defence.**
- **Ancient Knowledge:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 8.* **A Rakata remembers what its ancestors built.** **No penalty for lacking aptitude in Archaeology or Mysticism** — both are treated as skills you have aptitude in, for cost and for maximum rank.
- **Builder's Hand:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 6.* **You may operate Rakatan technology no other species can, without a check.**
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Archaeology, +2 Science.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Rakata.

**Availability:** Rakata are not available in campaigns set before the end of the Jedi Civil War. Almost all living Rakata remain on Lehon and do not leave until after the war — and Lehon is a world in the Unknown Regions that ships cannot reach without foreknowledge. A Rakata character presupposes both that the war is over and that someone found their world.

*Source: KOTOR Campaign Guide, p.18. Converted from Saga Edition.*

### Rakata, Flesh Raider

The Flesh Raiders are mutated, selectively bred Rakata — lower-caste soldiery produced by the Infinite Empire in the thousands and deployed in overwhelming numbers when the Rakata invaded Tython. They are bulky, they are carnivorous, and their teeth are built to strip a carcass to the bone. They live in tribes on the ridges, burn the ground they occupy bare, and signal each other across valleys with fires.

### Rattataki

Rattatak is a mountainous world in the Outer Rim, off every trade route and bordering Wild Space. It has almost no resources worth taking, which is why nobody took it — and why the people who evolved there spent their entire history fighting each other for what little there was.

The Rattataki are Near-Human: chalk-white or pale grey skin, no hair anywhere, and ice-blue eyes. Most wear dark facial tattoos, and many add piercings.

Their society settles questions in the pits. Guilt, ownership, leadership and grievance are all decided by combat, and the gladiatorial arenas are simultaneously the courts and the only entertainment anyone has. Children are tested in battle young because every able body in a clan is needed to hold its ground against the next one.

**They never developed space travel.** The isolation that shaped them also kept them planet-bound, and the galaxy arrived rather than the other way round — first slavers, who found the Rattataki too difficult to train and settled for selling them stronger opponents instead, then mercenaries who came for the pits and mostly stayed. **Basic came with them, and stuck.**

**Rattataki Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Constitution, −2 Charisma.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Pit-Fighter:** **+1 attack against any opponent who is the only enemy within 2 squares of you.** *One against one is the fight they know.*
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Intimidate, +2 Acrobatics.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Rattataki and Basic.

**Availability:** **Attested as a galactically present species three centuries after this campaign, not at 3956 BBY.** **But Rattatak was reached by slavers and by off-world mercenaries well before that, and both traded in both directions.**

> **So a Rattataki at the campaign date is off-world because someone brought them off-world.** **They did not book passage. Their homeworld has no ships.**

*Source: SWTOR-attested, admitted by owner decision. Era evidence recorded above.*

### Rodian

Rodians grow up in a culture built on hunting and tracking. They consider bounty hunting an art form, revering gladiatorial games and controlled violence. From a tightly controlled industrial world, the greatest aspiration of a young Rodian is to become the galaxy's best hunter.

Despite their low Wisdom, Rodians are extraordinarily perceptive — their multifaceted eyes and keen hearing give them a natural edge in tracking and observation. On Taris in 3956 BBY, Rodians are a common sight in the cantinas and commercial districts.

**Rodian Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Dexterity, −2 Charisma.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Antennal Sense:** **+4 Awareness to detect movement**, and **a Rodian cannot be flanked by anything it can hear.**
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Awareness, +2 Streetwise.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Rodese and Basic.

*Source: Revised Core Rulebook, p.30.*

### Selkath

The Selkath are an aquatic species from Manaan, a water world in the Inner Rim. They are peaceful by nature and wary of galactic conflicts — but their world is strategically vital, because it is the sole source of kolto, a healing agent that predates and at this date outperforms bacta for treating superficial wounds.

Manaan is neutral territory during the Jedi Civil War, and the Selkath guard that neutrality fiercely. They live in isolated underwater cities but trade and communicate freely among themselves. Their capital, Ahto City, is one of the few surface structures — a diplomatic and commercial hub built specifically for off-world visitors.

The tension at the heart of Selkath society is that they are isolationists who happen to control the galaxy's most important medical export. They maintain constant economic contact with the Republic while declining political involvement.

**Selkath Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Charisma.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres on land; swim speed available.
- **Waterbreathing:** **Cannot drown.**
- **Acute Hearing:** **+4 Alertness on anything heard rather than seen.** **And sustained exposure to a hostile sonic frequency forces a Will save at DC 15** — **on a failure the Selkath attacks the nearest creature, ally or enemy, for `1d4` rounds.** *The Progenitor's wailing at the Hrakert Rift drove the Selkath there to attack everyone around them.*
- **Kolto Sense:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 5.* **You identify a wound, poison, or illness by sight.** **+4 Medicine to diagnose rather than to treat.**
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Alertness, +2 Swim.
- **Languages:** Speak Selkath.

*Adapted from the KOTOR Campaign Guide, p.19. Converted from Saga Edition.*

### Sullustan

Friendly, outgoing, and fond of practical jokes, Sullustans are pragmatic and pleasant companions.

### Togruta

Togruta are a species of colourful, horn-crowned humanoids from the world of Shili, a planet of dense forests and open tundra. Their striped and patterned skin provides natural camouflage, and their hollow horns — called montrals — function as passive echolocation organs, giving them a spatial awareness that other species lack.

Togruta who leave Shili describe themselves as going to *hunt among the stars*. They are communal by nature but fiercely independent in practice, and their instinct for group tactics makes them formidable in combat.

The most remarkable thing about Togruta is what happens when they are blinded. Where most species lose their effective Dexterity when they cannot see, a Togruta's montrals can compensate: with a successful Listen check (DC 13), a Togruta can avoid the reduction of effective Dexterity to 3 while retaining the other penalties of blindness. The entry itself notes that many Togruta take the Blind-Fight feat to cover the remaining gap.

**Togruta Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Dexterity, −2 Constitution, +2 Wisdom.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Spatial Awareness:** **When blinded, a Togruta may attempt an Alertness check at DC 13.** **On success they avoid the reduction of effective Dexterity to 3.** **All four remaining penalties of the blinded condition still apply:** **−4 on Strength-based and Dexterity-based checks**, **−4 on Awareness checks**, **the Togruta still cannot see**, and **the 50% miss chance remains** — **montrals do not let a blinded Togruta unerringly locate a foe.** *UAA p.163.*
- **Montral Echo:** **You detect any moving creature within 5 squares, through walls, whether or not you can see it.** *Passive sonar. It is what montrals are for.*
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Stealth, +2 Acrobatics.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Togruti and Basic.

*Source: Ultimate Alien Anthology, pp.162–163.*

### Trandoshan

Their scaly hides provide a natural defence, and their eyes function in total darkness.

*Source: Revised Core Rulebook, p.31.*

### Twi'lek

From the dry, rocky world of Ryloth, the Twi'leks have made a place for themselves along the galactic rim. Known for their beautiful head-tails — called lekku — Twi'leks are sly, calculating beings who prefer to work behind the scenes. They watch, plan, and prepare, and rarely does an individual Twi'lek rush into anything.

Twi'leks can be found on planets across the Outer Rim, including Tatooine and Malastare. Some are diplomats, some entertainers, some criminals. The Force is strong in the species, and some Twi'leks gravitate toward Jedi studies.

Their lekku serve as a secondary communication channel — a subtle, silent language that Twi'leks can use to converse without anyone else noticing.

**Twi'lek Species Traits**

- **Dancer's Footing:** **+2 Defence in any round in which you moved at least 2 squares.**

### Verpine

The Roche asteroid field is a debris belt in the Mid Rim, and the Verpine have lived in it since before the Republic existed. **Some believe the asteroids are the remains of their homeworld, broken by a civil war nobody now remembers.** **Ask a Verpine and they will tell you they do not know, and mean it.**

They are green, bipedal, insectoid — long neck, two black eyes, antennae, three-fingered hands. **Their eyes resolve microscopic detail.** **Their antennae send and receive radio, so a group of Verpine reaches a decision almost instantly and any one of them may then speak for all of them.**

Their antennae send and receive on the radio band. **Two Verpine confer silently at short range without equipment, and any Verpine can hear an unencrypted transmission nearby without a comlink.** The practical consequence is that a group of Verpine reaches a decision almost instantly, and **any one of them may then speak bindingly for all of them** — a habit that has confused every species that has ever tried to negotiate with them one at a time.

**They are the finest engineers in the galaxy and have been for twenty thousand years**, and they hold that reputation without ever having been much interested in anything else. Verpine designs get adapted and improved from other species' work — Bothan, Arkanian — and come back better than the original.

**Verpine Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Intelligence, +2 Dexterity, −2 Strength, −2 Constitution.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Microscopic Sight:** **+4 on any check to examine, identify, or repair something small.** *A Verpine sees a hairline fracture the way anyone else sees a crack.*
- **Natural Armour:** **+1 natural armour bonus to Defence.** *Chitin.*
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Slicing, +2 Sleight of Hand.
- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Verpine and Basic.

**Availability:** **Present and unambiguous.** **Roche joined the Republic between 25,000 and 22,000 BBY.** **A Verpine pirate named Raxsus Nuli was arrested by the Jedi in 3996 BBY — forty years before this campaign — and Roche exported shatter guns *prior to the Jedi Civil War*.**

> **The best era evidence of any species added in this pass.**

*Source: Legends. Physiology, the radio antennae, the Roche history, and the 3996 BBY attestation are all attested; the mechanical values are ours.*

### Weequay

The Weequay come from Sriluur, a harsh world on the periphery of Hutt Space. They reached an industrial technology level before making contact with the rest of the galaxy, and since then the Hutts have been their most prominent employers — though Weequay appear in spaceports, cantinas, and mercenary companies across the Outer Rim.

Weequay are tough, strong, and socially blunt to the point of inscrutability. Their ability line is the widest spread on the roster — five adjustments — and the −4 to Charisma is the most severe social penalty in the game.

What makes Weequay biologically distinctive is their pheromonal communication. **The range is 20 metres, 40 upwind and 10 downwind.** Any species with a scent ability — Whiphid, Yuzzem — can tell that something is being said. **Detection and comprehension are separate: a non-clan Weequay knows a conversation is happening and understands none of it.** *UAA pp.181–182.*

**Weequay Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +2 Strength, +2 Constitution, −2 Intelligence, −2 Wisdom, −2 Charisma.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Leathery Hide:** **+2 natural armour bonus to Defence.** *Weequay skin is thick and sun-cured.*
- **Sun-Hardened:** *Racial feat — learned on level-up, maximum level 4.* **Immune to heat and to desert fatigue, and +2 on Fortitude saves against poison.**
- **Skill Bonuses:** +2 Scavenging, +2 Athletics.
- **Languages:** Speak and read Sriluurian (written Sriluurian uses hieroglyphics and communicates simple ideas only); speak Basic.

*Source: Ultimate Alien Anthology, pp.181–182.*

### Wookiee

Wookiees are long-lived, arboreal humanoids from the jungle world of Kashyyyk. They are enormously strong, fiercely loyal, and surprisingly adept with technology — they may look primitive, but they are quite comfortable with high-tech equipment and starship systems. Their customs revolve around honour and loyalty: the honor family, the sacred life debt, and the arboreal code that forbids using climbing claws in combat.

In 3963 BBY, Kashyyyk was among the Republic worlds overrun by the Mandalorians.

Wookiees understand Basic but cannot speak it — their vocal apparatus produces only the grunts, growls, and roars of Shyriiwook. When necessary, a Wookiee can fly into a rage of legendary proportions, gaining tremendous strength at the cost of recklessness and exhaustion.

**Wookiee Species Traits**

- **Ability Adjustments:** +4 Strength, −2 Dexterity, −2 Wisdom, −2 Charisma.
- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.
- **Wookiee Rage:** **Once per day a Wookiee may enter a rage.** **While raging: +4 Strength, +2 vitality per character level, and +2 on Will saves.**
- **Extraordinary Recuperation:** **A Wookiee regains vitality and wound points at twice the normal rate** — 2 vitality per level per hour, 2 wound points per day. **It doubles the cost of meditating**, since a Wookiee forgoes twice as much. *`REST-AND-MEDITATION-01`, from RCR p.33.*
- **Skill Bonuses:** +4 Intimidate, +2 Beast Handling.

- **Languages:** Read/write Shyriiwook and Basic; speak Shyriiwook; understand Basic. Wookiees cannot speak Basic or any language other than Shyriiwook.

### Zabrak, Iridonian

The Zabrak took to the stars early in galactic history and have settled a vast number of colony worlds — many Zabrak identify themselves by the colony they hail from rather than by species. They have an iron will and a survival instinct second to none. Distinguished by patterns of vestigial horns atop their heads, Zabrak are explorers, warriors, and settlers.

In 3963 BBY, Iridonia — the Zabrak homeworld — was among the Republic worlds overrun by the Mandalorians.

- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Zabrak and Basic.

### Zabrak, Clanhold

The Clanhold Zabrak never left Iridonia. They hold the highlands and the deep valleys the colonists were glad to be rid of — inhospitable terrain, fierce predatory life, and clans that have been settling boundaries the same way for longer than anyone has counted.

They carry the same vestigial horns and the same two hearts. What marks them out is the tattooing: full ritual facial work applied at the rite of passage, indicating clan, line, and what a Zabrak has personally done. An Iridonian colonist's tattoos are often decorative. A Clanhold Zabrak's are a record.

**Clanhold Zabrak Species Traits**

- **Size:** Medium.
- **Speed:** 10 metres.

- **Languages:** Speak and read/write Zabrak. **Basic is learned rather than native** — a Clanhold Zabrak who has left Iridonia speaks it.

## The Awkward Cases

A sourcebook that pretends its rough edges are smooth is lying to the reader. This section names the places where the work shows its seams.

**Two species have no source book.** Kaleesh and Echani do not appear in any d20 Star Wars book in any edition. They were built using the *Ultimate Alien Anthology's* Customized Aliens procedure, borrowing every quality from a named book species. The construction is auditable — you can check that Kaleesh's darkvision matches Gand's. What you cannot check is whether Kaleesh *should have* darkvision. That rests on aggregator descriptions, and no book confirms it. Both entries say so, on the values themselves rather than buried in a footnote.

**Several species are converted from Saga Edition.** Cathar, Miraluka, Kissai, Massassi, Rakata, Selkath, and Arkanian Offshoot were drawn from the *KOTOR Campaign Guide*, which is a Saga Edition book — a different game. Their ability adjustments and narrative content transfer cleanly. Their mechanical traits required conversion, and some Saga concepts (swift actions, trained skills, the condition track) do not exist in Revised. Where a Saga trait could not be cleanly converted, the entry notes it.

**Some species are not available at the campaign date.** Dashade's homeworld was destroyed forty years before the game begins, and surviving Dashade are vanishingly rare. Rakata remain on Lehon until after the Jedi Civil War, and Lehon is a world in the Unknown Regions that cannot be reached by accident. Kaleesh come from Wild Space, beyond the Outer Rim — no species on this roster with established Republic contact comes from that far out.

**One entry was locked and has since been opened by owner decision.** **The Flesh Raiders are now playable — `Rakata, Flesh Raider`.**

**The original objection stands as a description of the evidence and is worth keeping.** **Nothing in any source this game draws on says what they are at 3956 BBY.** They are attested at the founding of the Je'daii Order, twenty-two thousand years before the campaign, and then not again until three centuries after it. **No source gives them an ability line, a size, a speed, or a single trait.**

> **So the entry is authored rather than converted, and it is the only species record in the chapter of which that is true.** **Every value in it is a design decision made against the prose descriptions, not a figure lifted from a table.**

**The availability restriction does the work the lock used to do.** **Tython is lost at the campaign date and a Flesh Raider character requires a campaign that reaches it.**

**Nineteen species have thin era evidence.** The era pass ran every species against the available sources and recorded what was found and where it was looked for. For nineteen species, what was found amounts to: this species is spacefaring and galactically present in the source's framing, but nothing dates that presence to 3956 BBY specifically. That is an honest result, not a gap. Among the first species to develop interstellar travel (Devaronian) and present in spaceports for thousands of years (Weequay) are strong claims about antiquity — they are simply imprecise about the campaign year.

**Six species were closed at book grade.** Duros and Zabrak are named as Republic worlds in the KOTOR Campaign Guide's Timeline. Kel Dor joined the Republic over six thousand years ago per its own RCR entry. Bothan's homeworld is described in detail in the Gazetteer at the campaign date. Trandoshan's Republic contact is established through the Wookiee entry in the UAA. Human needs no citation.

**One species has a mechanical oddity no one else shares.** Devaronian carries two ability-adjustment arrays, selected by sex. It is not a subrace, not a choice, and the schema question of where to put it remains open.

**And Bith was listed as having no confirmed source — but it does.** The *Ultimate Alien Anthology* carries a full Bith entry at pages 23–24. The earlier extractor's note was incorrect.

**Two of four uploaded rulebooks failed intake.** The file labelled as the Revised Core Rulebook was the West End Games D6 REUP (Womp Rat Press) — a different game. A second file was fan homebrew with no standing. Both were caught at intake before any values were read from them. No citation in this chapter traces to either rejected document; every RCR value was read from the correct book after it arrived.

*Thirty-one species. Fourteen from the Revised Core Rulebook, seven from the Ultimate Alien Anthology, seven converted from the KOTOR Campaign Guide, one found in the UAA after being listed as missing, and two authored from scratch for this setting. The provenance varies, and now you know where each one stands.*
