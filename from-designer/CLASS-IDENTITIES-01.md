# CLASS-IDENTITIES-01 — what each class is, in plain terms

**⚠ This is an alignment check, not a rules document. No arithmetic, no citations, no derivations. If any line below is not what you pictured, that class needs revisiting before anything else proceeds.**

**Eighteen base classes. Twelve standard, six Force.**

**⚠ Correction: I said "fifteen base classes" in conversation last turn and then wrote "the full standard twelve plus the six Force base." Twelve and six is eighteen. The count was wrong and the list was right.**

# The standard twelve

**Soldier**
The professional fighter. More attack options than anyone in the game, the fewest skills, no Force.
*Only one who can step in front of an ally and take the hit meant for them.*

**Scout**
The one who walks in first and comes back out. Best saves in the game by a wide margin, widest skill list.
*Only one who can share a successful dodge — when the grenade lands, an ally gets your roll instead of theirs.*

**Smuggler**
The fringe operator. A ship, contacts, light fingers, and the deadliest opening strike in the game from concealment.
*Only one who shoots first when a conversation turns hostile — before anybody has acted.*

**Bounty Hunter**
Hits as often and as hard as a Soldier and knows twice as much.
*Only one who can reliably take a target alive rather than killing it.*

**Engineer**
Systems rather than hands. Slicing, security, science.
*Only one who can seize control of an enemy droid in the middle of a fight.*

**Machinist**
Hands rather than systems. Repair, salvage, demolitions.
*Only one who can repair a droid other than themselves — nobody else in the game can touch an ally droid.*

**Marksman**
The largest hit die in the game and the only class built on Constitution. Made to endure rather than to acquire.
*Only one who gets a last turn after the hit that should have put them down.*

**Agent**
The institutional infiltrator — Republic or Sith intelligence. Gets in by being taken for someone else, where the Smuggler gets in by not being seen.
*Only one who can talk an enemy out of the fight while the fight is happening.*

**Explorer**
The party's scholar, and the reason a non-Jedi can be one. Archaeology, xenology and science used to be a Jedi monopoly in a campaign about finding Rakatan ruins.
*Only one who can stop the campaign's hardest obstacles from scaling with the party.*

**Doctor**
Medicine's owner, in a system where it had sat on five classes and been central to none.
*Only one who can heal wounds rather than vitality — the slow track that otherwise mends one point a day.*

**Brawler**
The unarmed fighter, and the only non-Force class that can make unarmed damage scale at all.
*Only one whose strikes ignore armour — at the top, entirely.*

**Duelist**
One weapon, no shield, no second blade.
*Only one who gets sharply better when there is exactly one enemy in front of them, and no better at all in a crowd.*

# The Force six

**⚠ The three Jedi and three Sith are deliberate mirrors — same hit dice, same Force dice, same acquisition rates. What separates them is the *shape* of what they buy, not the numbers.**

> **A Jedi's class ability is conditional and answers a situation. A Sith's is unconditional and raises a number.**

**That is not a design choice we made. It is what the source data does, and we found it rather than inventing it.**

**Jedi Guardian**
The blade first, the Force held in reserve. Smallest Force pool of the three Jedi, largest hit die.
*Only one who can cross a room and strike in the same action.*

**Jedi Sentinel**
Hunts dark siders rather than duelling them. Slicing, stealth and security — the infiltrator of the Order.
*Immune to fear, then to stun, then to paralysis. Past a certain point almost nothing can take a turn away from them.*

**Jedi Consular**
The scholar and diplomat. Knows ten more Force powers than any other class and is the only one who can cast all fight.
*Her sustained powers last twice as long — and at the top, they keep running on the party after she goes down.*

**Sith Warrior**
The Guardian's mirror, and the inversion of it. Where the Jedi buys an answer to a situation, the Warrior buys a bigger number.
*Hits harder the more badly hurt he is. The feature never fires while he is winning.*

**Sith Inquisitor**
The Consular's mirror. The same depth of knowledge spent on damage rather than healing — every damage power in the game is dark.
*Nobody saves easily against her powers, and at the top a successful save still leaves you slowed.*

**Sith Assassin**
Kills from concealment. Fewer feats than any Sith, the deadliest single opening on the dark side.
*Only one who can disappear again immediately after the kill, without spending the round to do it.*

# ⚠ Where I am least confident you and I agree

**Three, and I would rather hear now than after the prestige classes are built on them.**

**The Agent.** **It is the only class in the roster that never had a stated purpose — every other new class had one written down. I inferred *institutional infiltrator* from the fact that two covert prestige classes exist and no covert base class did.** **If you pictured something else, this is the one to say so about.**

**The Duelist.** **Its ability switches off the moment a second enemy closes, which is most fights in the current playtest suite.** **That is deliberate — it rewards a player who isolates a target — but it means the class is quiet in exactly the encounters the scenarios test.**

**The Explorer.** **Its ability is spent once per *adventure* rather than per encounter. It is the only class in the game with a feature at that scale, and nothing else in the corpus works that way.**

# On the Pirate

**Added to the list. Not started — `§1` above is the block and this waits behind it.**

**⚠ One thing to settle before any numbers, because it is the failure this project keeps repeating.**

**A Pirate overlaps three classes that already exist:**

    Smuggler        a ship, the fringe, crime, stealth
    Bounty Hunter   takes things from people by force, Mandalorian flavour
    Brawler         boarding actions are unarmed and ugly

> **The Smuggler has already absorbed one class and displaced another. The tech pair had to be split at 89% overlap. The Agent nearly repeated it and only measurement caught that.**

**The distinction I would build on, if you agree with it: a Smuggler works alone and avoids the fight; a Pirate has a crew and starts it.** **That would make the Pirate the game's only group-combat class — abilities that scale with allies present rather than with your own numbers — and nothing currently occupies that space.**

**Three questions before I draft anything:**

**Is the Pirate a crew leader, or a solo raider?** *The first is a real gap. The second is a Smuggler who fights.*

**Does it come with ship rules?** *Nothing in the corpus covers ship combat, and a Pirate whose identity is a ship needs a system that does not exist.*

**Standard base, or prestige?** *If the answer to the first is "crew leader," it may belong beside `Officer` in the prestige list rather than as a nineteenth base class.*
