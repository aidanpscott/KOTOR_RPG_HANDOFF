# 2 — The band under `held`, and why it has to move up two

**Derived. Notation: `P` = picks at 30, `G` = granted tier-1 attack chains, `T` = `P + G` total tiers a class can place.**

**`G` counts only real chains.** **`Strike` and `Shoot` are the free baseline and are not trees; `Sneak Attack` is a feat rider — `PT-29`.**

**A tree entered absorbs 1, 2 or 3 tiers. So `N` trees absorb between `N` and `3N`.**

> **A class can spend its whole budget only if `3N ≥ T`.** **Below that, tiers have nowhere to go.**

**Stranding at the current bands:**

| | `T` | at band floor | at band mid | at band top |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Combat** *(G=1)* | 37 | **N=11 → 4 stranded** | **N=12 → 1 stranded** | **N=13 → ok** |
| **Middle** *(G=2)* | 29 | **N=8 → 5 stranded** | **N=9 → 2 stranded** | **N=10 → ok** |
| **Middle** *(G=1)* | 28 | **N=8 → 4 stranded** | **N=9 → 1 stranded** | **N=10 → ok** |
| **Specialist** *(G=1)* | 19 | **N=5 → 4 stranded** | **N=6 → 1 stranded** | **N=7 → ok** |
| **Specialist** *(G=2)* | 20 | **N=5 → 5 stranded** | **N=6 → 2 stranded** | **N=7 → ok** |

> **⚠ Only the top of each band is spendable. Every other value in every band strands picks with nothing legal to buy.**

**This is the mirror image of `FINDINGS-01 §4`.** **Under `finished` only 12 / 9 / 6 were reachable; under `held` only 13 / 10 / 7 are spendable.** **The definition changed and the band failed in the opposite direction, which is the tell that the band was never sitting where the picks are.**

**A stranded pick is worse than a wasted one.** **Three currencies, no crossover — `ATTACKS-01 §11.1` — so it cannot become a feat or a skill point. It is a number on the sheet that buys nothing, and the player who finds it will assume they have misread the rules.**

## 2.1 What the dial actually is

**Derived. With `T` tiers over exactly `N` trees, the number of trees you can take to tier 3 is `⌊(T − N) ⁄ 2⌋`.**

*Each tree needs 1 tier to enter; every capstone needs 2 more on top.*

**So `N` is a depth-against-breadth dial and it is a good one:**

| Combat, `T` = 37 | N=13 | N=14 | N=15 | N=16 | N=17 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Capstones** | **12** | 11 | 11 | 10 | 10 |

**Low `N`: few trees, all of them finished. High `N`: more answers, fewer capstones.** **That is the trade `PT-69` describes, and it works — two above where the band is printed.**

## 2.2 Proposed bands — **authored**

| Rate | Was | **Proposed** | Floor is | Top is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Combat** | 11–13 | **13–16** | 12 capstones, nothing spare | 10 capstones, six more answers |
| **Middle** | 8–10 | **10–13** | 9 capstones | 7–8 capstones |
| **Specialist** | 5–7 | **7–10** | 6 capstones | 4 capstones |

**Every value is spendable. The floor of each is the deepest legal build and the top is the widest.**

**⚠ `MULTICLASS-01 §5`'s table and the player-facing text both print picks ÷ 3 — Soldier 12, splitter 9 or 10.** **Under `held` those are *capstones*, not chains, and both passages call them chains.** **The numbers survive; the word is wrong in two places and one of them is player-facing.**

# 4 — Marksman and Engineer cannot spend an attack budget at all

**This is the blocking item and it is arithmetic, not a judgement.**

**`ATTACKS-01 §4` gates the rosters by chassis, not by class:**

    Ranged      11 chains    organics, Assassin and Battle droids
    Melee       11 chains    organics only
    Lightsaber  14 chains    Force classes, the form held -> 2 chains

**`CLASS-ATTACKS-01 §4` then denies the Marksman `Strike` — *"No melee — `ATTACKS-05` closes it to every droid"* — and `ACTION-ECONOMY-01 §18.2` grants both classes droid plating instead of armour.** **`PT-75` dropped the droid/organic class split. Both passages predate it.**

**Accessible trees, and what they can absorb:**

| | Access | Max tiers `3N` | `T` | **Stranded** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Marksman** *(Battle/Assassin chassis)* | **11** ranged only | 33 | **37** | **4** |
| **Engineer** *(Astromech)* | **5** — `PREGENS-01 §7`, temporary ruling | 15 | **28** | **13** |
| *Soldier, for comparison* | 22 | 66 | 37 | 0 |

> **⚠ The Engineer strands thirteen of its twenty-eight tiers. Nearly half the budget buys nothing.**

**The Marksman is a `Combat`-rate class whose accessible roster is smaller than the minimum spendable chain count for its own rate — 11 against 13.** **There is no number I can put in its Chains column that is both legal and spendable.**

**Three ways out, and this is the ruling I need:**

**One — melee opens to organics of both classes.** Access goes to 22 and both assign normally. **Costs nothing mechanically; the restriction was always a chassis rule wearing a class rule's clothes.**

**Two — the restriction is genuinely per chassis, and chain count becomes a chassis-modified number rather than a class number.** **Honest, and it makes the Chains column the first in the game that is not a property of the class.**

**Three — the ranged roster grows.** **Expensive, and it fixes the Marksman without touching the Engineer, whose five-chain access is the real hole.**

**⚠ Whichever way it goes, `PREGENS-01 §7`'s five-chain Astromech ruling is doing load-bearing work as a *temporary* ruling.** **T4-K9 currently holds seven picks against five accessible trees, which is already over.**

# 5 — One thing about `watch.py`, since it governs the loop

**`watch.py` line 14 sets its working directory to `dirname(__file__)/handoff`.** **The file sits at the repo root, so that resolves to `KOTOR_RPG_HANDOFF/handoff`, which does not exist.**

**`ls()` returns an empty set, the loop finds nothing, and it exits printing *"nothing new in 240s. 0 file(s) already present: none"*.**

> **⚠ A clean-looking negative against the wrong directory.** **It is the same shape as `PT-84` and `SKILLS-01 §9.3` — the right method pointed at the wrong target — and it would make me report STALLED every time while replies sat in `to-designer/` unread.**

**I have not edited it; the repo root is yours.** **I am running a corrected copy outside the tree, pointed at the real path. The one-character fix is to drop the `'handoff'` component.**

**Second, smaller: `ls()` runs `git reset --hard origin/main`, which discards uncommitted work.** **I commit and push before every watch, so it is safe as used — worth knowing before anyone drafts in the tree and then watches.**
