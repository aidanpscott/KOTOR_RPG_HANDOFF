# 1 — `ATTACKS-05` states three different chain counts, in itself

**It is a generated file — *"Generated by `scripts/gen_docs.py`. Do not hand-edit — regenerate from `data/attacks.json`."***

| Where | Count |
|---|---|
| **Header, line 5** | *"Eight axes. **14 chains**, 34 entries."* |
| **Its own axis table** | Flurry · Dual Strike · Cleave · Critical Strike · Power Attack · Quick Attack · Sweep Attack · Disarming Strike · Guarding Stance · Parry — **10** |
| **Its own gate table** | the same **10** |
| **Its own prose, `§ The four ability gates`** | *"Four gates against **eleven** chains leaves seven open to anyone."* |

**`ATTACKS-01 §4` says melee is 11 chains and 31 entries, and that reconciles:** **10 tiered chains at 3 tiers = 30 entries, plus `Strike`, which the gate table lists with no level and no gate. 11 chains, 31 entries.**

> **So `ATTACKS-01` is right and the generated header is wrong.** **14 is the lightsaber roster's count and it has leaked into the melee header — `FEATS-LIBRARY-01`'s own header repeats it, so the leak is upstream of both.**

**⚠ The point is not the number. It is that a file marked *do not hand-edit, regenerate from source* disagrees with itself, which means `scripts/gen_docs.py` or `data/attacks.json` carries the defect.** **Regenerating will not fix it and will reproduce it.**

**This also closes one line of `FINDINGS-10 §3` item 16.** **The melee count is 11/31; the 14 in two headers is wrong. The ranged and total figures still need `ATTACKS-04` and `ATTACKS-07`.**

# 2 — ⚠ Ability gates are a second cap on chain count and the model does not know about them

**`ATTACKS-05`'s gate table, derived:**

    Strength 12      Cleave · Power Attack · Sweep Attack
    Dexterity 13     Flurry
    both             Disarming Strike
    none             Dual Strike · Critical Strike · Quick Attack · Guarding Stance · Parry

**And the document works the consequence itself:** *"A Guardian at Strength 16 / Dexterity 10 loses Flurry and Disarming Strike; a Smuggler at Dexterity 16 / Strength 10 loses Power Attack, Sweep Attack, and Disarming Strike."*

> **A chain count caps how many trees a character may enter. Ability gates cap which trees exist for them at all — and unlike the chain count, that cap varies between two characters of the same class.**

**Checked against the ten assignments and none currently breaks:**

    Consular   Str 10 / Dex 14   loses 4 melee   access 20   N=13  ok
    Smuggler   Str 10 / Dex 16   loses 3 melee   access 19   N= 8  ok
    Scout      Str 12 / Dex 16   loses none      access 22   N=17  ok
    Guardian   Str 14 / Dex 14   loses none      access 24   N=18  ok

*Spreads from `k2_classes.2da`'s recommended arrays.*

**⚠ But it is close for the Consular, and it is a live problem for the twelve unwritten classes.** **A **Doctor** or an **Explorer** built on Intelligence and Wisdom could clear neither gate and lose five of eleven melee chains — and if either is given a high chain count it will not be reachable.**

**Recommendation, authored: when a class's chain count is assigned, state it against the class's recommended array and note the floor.** **One line per class, and it stops a number being printed that some builds of that class cannot use.**

**⚠ And `ATTACKS-04`'s gates are unknown to me. If the ranged roster gates on Dexterity the way melee gates on Strength, the Marksman — Constitution primary, `14 / 14 / 16 / 8 / 8 / 8` — should be checked before its rate is settled.**

# 3 — `Precise Shot I–V`, as `REPLY-10` asked

**Derived. `PRECISE_SHOT_I`–`V` are granted to `sct` and to nobody else, at levels 4, 8, 12, 16, 20.** *The `XXXPRECISE_SHOT_I–V` rows beside them are cut content granted to no one — the same live-versus-cut pairing as `Force Focus`.*

**`ATTACKS-01 §9.1` already records what happened to them:** KOTOR's Precise Shot did `+1` damage **and** enemy Blaster Bolt Deflection `−2` across five tiers; **the deflection half became the `Marksman` feat and the name was reused for the Precision tree's tier 1.**

**So the source's five-tier Scout grant is our three-tier `Marksman` feat chain — `−2 / −4 / −6` to an enemy's BBD.**

**Recommendation, authored: grant the `Marksman` feat chain to the Scout at 1 / 6 / 12, exactly as `Targeting` was repriced.** **Five source tiers to three, on the ladder every other granted class chain uses, and left purchasable by anyone — *a grant is not a lock*.**

**Priced: it does nothing against anyone without Blaster Bolt Deflection, which is everyone who is not a Force user.** **Against a Jedi it is `−6` on an opposed roll by level 12. That is a narrow counter and the Scout is the class the source pointed it at.**

## 3.1 ⚠ And there is a name collision

**`Marksman` is a feat in `FEATS-LIBRARY-01` and a class in `CLASS-ROSTER-01`.**

**The project has already renamed twice for exactly this** — `SKILLS-01 §12.4`: *"Our skill feat was called Spacer, which collided with the Duros grant — it is now Freetrader. Our Acrobatics-and-Swim feat was called Evasion, which collided with the Scout's damage-avoidance feat — it is now Nimble."*

**Granting the `Marksman` feat to the **Scout** and not to the **Marksman** makes the collision actively misleading rather than merely untidy.** **The feat should be renamed. `Bolt Breaker` or `Deflection Breaker` — the class name is settled and the feat's is not.**

# 4 — The **owner unassigned** bucket, worked in one pass

**I proposed this in `FINDINGS-09 §5`. `FEATS-LIBRARY-01 §5` files fourteen chains as *Restricted — owner unassigned*. `feat.2da` names an owner for nine of them.**

## 4.1 Six assign cleanly

| Feat | Granted to | At |
|---|---|---|
| **`Force Channel`** *(was `Force Focus`)* | **Jedi Consular** | 1 / 6 / 12 |
| **`Jedi Defense`** | **all nine Force columns** | 1 |
| **`Jedi Sense`** | **the three base Jedi only** | 1 |
| **`Knight Sense`** | **Jedi Master · Sith Lord** | 1 |
| **`Master Sense`** | **Sith Assassin · Sith Marauder · Jedi Watchman · Jedi Weaponmaster** | 1 |

**`Regenerate Force Points` also has owners — Sith Lord and Jedi Master, at 1st.** **⚠ The two classes with a Force die of 10, which is the pattern rather than a coincidence.**

## 4.2 ⚠ `Jedi Sense` is not a chain and the library prints it as one

**`FEATS-LIBRARY-01` lists `Jedi Sense` → `Knight Sense` → `Master Sense` as a three-tier chain.**

**Derived: they are three parallel grants to three disjoint sets of classes.** **A Sith Assassin is granted `Master Sense` at 1st level and is never granted `Jedi Sense` at all — `jsn`-style tiering does not apply.**

> **If it is read as a chain, a Sith Assassin must buy two feats it will never use to reach the one the source hands it at 1st level.**

**Recommendation: print them as three entries, not three tiers, each with its class list.** **The scaling formulas differ too — `level/6`, `level/8`, `level/5` — which is not how a replacement ladder behaves anywhere else in the corpus.**

## 4.3 One is misfiled and four are genuinely unowned

**⚠ `Empathy` appears twice.** **It is in the unassigned bucket *and* in `SKILLS-01 §12`'s ten universal skill feats, covering Persuade and Medicine.** **It is a universal skill feat and should not be in the restricted section at all.**

**Genuinely granted to nobody, and correctly universal: `Finesse: Melee Weapons`, `Finesse: Lightsabers`, `Stealth Run`, `Regenerate Vitality Points`.** **Recording the considered yes so nobody re-raises them.**

**And `Weapon Proficiency: Lightsaber` is granted to seven of nine Force columns — not to Sith Lord or Jedi Master, which are granted `Weapon Focus: Lightsaber` instead.** **That is coherent: both are prestige classes entered from a base class that already granted proficiency.** **Worth stating, because it is the first evidence in the data that prestige grant lists assume the base class's.**

# 5 — The three save progressions, named and extended

**This is `FINDINGS-10 §3` item 4 and it blocks all twelve unwritten classes.**

**Derived from `cls_st_soldier.2da` and `cls_st_jedi_g.2da`, all twenty rows, fitted exactly:**

| Ladder | Closed form | L1 | L10 | L20 | **L30** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Strong** | **`2 + ⌊L ⁄ 2⌋`** | 2 | 7 | 12 | **17** |
| **Hybrid** | **`⌊(2L + 6) ⁄ 5⌋`** | 1 | 5 | 9 | **13** |
| **Weak** | **`⌊L ⁄ 3⌋`** | 0 | 3 | 6 | **10** |

**All three verified against every row of both files. The hybrid increments on a 3-2-3-2 alternation from level 2 — levels 2, 5, 7, 10, 12, 15, 17, 20 — which is why no simple half-rate expression fits it.**

**⚠ The source tables stop at 20 and `PT-55` sets the ceiling at 30.** **The L30 column above is the closed forms extended, and it is authored on that basis: the forms are exact for 1–20 and the extension assumes they continue.**

**Where the ten classes sit:**

    strong / strong / strong    Scout                          36
    strong / strong / hybrid    Jedi Guardian · Jedi Sentinel   33
    strong / hybrid / strong    Jedi Consular                   33
    strong / strong / weak      Bounty Hunter (PT-93)           30
    weak   / strong / strong    Engineer (proposed)             30
    strong / weak   / weak      Soldier · Marksman              24
    weak   / strong / weak      Smuggler · Machinist            24

**Three totals — 36, 33, 30, 24 — and the twelve unwritten classes can be placed on them without inventing a fourth.**

**⚠ One thing the table makes visible.** **The Scout is the only class at 36 and there are two at 30 and four at 24.** **If any of the twelve is meant to be defensively distinctive, 33 and 30 are where the room is; 24 is already crowded.**
