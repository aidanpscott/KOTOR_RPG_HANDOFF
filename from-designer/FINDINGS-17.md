# 3 — The ladders do not make the twelve writable. The assignment rule does.

**`FINDINGS-12 §5` places the ten and observes that *"33 and 30 are where the room is; 24 is already crowded."* That is a hint. Here is the rule it implies, derived from the ten placements.**

## 3.1 Rate does not predict the save profile

    Combat       Soldier 24 · Marksman 24 · Guardian 33
    Middle       Bounty Hunter 30 · Engineer 30 · Sentinel 33 · Scout 36
    Specialist   Smuggler 24 · Machinist 24 · Consular 33

**Every rate carries at least two different totals and `Combat` and `Specialist` both bottom out at 24. Rate is the wrong axis.**

## 3.2 What does predict it — the count of strong saves tracks how many jobs the class has

| Strongs | Total | Classes | The shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **24** | Soldier · Marksman · Smuggler · Machinist | **one job.** Hit things, or fix things |
| **2** | **30** | Bounty Hunter · Engineer | **two jobs.** Fights and tracks; fights and repairs |
| **2 + hybrid** | **33** | all three Jedi | **a second system.** The hybrid is what the Force pays for |
| **3** | **36** | Scout | **the generalist.** One class, and the source's own anomaly |

**And *which* saves follow the primary ability, in all ten:**

    Strength or Constitution primary  ->  Fortitude strong    Soldier · Marksman · Guardian
    Dexterity primary                ->  Reflex strong       Smuggler · Machinist · Sentinel · Scout · BH
    Wisdom primary                   ->  Will strong         Consular

**⚠ One exception, and it is mine.** **The Engineer at `6 / 12 / 12` is Dexterity-primary with a strong Will, proposed in `FINDINGS-07 §2.2` on the argument that *a mind that does not stop working under pressure* is what separates it from the Machinist.** **It is the only placement that departs from the ability rule and it should be marked as a departure rather than read as precedent.**

## 3.3 The rule, authored

> **A class takes Fortitude, Reflex or Will strong according to its primary ability. It takes a second strong if it has two jobs, a hybrid instead if the second is the Force, and a third only if breadth is the whole class.**

**Applied to the twelve, this is a one-line decision each rather than an open question:**

    Doctor · Explorer      Wisdom or Intelligence primary -> Will strong; second job? -> 30
    Brawler · Duelist      Strength or Dexterity          -> one job -> 24
    Agent                  Dexterity, two jobs            -> 30
    Sith Warrior           Strength, second system        -> 33, hybrid on Will
    Sith Inquisitor        Wisdom, second system          -> 33, hybrid on Reflex
    Sith Assassin          Dexterity, second system       -> 33, hybrid on Will

**⚠ Marked as a shape, not a set of rulings.** **Each of the twelve still needs its primary ability set first, and none has one.** **But the ladder plus this rule means the save line is a consequence of two decisions already required rather than a thirteenth decision.**

**⚠ And the crowding observation survives and sharpens: four classes sit at 24 and one at 36.** **If any of the twelve is meant to be defensively distinctive, the room is at 30, and 36 should stay the Scout's alone — it is the source's anomaly and nothing explains it, so authoring a second one would be inventing a pattern where there is a curiosity.**
