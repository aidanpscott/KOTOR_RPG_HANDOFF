# FINDINGS-22 — What separates a Sith from a Jedi. Then the Warrior and the Inquisitor.

**`REPLY-20` asked for the principle before the numbers. `§1` is the principle and it is derived, not authored.**

---

# 1 — The source already answers it, and the answer is a *shape*, not a number

**Derived, `feat.2da`, every granted class chain with proficiencies and `Sneak Attack` stripped:**

    jgd   Force Jump  x3            jsn   Force Immunity  x3       jcn   Force Focus  x3
    sma   Ignore Pain x3            sld   Dark Side Corruption     sas   -- nothing --
          Increase Combat Damage x3       Regenerate Force Points

> **⚠ Every base Jedi has a three-tier class chain. No Sith base class has one at all.**

**And the two Sith prestige chains that do exist are the same effects the Jedi prestige gets, reshaped:**

| Jedi Weaponmaster | Sith Marauder | The difference |
|---|---|---|
| **`Inner Strength`** −5 / −10 / −15% damage taken | **`Ignore Pain`** −5 / −10 / −15% damage taken | **none. Identical mechanic, different word** |
| **`Increase Melee Damage`** +2/+4/+6, **melee only** | **`Increase Combat Damage`** +2/+4/+6, **unarmed, melee *or ranged*** | **⚠ the Sith version covers a weapon the Jedi's does not** |
| **`Deflect`** — Blaster Bolt Deflection scaling with level | **— none —** | **⚠ granted to `jwm` alone in either game** |

## 1.1 The principle

> **A Jedi chain is conditional and answers a situation. A Sith chain is unconditional and raises a number.**

**`Force Jump` needs a target more than ten metres away. `Force Immunity` is pure defence and does nothing on your turn. `Force Focus` multiplies a buff you had to cast first.** **All three are tools that require the right moment.**

**`Ignore Pain` is always on. `Increase Combat Damage` is always on, and it does not care what you are holding.** **Neither needs a moment.**

**⚠ And `Deflect` is the sharpest single fact: the Jedi have a defensive answer to the setting's primary weapon and the Sith are given none.** **The Sith solution to a blaster is to carry one — which is exactly what `Increase Combat Damage` covering ranged says.**

## 1.2 Why this makes identical stat blocks acceptable

**`PT-125`'s mirror gives six Force base classes three stat blocks. That is only a problem if the stat block is where identity lives, and `ATTACKS-01 §11.4` already ruled it is not:**

> *"Breadth buys the right answer more often; it does not buy more actions. Depth is where power lives."*

**A Jedi and a Sith with the same hit die, Force die and rate are not the same character if one buys conditional answers and the other buys flat increases.** **They diverge in play at the point of choosing what to spend on, which is where every other class in this system diverges.**

**⚠ And the Force partition does most of the remaining work before any class rule fires.** **`PARTITION-01`: 30 dark powers, 28 light. `PREGENS-01 §2`: seventeen damage powers and *every one is dark*.** **A light Jedi's pool goes on healing, control and buffs; a Sith's goes on damage. That is the largest mechanical gap between any two characters in the game and it is already ruled.**

## 1.3 The balance check, because a flat increase is the shape that usually wins

**Flat is not strictly better here, and the corpus says why.** **`ATTACKS-01 §11.4`: one declaration per round, and 107 of 110 entries consume it.** **A flat `+3` applies to whichever attack you declared; a conditional tool changes *which* attack is available.**

**At low level flat wins, because you have few options and they are all the same.** **At high level the Jedi has twelve capstones and the Sith has a bigger number on all of them.** **That is a real trade with a crossover, not a dominance.**

## 2.1 The one skill that changes

**The Guardian's six are Alertness · Athletics · Awareness · Mysticism · Persuade · Medicine.**

**`PT-81` cut `Intimidate` from the Guardian with the reason stated:** *"`Intimidate` now sits on Soldier, Bounty Hunter and Marksman only — the three classes that frighten people for a living. **A Jedi persuades.**"*

> **⚠ That ruling wrote the Sith Warrior's list without knowing it.** **If a Jedi persuades, a Sith intimidates — and `Medicine` is the skill a Sith has least use for.**

**One swap, `Medicine` → `Intimidate`, and it is the only skill difference between the two classes.** **That is thin on its own and it is not carrying the identity; `§1.1` is.**

## 2.2 Chain count — 15

    Soldier          14 chains   13 capstones   no second system at all
    Sith Warrior     15          12             Force, spent on damage
    Jedi Guardian    18          11             Force, spent on answers

**One tree wider than the Soldier and three narrower than the Guardian.** **The Guardian's breadth is a light-side argument — it holds answers because it is trying to end the fight without killing. The Warrior is not.**

## 2.3 The class feature — `Unrelenting`

**Sith Warrior-only chain, 1 / 4 / 8. Unconditional in shape, per `§1.1`, with a trigger that is a state rather than a tactical window.**

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Unrelenting`** | 1 | **While your vitality is below half, +2 damage on every attack you make.** No declaration, no action, no limit per encounter. |
| › **`Past Caring`** | 4 | **+4 below half.** |
| ›› **`Nothing Left To Lose`** | 8 | **+6 below half, and +2 to attack while below a quarter.** |

**Derived from the pair it inverts.** **`Inner Strength` and `Ignore Pain` are the same −5/−10/−15% damage taken; the Jedi and Sith prestige lines both answer being hurt.** **The Jedi answer is *take less*. There is no Sith offensive equivalent in the source and this is it: the Sith answer is *do more*.**

**Priced.** **`+6` damage at level 8 against Korr's `Barrage` at 27.3 a round is roughly a 22% increase — while at half vitality or less.** **`Weapon Specialization` is `+2` for one feat unconditionally; this is `+6` for three feats, conditionally, on a d10 class that has to be losing to collect.**

**Not dominant:** **it never fires while you are winning.** **⚠ Which is the opposite of the `Vornskr's Frenzy` failure — that reduced every defender to Defence 10 and was cut. This one requires the Warrior to be at risk and gives the enemy the choice of whether to trigger it.**

**The moment:** **the fight turns, he should be finished, and he hits harder than he did at full strength.** **Malak on the Star Forge.**

## 3.1 Chain count — 11

    Smuggler          8 chains   7 capstones
    Sith Assassin     9          6
    Sith Inquisitor  11          5
    Jedi Consular    13          4

**Two trees narrower than the Consular and one capstone deeper.** **`FINDINGS-10 §1.2` put the Consular near the top because attacks are her third system and she wants an answer available rather than a finished one.** **The Inquisitor's powers are damage — the dark side holds all seventeen — so she reaches for the blade less often and means it more when she does.**

## 3.2 ⚠ Powers known needs a ruling and I will not guess it

**`classpowergain.2da` gives the **Consular** an accelerated column — 11 at level 8 and **41** at 30, against the Guardian's and Sentinel's 31.** **It is the only base class with extra grants beyond +1 per level.**

**There is no Sith equivalent to mirror.** **`sld` is prestige and prints 2 at 1st with 38 by 30; `sma` and `sas` print 1 at 1st.**

**Two readings:**

**Mirror the Consular at 41.** **The Inquisitor is the caster of its side and the mirror has been applied on every other axis.**

**Give it the Guardian's 31.** **⚠ Then the Inquisitor has the Consular's Force die and hit die and 10 fewer powers — which makes it a strictly worse Consular, and the mirror was adopted specifically to avoid that.**

**I would take 41 and I have written the record that way, marked pending.** **The alternative is not a smaller Inquisitor, it is a broken one.**

## 3.3 The class feature — `Dominion`

**Sith Inquisitor-only chain, 1 / 4 / 8.**

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Dominion`** | 1 | **+1 to the saving throw DC of every Force power you cast.** |
| › **`Advanced Dominion`** | 4 | **+2.** |
| ›› **`Master Dominion`** | 8 | **+3**, and **a target that saves against one of your powers takes the `Slowed` condition** rather than nothing — `ACTION-ECONOMY-01 §19.3`. |

**Unconditional and a flat increase, per `§1.1`, and it is the exact inversion of the Consular's chain.** **`Force Focus` extends the duration of powers you cast on your own side. `Dominion` raises the difficulty of powers you cast on theirs.**

> **One helps her party stay standing longer. The other stops the enemy from getting a turn.**

**Priced.** **`+3` DC is roughly `+15%` on the land rate of every power, for three of eleven lifetime feats — the same proportional cost the Assassin pays for `Vanish`.**

**⚠ The capstone is deliberately not more DC.** **`ACTION-ECONOMY-01 §19.1` already rules that a saved stun becomes `Slowed` — *"a stun is never wasted"* — and this generalises that one clause to the Inquisitor's whole roster.** **It uses machinery that exists rather than adding a fourth condition.**

**Not dominant:** **it does nothing to a power without a save, nothing to damage rolls, and nothing at all to `Throw Lightsaber` or a blade.** **A Sith who wants to hit things is a Warrior.**
