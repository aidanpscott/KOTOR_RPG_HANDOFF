# FINDINGS-27 — Explorer revised, Duelist adjusted, Pirate drafted

**Three owner directions from conversation. `§1` and `§2` change classes already adopted; `§3` is new.**

# 1 — Explorer: the per-adventure feature is withdrawn

**Owner:** *"The idea is that explorers can be good in combat, but they are the guys you want when you're not in combat: they are the guys you go in the ruins with and get stuck and they can solve the puzzle. Think of them like the Indiana Jones of Star Wars."*

**⚠ `FINDINGS-23 §6.2`'s `Prior Study` is withdrawn.** **It was declared in advance, spent once per adventure, and rewarded guessing right about what you would meet.** **That is a resource, not a competence, and the class is a competence.**

**And I flagged the shape myself in `CLASS-IDENTITIES-01` as one of the three I was least confident about — *the only class in the game with a feature at that scale*. It was the wrong scale.**

## 1.1 The replacement — `Read the Ruin`

**Continuous, out of combat, and it makes the party's problem *not being stuck* rather than *rolling higher*.**

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Read the Ruin`** | 1 | **When you fail a knowledge, `Security` or `Awareness` check to understand, open or navigate something, you learn *why*** — one concrete fact about what would work. **You may retry once that condition is met.** |
| › **`Methodical`** | 4 | As above, and **you may take 10 on those checks even under pressure**, where taking 10 is otherwise unavailable. |
| ›› **`Nothing Is Sealed Forever`** | 8 | As above, and **once per scene one such obstacle drops one difficulty tier** for the whole party — `SKILL-RESOLUTION-01 §2`'s ladder, so Heroic 30 becomes Formidable 25. |

**Why this shape.** **`SKILL-RESOLUTION-01 §2` says the thing that matters at a table is *"Auto-succeeds at"* — the total that beats a DC by taking 10 — *"because most skill use is not under pressure."*** **The Explorer's tier 2 is that sentence turned into a class ability: it is never not taking 10.**

**And tier 1 is the Indiana Jones move rather than a bonus.** **A failed roll normally ends the attempt. For an Explorer it produces information, so the party is delayed rather than stopped — which is what *"they get stuck and he solves it"* actually describes.**

**⚠ It also lands on an open item.** **`SKILL-RESOLUTION-01 §7` lists knowledge-skill DCs as unset — *"common 10, specialist 20, lost 30 is a starting shape, not a decision."*** **Tier 3 moves an obstacle one tier, so that ladder needs to exist before tier 3 is playable. Flagged, not blocking — the class works at tiers 1 and 2 without it.**

**Not dominant:** **it does nothing in combat, and it cannot make an impossible check succeed — it tells you what would.**

# 2 — Duelist: no longer switches off

**Owner:** *"Don't make him useless in a fight with multiple enemies. His special feats are tailored to a single-handed weapon with no offhand — and not a two-handed weapon either — and fighting 1-on-1."*

**⚠ `FINDINGS-23 §4.2`'s `Single Combat` required *exactly one enemy within reach*. Two enemies closing turned it off entirely.** **Revised: it now attaches to a chosen enemy rather than to the state of the battlefield.**

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Single Combat`** | 1 | **At the start of your turn choose one enemy. +2 attack and +2 Defence against that enemy** until your next turn. |
| › **`Nobody Interrupts`** | 4 | **+4 against your chosen enemy, and +1 against everyone else.** |
| ›› **`Just Us Then`** | 8 | **+4 and +1 as above**, and **your chosen enemy gains no benefit from flanking against you.** |

> **The focus is preserved and the cliff is gone.** **A Duelist in a crowd still has a chosen enemy and still gets something against the rest; a Duelist facing one opponent has everything pointed at them.**

## 2.1 The wielding condition, which was missing

**Owner: one-handed weapon, empty off hand, and not a two-hander. `FINDINGS-23` did not state it.**

> **⚠ `Single Combat` requires a single one-handed weapon and an empty off hand.** **A two-handed weapon does not qualify, and neither does a second weapon or a shield.**

**⚠ This is *not* the same condition as the `Dueling` feat.** **`FEATS-LIBRARY-01` gives `Dueling` to *"unarmed or wielding a single blaster pistol, melee weapon, or lightsaber"* — which does not exclude a two-hander and does include unarmed.** **The Duelist's chain is narrower on purpose. A Duelist will normally hold both and they are two separate conditions, both met by the same hand.**

**⚠ And it creates a real build tension worth stating: the Duelist cannot use `Dual Strike`, `Two-Weapon Fighting`, or any two-handed weapon's 1.5× Strength damage — `ATTACKS-01 §12.5`.** **The class gives up the two largest melee damage multipliers in the game to hold one blade properly.**

# 3 — Pirate

**Owner: a Smuggler who fights. Solo raider. Skilled pilot and dogfighter. Fights dirty, steals, sometimes kills. **Standard base class**.**

## 3.1 The archetype, looked up

**⚠ Two of the three most famous Star Wars pirates are inadmissible under this project's own canon rules.**

**`Hondo Ohnaka` is *The Clone Wars*, ruled Disney canon and excluded. `Andronikos Revel` is SWTOR, excluded as events that did not occur.**

**What survives is Legends and it is the better model anyway:**

**`Nym`** — a Feeorin pirate captain, an exceptional pilot flying a heavily customised ship, who raided the wealthy and the corrupt and avoided killing the innocent.
**`Xim the Despot`** — an Old Republic pirate warlord who ruled an empire before the Hutts broke it.

**And Wookieepedia's Legends article gives the distinction that is worth stealing:**

> **Piracy is *"hunting, hostage taking, and theft of cargo."*** **Morals varied — some were cutthroats, others *"restricted their actions to the rich."*** **A pirate sanctioned by a government is a **privateer**, and privateers *"held themselves to a more civilised standard."***

**⚠ That maps onto `ALIGNMENT-01` for free and needs no new mechanism.** **The same class plays as a privateer or a cutthroat and the alignment system already records which.**

## 3.3 ⚠ Measured against the Smuggler before proposing it

**`FINDINGS-24 §1` is why. The Agent's premise failed this test and had to be rebuilt.**

    Smuggler 11   Alertness Appraise Awareness Slicing Demolitions Persuade
                  Pilot Security Sleight-of-Hand Stealth Streetwise
    Pirate 8      Pilot Appraise Athletics Alertness Awareness Intimidate
                  Streetwise Scavenging

    shared 5      Pilot · Appraise · Alertness · Awareness · Streetwise
    Pirate only 3 Athletics · Intimidate · Scavenging
    Smuggler only 6

    shared / smaller list   5/8 = 63%

**Below the Agent's 78% and well below `PT-83`'s 86% Scout/Soldier pair, which was kept.**

**And the split is not only the list.** **The Smuggler is `Specialist` with 18 attack picks; the Pirate is `Middle` with 27.** **The Smuggler has `Stealth`, `Security`, `Slicing` and `Sleight of Hand` and the Pirate has none of them — it does not sneak, pick locks, slice or palm.**

> **The Smuggler gets past you. The Pirate goes through you.**

## 3.4 Feature — `Plunder`

**Owner: *fights dirty, steals, sometimes kills.* The theft is the part nothing implements.**

**`SKILL-RESOLUTION-01` already permits it between NPCs — *"a bounty hunter lifts a datapad off a rival"* — and `ATTACKS-05`'s disarm chain lets you take a weapon.** **What no class can do is profit from a body mid-fight.**

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Plunder`** | 1 | **When you reduce an enemy to 0 wounds or below, take one carried item as a free action** — a weapon, medpac, grenade, spike or mine. **It is immediately usable.** |
| › **`Quick Hands`** | 4 | **Also on a critical hit, without dropping them.** |
| ›› **`Everything Is Cargo`** | 8 | As above, and **once per encounter the item you took costs no Gear action to use.** |

**Why it is worth something.** **`SKILL-RESOLUTION-01 §5` puts six skills in resource mode and tracks consumables — spikes, medpacs, mines, repair parts.** **In a system that counts them, taking them off the dead is a real economy and no other class touches it.**

**Not dominant:** **it does nothing against an enemy carrying nothing, nothing against most droids, and it requires the kill first.** **It gives no bonus to attack, damage or defence at any tier.**

**The moment:** **he shoots the trooper, takes his grenade, and throws it at the next one.**

## 3.5 ⚠ The piloting half has no system to attach to

**Owner named *skilled pilot and dogfighter* first, and I cannot build on it.**

**`Pilot` is a class skill and that is all the corpus can currently support — there are no ship rules, no vehicle combat, and no dogfighting mechanic anywhere in `docs/`.**

> **⚠ Second class in the roster whose identity is half-blocked on a document that does not exist.** **The Machinist's upgrade chain waits on `EQUIPMENT-01` and the crafting port; the Pirate's dogfighting waits on ship rules that have never been started.**

**Both classes are complete and playable without them. Both are missing the half a player would name first.**

# 4 — Plain-language lines, revised

**Explorer** — the party's scholar and the reason a non-Jedi can be one. *When the party gets stuck on something old, he is why they stop being stuck.*

**Duelist** — one blade, one hand, nothing in the other. *Picks an opponent and gets much better against them — and no worse against anyone else than he was.*

**Pirate** — a smuggler who fights, and a thief where a smuggler is not. Flies well, hits hard, keeps nothing he did not take. *Only one who can take an enemy's gear off them mid-fight and use it in the same breath.*
