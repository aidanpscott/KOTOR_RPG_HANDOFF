# FINDINGS-29 — Operative, Shadow Hunter, Commando, and the three that were unblocked

**Owner direction on the first three. `§1` explains why the ruling answers my `FINDINGS-28 §1` objection rather than overruling it.**

---

# 1 — ⚠ Ranged-against-melee works, and my objection was aimed at the wrong thing

**`FINDINGS-28 §1` argued that *ranged covert* and *melee covert* is not a class distinction, because `ATTACKS-01 §4` gives every organic both rosters.** **That is true of the *weapons* and I stopped there.**

**It is not true of the *stealth*, and that is the whole class.**

**`ATTACKS-01 §13` on the `Sneak Attack` rider:** *"Attacking reveals you, so it fires once per approach unless you hide again."*

> **⚠ At range, revealing yourself costs you nothing immediately — the enemy knows someone shot but not from where, and you are not adjacent to anything.**
> **In melee, revealing yourself puts you next to what you just stabbed.**

**Two different problems:**

    ranged covert   the shot gives away a position you can leave
    melee covert    the kill leaves you standing in the open, in reach

**So the split is real and it is not about the weapon.** **It is about what concealment costs once it breaks, and that differs by range in a way no amount of buying chains can equalise.**

**⚠ The owner's ruling stands and my objection was correct about the surface and wrong about the mechanism.** **Recorded because `FINDINGS-28 §1` is on file recommending a different split and should not be read as still live.**

## 2.1 Feature — `No Firing Position`

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`No Firing Position`** | 1 | **When you attack from concealment at more than 10 metres, you are revealed only to enemies who succeed on an `Awareness` check against your `Stealth` result.** Everyone else does not know where the shot came from. |
| › **`Ghost Fire`** | 4 | As above, and **enemies who fail may not move toward you** that round — they do not know where to go. |
| ›› **`Never Seen`** | 8 | As above, and **a failed check leaves you Hidden from that enemy entirely**, so the `Sneak Attack` rider fires again on your next attack against them. |

**⚠ The capstone is the point.** **`ATTACKS-01 §13` says stealth damage *"fires once per approach unless you hide again."*** **The Sith Assassin's `Vanish` buys the re-hide with a check after a kill. The Operative never loses concealment against anyone who did not spot the shot.**

**Not dominant:** **it requires range — inside 10 metres it does nothing at all.** **And it is a contested check per enemy, so a squad with good `Awareness` finds you on the first volley.**

**⚠ Two parents, deliberately.** **The Operative continues the Agent alone because it is institutional work. A melee killer comes from either the institution or the underworld, and `PT-138`'s grammar permits *a class level* as a holding without saying it must be one class.**

## 3.1 Feature — `Nobody Saw Him Leave`

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Nobody Saw Him Leave`** | 1 | **After a melee attack of yours reduces an enemy to 0 wounds or below, you may move up to half your speed as a free action.** |
| › **`Between Breaths`** | 4 | **Full speed**, and the movement provokes no opportunity attacks. |
| ›› **`He Was Never Here`** | 8 | As above, and **at the end of that movement make a `Stealth` check as a free action** against each enemy's `Awareness` or `Alertness`. Against those you beat, you are Hidden. |

**The mirror of `§2.1` and the reason the two classes are not one.** **The Operative's problem is that shooting reveals a position; the Shadow Hunter's is that killing leaves him standing next to the body.** **One solves it by not being locatable, the other by not being there.**

**⚠ Overlap with the Sith Assassin's `Vanish` is real and bounded.** **`Vanish` re-hides after a `Sneak Attack` and costs a check; this moves first and only checks at tier 3.** **A Sith Assassin disappears where he stood. A Shadow Hunter is somewhere else before he disappears.**

**Not dominant:** **both require the kill.** **Against anything that survives the hit, neither tier does anything.**

# 4 — Commando

**Owner: *"a more advanced version of the Soldier — basically the Weapon Master prestige class from D&D 3, whereby a Commando chooses a weapon specialisation and is now an expert in that field."***

## 4.1 ⚠ The hook already exists and it is currently Jedi-only

**Derived. `FEATS-LIBRARY-01`, under **Any prestige class**:**

    Superior Weapon Focus: Lightsaber    +1 attack, above the Specialization tier
    › Advanced                           +2
    ›› Master                            +3

**And the game has six weapon families with a full `Proficiency → Focus → Specialization` ladder:** Blaster · Blaster Rifle · Melee Weapons · Lightsaber · Assault Cannons · Wrist-Mounted.

> **⚠ A superior tier exists above Specialization for exactly one of the six, and it is the Jedi weapon.** **A prestige Jedi can reach `+3` attack beyond Specialization with a lightsaber. A prestige Soldier cannot reach it with anything.**

**The Commando is the class that closes that, and the mechanism is already written — it only needs to stop being lightsaber-only.**

## 4.2 The record

| **Hit die** | **d10** | |
| **Primary** | **Strength** | |
| **Saves** | **12 / 6 / 6** — Fort Strong · Reflex Weak · Will Weak | `PT-123`, one job. The Soldier's profile |

**⚠ That requirement is the class declaring itself.** **You cannot enter the Commando in the abstract — you enter it holding the top of a weapon ladder, and the weapon you hold is the one you are now a Commando of.**

## 4.3 Feature — `Chosen Weapon`

**Declared at entry. One of the six families, permanent, never changed.**

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Chosen Weapon`** | 1 | **+1 attack with your chosen family, above the `Specialization` tier.** |
| › **`Advanced Chosen Weapon`** | 4 | **+2 attack, and +1 damage.** |
| ›› **`Master Chosen Weapon`** | 8 | **+3 attack, +2 damage**, and **your threat range with that family widens by one**. |

**⚠ This is `Superior Weapon Focus: Lightsaber` generalised to all six families and given a class to live in.** **Same numbers on the attack axis, plus a damage rider and a threat step that the lightsaber version does not have — because a Commando has nothing else, where a Weaponmaster also has `Deflect` and `Inner Strength`.**

**Priced.** **`+3` attack and `+2` damage on one weapon family, for three of eighteen feats.** **`Weapon Focus` is `+1` attack and `Weapon Specialization` `+2` damage, each for one feat, so the chain is roughly three feats' worth of bonus for three feats — and it is family-locked where those are only family-limited.**

**⚠ The threat step at tier 3 is the part to watch.** **`ATTACKS-01 §12.1` defines threat multipliers, and a vibrosword at 19–20 widened by one becomes 18–20 before any Precision chain applies.** **It stacks with `Deathstroke` and should be checked against that chain rather than assumed independent.**

**⚠ And `Superior Weapon Focus: Lightsaber` should probably be retired into this.** **Two chains doing the same thing, one available to any prestige class and one to a class, is the collision the project has renamed for twice.** **Flagged, not done — it is a feat-library decision.**

# 5 — The three that were unblocked

## 5.1 Beast Master

**Anchored by a written ruling. `SKILLS-01 §3`:** *"`Beast Handling` gives non-Force characters parity with a Force option. `Beast Trick`, `Beast Confusion` and `Dominate Beast` already do this job for Jedi."*

    Middle · d8 · Wisdom · 15 feats · saves 12/6/12 · skill base 4 · 13 chains, 9 capstones
    Skills 6   Beast Handling · Botany · Scavenging · Athletics · Alertness · Awareness

> **Entry: character level 10, **Scout 6 or Explorer 6**, and `Beast Handling` 8 ranks.**

**Feature — `Bonded`.** **T1: one beast of your level or lower accompanies you and acts on your initiative. T2: it gains your `Beast Handling` bonus to its attack rolls and saves. T3: a second beast, or the first returns at full vitality after each encounter.**

**⚠ Same open question as `Field Override` and `Cover Identity` — whether a controlled creature's declaration is its own or its controller's. Fourth instance. It has been open since `REPLY-08`.**

## 5.2 Jedi Sage

    Specialist · d6 · Force die 10 · Wisdom · 11 feats · saves 12/9/12 · skill base 4 · 13 chains, 4 capstones
    Skills 6   Archaeology · Xenology · Mysticism · Science · Alertness · Awareness

> **Entry: character level 10, **Jedi Consular 6**, and `Mysticism` 8 ranks.**

**Feature — `The Long View`.** **T1: once per encounter, after seeing a d20 result on any check made by an ally within 20 metres, add your Wisdom modifier to it. T2: twice. T3: twice, and one may be applied after the outcome is known but before the next character acts.**

**⚠ The Jedi Master teaches and the Sage advises, and both come from the Consular.** **`Light Side Enlightenment` shifts companions' alignment over a campaign; this changes one roll in a fight. Different scales, same parent, no overlap.**

## 5.3 Sith Sorcerer

    Specialist · d6 · Force die 10 · Wisdom · 11 feats · saves 12/9/12 · skill base 4 · 13 chains, 4 capstones
    Skills 6   Archaeology · Xenology · Mysticism · Intimidate · Alertness · Awareness

> **Entry: character level 10, **Sith Inquisitor 6**, and `Mysticism` 8 ranks.**

**Feature — `Consume`.** **T1: when an enemy within 20 metres is reduced to 0 wounds or below by any source, regain Force points equal to your Wisdom modifier. T2: twice that. T3: twice, and the working maximum lost to degradation is restored at the same rate.**

**⚠ The Sage's mirror under `PT-129` — conditional against unconditional.** **The Sage spends a reaction on someone else's roll; the Sorcerer gains a resource automatically whenever anything dies.** **And tier 3 is the only thing in the corpus that reverses `FORCE-POOL-01-v3 §4`'s ceiling degradation, which is why it is the capstone and not tier 1.**

# 6 — Plain-language lines

**Operative** — the shot came from somewhere and nobody knows where.
**Shadow Hunter** — he is not standing where the body is.
**Commando** — one weapon, chosen once, better with it than anyone alive.
**Beast Master** — the answer to a Jedi's `Dominate Beast`, for people who cannot use the Force.
**Jedi Sage** — the one who tells you the thing that makes the roll go your way.
**Sith Sorcerer** — every death on the field puts something back in his hands.
