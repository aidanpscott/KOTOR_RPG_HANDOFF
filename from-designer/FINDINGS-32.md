# FINDINGS-32 — Form acquisition, and two corrections

**⚠ `§1` is a flag on the `Superior Two-Weapon Fighting` ruling before it is implemented. `§3` is the forms work, which `ATTACKS-06 §1` assigned to this workstream and nobody had started.**

---

# 1 — ⚠ Restricting who may take `Superior Two-Weapon Fighting` does not resolve the contradiction

**Owner: *"we keep superior two weapon fighting and only 1 or 2 classes can use it."***

**Restriction fixes *availability*. The problem is *the numbers*.**

    ACTION-ECONOMY-01 §7.2   "Never zero — the principle every attack chain follows."
    Superior TWF, tier 2      0 main / −2 off
    Superior TWF, tier 3     +2 main / −1 off, with a balanced off-hand

> **⚠ A Gunslinger holding it still reaches zero, and then a positive modifier. The rule is still broken; it is broken by one class instead of by all of them.**

**Three ways to close it, and they are genuinely different decisions:**

**Amend the principle.** *"The dual-wield penalty never reaches zero **except through a class-restricted chain**."* **⚠ Cheapest, and it means a Gunslinger with two pistols is strictly better off than with one — which is the `Duelist`'s entire premise stated in reverse.**

**Keep the principle and re-floor the chain at `−1`.** **⚠ Then it does nothing: `Master Two-Weapon Fighting` already reaches `−1` and any character may buy it.**

**Keep the principle and change what the chain does.** **It stops being about the penalty and becomes about the off hand** — *the off-hand attack ignores the penalty, the main hand still takes it.* **⚠ Under `ACTION-ECONOMY-01 §7.1` a second weapon adds exactly one attack, so this improves one attack per round and never goes positive.** **I would take this one.**

## 1.1 The two classes

**Proposed: `Gunslinger` and `Shadow Hunter`.**

**One per range band, which mirrors how `Chosen Weapon` covers all six weapon families on the Commando.** **The Gunslinger is two pistols by owner ruling; the Shadow Hunter is the only melee class in the roster whose identity is precision rather than weight.**

**⚠ And it stacks on the Gunslinger with `Both Barrels`, which is a lot on one class.** **`Both Barrels` is targeting and `Superior` is accuracy — different axes, but a Gunslinger holding both has near-penalty-free fire distributed across four targets. Worth watching in play rather than pre-solving.**

# 3 — Form acquisition

**`ATTACKS-06 §1`:** *"Which classes grant which forms, on what schedule, and what training in play looks like, belongs to the class workstream."* **Three questions. `§3.1` to `§3.3`.**

**⚠ `FORMS-01` is not in holdings.** **`ATTACKS-06` describes it as holding the seven forms as feats in one exclusion group. Everything below is built on `ATTACKS-06`'s own descriptions and should be checked against `FORMS-01` before adoption.**

## 3.1 Which forms — matched on what each one does

**Every Force base class is granted **Form I, Determination** at 1st level.**

**Derived, `ATTACKS-06`:** *"The form every initiate learns and the one to fall back on when nothing else will do."* **That is a grant rule stated as flavour.**

**And one further form at 5th level, by class:**

| Class | Form | Why, from the form's own text |
|---|---|---|
| **Jedi Guardian** | **V — Perseverance** | *"returning blaster bolts to their origin"* · **the counter.** The class that stands in front and sends it back |
| **Jedi Sentinel** | **III — Resilience** | *"defensive coverage… a true master was considered invincible"* · **outlast.** The class whose case is surviving the exchange |
| **Jedi Consular** | **VI — Moderation** | *"extensive integration of Force powers"* · **blade and Force.** ⚠ `PREGENS-01` already has Meris holding it |
| **Sith Warrior** | **VII — Ferocity** | *"the most vicious form… focus on facing a single opponent"* · **runs on emotional intensity.** ⚠ `PREGENS-01`'s Dark Jedi already holds it |
| **Sith Inquisitor** | **VI — Moderation** | the Consular's mirror, and the same reason. **Forms are not exclusive between classes** — the exclusion group is per character |
| **Sith Assassin** | **II — Contention** | *"precision swordplay… heavy focus on protecting one's weapon to avoid being disarmed"* · **the duel.** Carries **Penetration**, the ninth axis |

**⚠ Form IV, Aggression, is granted to no class and that is deliberate.**

**`ATTACKS-06`:** *"the most acrobatic and fastest form… extremely exhausting,"* **and `ATTACKS-01 §7.1` records that its `Saber Swarm` chain costs two Force points per use.**

> **It is the one form that must be taught. A class cannot train you into something that expensive; someone has to show you.**

## 3.2 On what schedule

    1st level    Form I, Determination        every Force base class, granted
    5th level    one further form             by class, granted, as above
    thereafter   any form                     learned only — §3.3

**Two granted forms and no more. Everything else is play.**

**⚠ Why 5th and not 1st.** **`ATTACKS-06 §2`:** *"Switching forms is the cost of switching attacks, which makes the form choice a decision every encounter rather than a one-time pick."* **A character with one form has no decision to make. The second grant is where the mechanic starts working, and four levels is long enough that the first form is genuinely the one you learned to fight with.**

## 3.3 What training looks like

**`ATTACKS-06 §1` names the three routes and they are the whole rule:** *"a master, a holocron, an enemy who teaches by defeating you."*

> **A form learned after 5th level costs one feat, and requires one of those three events. The feat is the price; the event is the permission.**

**⚠ Neither alone is sufficient.** **A character who finds a holocron and has no feat to spend does not learn the form. A character with a spare feat and no teacher does not either.**

**And there is precedent for exactly this shape.** **`ATTACKS-01 §14` on the Echani chain:** *"the only thing in the corpus a GM grants on a narrative event rather than a level."*

**Forms are the second, and they are a better fit than the Echani chain was** — **being taught a fighting style by the person who just beat you with it is the most characteristic thing that happens in this setting.**

## 3.4 What this settles and what it does not

**Settled: every Force character now has a form at 1st level and a second at 5th, and a stated route to more.** **Six base classes that were complete except for this are now complete.**

**⚠ Not settled — the four Force prestige classes with a martial identity.** **`Jedi Weaponmaster`, `Sith Marauder`, `Jedi Watchman` and `Sith Battlemaster` are drafted and none states whether entering grants a form.**

**I would say no.** **`CLASS-ATTACKS-01 §5` as scoped by `PT-141` says a prestige class grants no attack credits, and a form is closer to a credit than to a feat chain — it is access to entries rather than a bonus.** **A Battlemaster is *already* the class about having more forms; granting one on entry would pay it twice.**

**⚠ And that makes the Battlemaster finishable.** **`FINDINGS-30 §4.3` flagged it as drafted on top of a system that did not exist. The system now exists, and `Master of Forms` reads against it cleanly: tier 2 holds two of the forms you have learned, and learning them is `§3.3`.**

# 4 — Plain-language lines

**Form acquisition** — every Force user learns the basic form at first level and one more suited to their class at fifth. Everything after that has to be taught to you by a master, a holocron, or somebody who beats you with it.
