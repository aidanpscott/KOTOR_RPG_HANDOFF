# FINDINGS-34 — ⚠ The Battlemaster is drafted at `Middle`, not `Combat`

**Owner, in conversation:** *"sith marauder is the middle one while sith battlemaster is combat and sith lord is specialist."*

**`FINDINGS-30 §4.2` drafted the Battlemaster at **`Middle`**, 16 feats — the same rate as the Marauder.** **One of us has moved and it should be settled before the class is adopted.**

---

# 1 — ⚠ No Force class is `Combat` in the source. Not one.

**Derived, `k2_featgain.2da`, cumulated to 30. `Combat` needs 18.**

    Guardian      16      Weaponmaster  16      Marauder      16
    Sentinel      15      Watchman      15      Sith Lord     11
    Consular      11      Jedi Master   11      sas row       10

> **⚠ The highest Force column in either game is 16. Every Force class the source describes is `Middle` or `Specialist`.**

**The `Jedi Guardian` and `Sith Warrior` sit at `Combat` only because we put them there** — **20 feats, authored by `PT-84` and mirrored by `PT-125`. The source gives `jgd` 16.**

**So `Combat` is not a rate the Force side has. It is a rate we granted twice.**

---

# 2 — Which makes the owner's reading a better design than my draft

**I gave the Battlemaster 16 because it continues the Warrior at the same tier as the Marauder does — `PT-124`'s `+2` Force die rule applied to acquisition as well.**

**⚠ That produces two Warrior continuations identical on rate, feats, hit die, Force die, saves and skill base.** **They separate on the class chain alone: `Ignore Pain` and `Increase Combat Damage` against `Master of Forms`.**

**One axis of difference between two prestige classes from the same parent is thin, and it is the thing this project has had to fix three times.**

**At `Combat` they separate on two:**

| | Sith Marauder | **Sith Battlemaster** |
|---|---|---|
| **Rate** | Middle — 27 picks | **Combat — 36 picks** |
| **Feats at 30** | 16 | **18** |
| **`T`** | 31 | **40** |
| **Chains** | 13 → 9 capstones | **14 → 13 capstones** |
| **Identity** | endures and hits harder | **knows every way to fight** |

> **The Marauder outlasts you. The Battlemaster out-fights you.** **That reads, and 16-against-16 did not.**

**⚠ And it is coherent with `Master of Forms`.** **A class whose feature is holding more forms wants the picks to buy entries in all of them. At `Middle` it holds twelve accessible entries on 27 picks; at `Combat` it holds twelve on 36.**

## 2.1 What it costs

**A third authored departure on the Force side.** **`PT-84` authored the Guardian to 20, `PT-125` mirrored the Warrior at 20, and this would author the Battlemaster to 18.**

> **⚠ Three of the fourteen Force classes would be at a rate the source gives none of them.**

**And it makes the Battlemaster the only `Combat`-rate prestige class in the game apart from the `Commando`.**

**Neither is a reason not to do it. Both are reasons the ruling should say it is authored.**

## 2.2 The curve, if it goes to 18

**⚠ Not an endpoint. `FINDINGS-01 §1` found two authored totals with no schedule behind them and I am not adding a third.**

**Reuse the Marksman's rebuilt curve, as the `Brawler` does** — **1 / 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 / 15 / 18 at levels 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30.** **The thirty rows already exist and cannot drift.**
