## 1.1 The curve — reused, not authored

**1 / 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 / 15 / 18 at levels 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30.** **The Marksman's rebuilt column, already used by the `Brawler`.**

> **⚠ Thirty rows that already exist and have already been checked. `FINDINGS-01 §1` found two authored totals with no schedule behind them and this is the third class built to avoid becoming a fourth.**

## 1.2 Chain count — 18, and it is the wide end of `Combat` on purpose

    Commando            14 chains   13 capstones     the deep Combat prestige
    Sith Battlemaster   18          11               the wide one
    Jedi Guardian       18          11               its Jedi-side analogue in rate

**⚠ The feature is *access*, so the chain count should be too.** **`Master of Forms` tier 2 holds two forms at once — four lightsaber chains instead of two, so twelve accessible lightsaber entries instead of six.** **A class that doubles its own access and then enters only fourteen trees has bought something it cannot use.**

**And the rate change is what makes the feature work.** **At `Middle`'s 27 picks, holding two forms was a wider door with no budget to walk through it. At 36 the Battlemaster can actually buy into both.**

# 2 — ⚠ This is the first prestige entry to cross a rate boundary, and the rule for it was written as a hypothetical

**`CLASS-ATTACKS-01 §5`:**

> *"Where entry crosses a rate boundary, the new rate applies from the level of entry and picks already spent are not recalculated. **No prestige entry in the current list crosses a boundary. If one ever does, this is the rule.**"*

**One now does. Two, in fact — and it is `PT-143`'s open Sith entry that creates them.**

**Any Sith base class may enter any Sith prestige class. So:**

    Sith Inquisitor  Specialist  ->  Battlemaster  Combat    two boundaries
    Sith Assassin    Specialist  ->  Battlemaster  Combat    two boundaries

**Derived, against `CLASS-ATTACKS-01 §3`'s schedules:**

    pure Sith Inquisitor to 30                        18 picks
    Inquisitor 15 / Battlemaster 15                   27      +9
    Inquisitor 12 / Battlemaster 18                   28      +10
    Inquisitor 10 / Battlemaster 20                   30      +12

    pure Sith Warrior to 30                           36
    Warrior 10 / Battlemaster 20                      36      +0

> **⚠ An Inquisitor who prestiges into the Battlemaster at the earliest legal level gains **twelve** attack picks — four whole chains — over staying pure.**
> **A Warrior who does the same gains nothing, because no boundary is crossed.**

## 2.1 How this compares to the one we already accepted

**`FINDINGS-26 §2` found that prestige entry beats staying pure on *feats*, by two out of sixteen — 12.5%.** **`PT-142` accepted it and set entry at 10 rather than 15 so the optimum was chosen rather than handed over.**

**This is 18 picks becoming 30. That is 67%.**

**⚠ Five times the size of the distortion the project already decided was small enough to live with.**

## 2.2 Three ways to close it

**Accept it, as `PT-142` accepted the feat version.** **⚠ It is not the same size and I would not. A Specialist Sith would be strictly better off entering the Battlemaster than staying in their own class, whatever they intended to play.**

**Gate the Battlemaster on rate rather than on order** — *entry requires a `Combat`-rate Sith class, which is the Warrior.* **⚠ Clean, and it reverses `PT-143` for one class. The steering the owner wanted is preserved by the `Weapon Focus: Lightsaber` holding; this makes it a wall instead.**

**Read picks at the *lower* of the two rates for levels after a boundary crossing.** **⚠ General, applies to any future case, and it is a real rules change to `CLASS-ATTACKS-01 §5`.** **It also punishes the honest multiclass, which `MULTICLASS-01 §5` spent four deleted systems trying to avoid.**

**I would take the second.** **It is one line, it affects one class, and it does not touch the rate machinery — which is the thing `MULTICLASS-01` warns hardest about changing.**

# 3 — What does not change

**`Master of Forms` is unaltered.** **The rate change makes it better without repricing it, because more picks means more of the twelve accessible entries are reachable.**

**⚠ And the Marauder is untouched at `Middle`.** **The pair now differs on rate, feats, picks, `T`, chains and capstones — six axes, where `FINDINGS-34 §2` found one.** **That was the point of the ruling.**
