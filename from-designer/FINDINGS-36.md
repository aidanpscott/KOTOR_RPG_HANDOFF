# FINDINGS-36 — the rate floor, and the Weaponmaster at `Combat`

**Both owner-ruled. `§3` is the consequence that was accepted deliberately and should not read as an oversight later.**

---

# 1 — The rate floor

> **A prestige class never lowers your attack-pick rate.** **From the level of entry you accrue at the higher of your current rate and the prestige class's own.**

**⚠ Scoped to prestige entry only.** **`MULTICLASS-01 §3.1` governs ordinary multiclassing and rejected take-higher by name — *"neither summing nor taking the higher rate."*** **That stands. This is an exception for prestige and `PT-58` is the warrant:**

> *"A Weaponmaster was never something else — prestige entry continues a path rather than starting one."* **Credit is for the leap. A prestige class is not a leap.**

**And the flavour agrees: you do not get worse at fighting by learning something new.**

## 1.1 ⚠ One case the wording does not cover

**"Your current rate" is unambiguous for a single-class character and undefined for a multi-base one.**

**A Soldier 5 / Smuggler 5 entering a prestige class holds `Combat` and `Specialist` at once.**

**Proposed: the highest rate among the classes you hold.** **It is a floor rule, so the highest is the only reading consistent with what the rule is for. ⚠ Stated because it will otherwise be decided at a table.**

**Curve: 1 / 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 / 15 / 18 at levels 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30** — **the Marksman's existing column, already used by the `Brawler` and the `Battlemaster`.** **⚠ Not an endpoint. Thirty rows that already exist.**

## 2.1 Chain count — 15, and it completes the `Combat` spread

    Commando              14 chains   13 capstones    one weapon, taken furthest
    Jedi Weaponmaster     15          12              one weapon and a Force reserve
    Sith Battlemaster     18          11              every way of fighting

**The Weaponmaster narrows and deepens relative to the Guardian's 18 and 11 — which is what *"kept going with one weapon"* means in picks.**

## 2.2 ⚠ On the mix-up

**Both the Weaponmaster and the Watchman are `Middle` in the source** — `jwm` 16, `jwa` 15, and `Middle` is 15–16. **Both were read off `k2_featgain.2da` and reported as found. Neither was a choice.**

**What the source does not have is a rate ladder across the Jedi prestige line. Raising the Weaponmaster creates one, and it makes the two orders mirror exactly:**

    Jedi    Weaponmaster Combat · Watchman Middle · Jedi Master Specialist · Sage Specialist
    Sith    Battlemaster Combat · Marauder Middle · Sith Lord   Specialist · Sorcerer Specialist

**⚠ Four authored Force departures now: Guardian 20, Sith Warrior 20, Battlemaster 18, Weaponmaster 18.** **The source has no Force class above 16. The record should say so in one place rather than four.**

# 3 — What the two fixes do, and the part accepted deliberately

**Every downward penalty is gone:**

    Guardian -> Weaponmaster   +0        Guardian -> Jedi Master   +0
    Guardian -> Watchman       +0        Sentinel -> Watchman      +0
    Warrior  -> Battlemaster   +0        Consular -> Jedi Master   +0

**⚠ And the upward gains doubled rather than shrank:**

    Consular   -> Weaponmaster    was +6   now +12
    Inquisitor -> Battlemaster        +12      +12

**Raising the Weaponmaster means a `Specialist` Force user entering it crosses two rate bands instead of one.**

> **⚠ Owner-accepted, explicitly, and it is not an oversight.** **The trade was priced in `FINDINGS-35`'s follow-up: a Consular who takes this path gives up **seven Force powers** — from 41 to 34, and the Consular's is the only accelerated power column in the game — and a sixth of their Force pool.**

**So the optimal attack-pick build for any `Specialist` Force class is to enter the `Weaponmaster` or the `Battlemaster` at level 10.** **It costs the thing that class was built on. That is a trade, not an exploit, and it is the same shape the project accepted for feats in `PT-142`.**

# 4 — Still open, and now more pressing

**⚠ Which chain count applies to a multiclass character?**

**A Consular 10 / Weaponmaster 20 holds two: 13 from the Consular and 15 from the Weaponmaster.** **Nothing states which governs, and `CLASS-ATTACKS-01 §2.3` assigns the number per class as though a character only ever holds one.**

**It was open before these rulings and it is now the only thing between here and a complete prestige tier.** **Three readings — the highest, the most recent, or the one you hold most levels in — and they differ by up to five trees.**
