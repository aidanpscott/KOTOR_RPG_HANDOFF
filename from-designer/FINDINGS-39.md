# 1 — The Agent

**`REPLY-29`: take the spy, drop the impersonation. `Charisma` → `Intelligence`, cover and gadgets, `Cover Identity` withdrawn.**

## 1.1 ⚠ The problem the rebuild created, and where the new axis came from

**`PT-135` put the class on `Charisma` because `Smuggler` and `Jedi Sentinel` each hold all four covert skills and `Charisma` was the only thing separating it.** **Moving to `Intelligence` removed that and put nothing back.**

**⚠ So the class needed a third axis that is neither stealth nor charisma. The corpus has one and nothing uses it.**

**`PT-3`:** *"Standing in an `o` square grants +2 Defence and +1 Reflex regardless of firing angle."* **And `EQUIPMENT-01`'s three-quarters and total cover are positional too — *"a `#` square is total cover and cannot be shot through or into."***

> **⚠ Cover is a fully specified positional system and exactly one thing in the game references it: `Run to Ground`, which lets a Bounty Hunter ignore it.** **Nothing *uses* it.**

**Which is the same shape as `Quarry`, `Field Override`, `Jury Rig` and `Cover Identity` before it — a rule the corpus already has and no class can operate.**

**And it separates the three cleanly:**

    Smuggler        not seen                  stealth
    Jedi Sentinel   not seen, plus the Force  stealth
    Agent           seen, and behind something cover

**⚠ `REPLY-29`'s own SWTOR reference is *"cover as a positional resource with abilities that only work from it."* That is `PT-3` almost word for word, and `PT-3` predates it.**

## 1.4 Feature — `Field Position`

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Field Position`** | 1 | **While you are in cover, your attacks gain `+2`** — the same bonus the cover gives your Defence. |
| › **`Shift`** | 4 | **Once per round you may move from one cover square to another within your speed as a free action**, provoking nothing. |
| ›› **`Never In The Open`** | 8 | **Once per encounter, when targeted by an attack you can see, move to any cover square within your speed before it resolves.** |

**Cover as offence, then as mobility, then as escape. All three read `PT-3`'s existing positional rule and add no new geometry.**

**⚠ Not dominant, and the constraint is unusually hard: it does nothing in the open.** **Cover is terrain, so the class's whole feature is a property of the encounter rather than of the character — which is the sharpest *"would a player actually choose it"* test in the set, and the answer is that they choose their position instead.**

**⚠ And it does not overlap the `Operative`, which still lists `Agent 6` as its entry.** **The Operative's `No Firing Position` is about concealment — nobody knows where the shot came from. The Agent's is about terrain — they know exactly where you are and cannot reach you.**
