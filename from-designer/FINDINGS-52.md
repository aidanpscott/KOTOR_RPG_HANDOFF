# FINDINGS-52 — `Master of Forms`, rebuilt for forms-as-feats

**Owner: acquisition confirmed at Determination / class form / player's choice. And `Master of Forms` becomes a `Jedi Weaponmaster` feat as well as a `Sith Battlemaster` one.**

---

# 1 — Why the old version cannot survive

**`Master of Forms` as adopted was *hold two forms at once, then switch freely*.** **Under `PT-184` every Force base class holds **three** by level 12, permanently.**

> **⚠ Holding two is now below average, and switching costs nothing because nothing is exclusive.**

**Both clauses are dead. The chain needs a new payload, not a reprice.**

---

# 2 — What is actually scarce now

**Derived. A form unlocks 2 chains of 3 entries.**

    3 forms — every Force class by level 12       6 chains   18 entries
    6 forms — three more from a class feature    12 chains   36 entries
    7 forms — all of them                        14 chains   42 entries

**⚠ Access is no longer the constraint. Feats are.**

**Seven forms cost four feats beyond the three free ones, out of eighteen for a `Combat` class — 22% of a career to hold the roster.**

> **So the feature should buy **forms**, which are now feats, rather than buying permission, which is now free.**

---

# 3 — The proposal

**`Master of Forms` · 1 / 4 / 8 · `Jedi Weaponmaster` and `Sith Battlemaster`.**

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Master of Forms`** | 1 | **You gain one lightsaber form of your choice.** |
| › **`Two Ways to Fight`** | 4 | **A second**, and **once per encounter you may declare an attack from a form other than your active stance.** |
| ›› **`Every Way to Fight`** | 8 | **A third**, and **you may declare attacks from any form you hold, at any time.** |

**Three feats spent to gain three forms is net zero on the feat economy** — **what it buys is that a Weaponmaster or Battlemaster reaches six of seven forms while a Guardian reaches three, without either spending more.**

**⚠ And the second clause is written so it degrades safely.** **If `FINDINGS-51 §2.3`'s stance layer is adopted, tiers 2 and 3 are the class's real distinction — everyone else is locked to one stance's attacks. If it is not adopted, the clause is inert and the chain is still three free forms.**

> **One line, `PT-178`: *You learn extra fighting forms and can use all of them at once.***

---

# 4 — ⚠ Sharing it re-opens the problem `FINDINGS-34` closed

**`FINDINGS-34` moved the Battlemaster to `Combat` because it and the Marauder were identical on six axes and separated only by the class chain.**

**Giving `Master of Forms` to the Weaponmaster does the same thing to a different pair:**

    Jedi Weaponmaster   Combat · d10 · Force 6 · STR · 18 feats · 15 chains · Deflect
    Sith Battlemaster   Combat · d10 · Force 6 · STR · 18 feats · 18 chains · —

> **⚠ They would differ on three chains, `Deflect`, and nothing else. The Battlemaster's signature would belong to both.**

## 4.1 The fix uses a distinction the project already has

**`PT-101`: a restricted chain is **granted**, not bought.**

> **The `Sith Battlemaster` is **granted** `Master of Forms` at 1 / 4 / 8. The `Jedi Weaponmaster` may **buy** it.**

**Then:**

    Battlemaster   three forms free, three feats left over for anything else
    Weaponmaster   three forms for three of his eighteen feats

**⚠ The Sith *is* forms. The Jedi *can do* forms. That is the same distinction `PT-129` already draws between the two orders — the Sith gets it unconditionally, the Jedi pays a condition — and it costs no new machinery.**

**And it keeps the Weaponmaster's own identity intact: `Deflect` is still the thing no Sith gets, and now three feats decide whether he is also a form-master.**

# 5 — Acquisition, confirmed and closed

    level  1   Determination        universal
    level  6   the class's own      Guardian Perseverance · Sentinel Resilience ·
                                    Consular Moderation · Warrior Ferocity ·
                                    Inquisitor Moderation · Assassin Contention
    level 12   player's choice      any form not held

**Everything beyond: one feat, plus a master, a holocron, or an enemy who teaches by defeating you.**

**⚠ `Sith Assassin` gets `Contention` at 6 and it is the one worth a second look** — `FORMS-01 §6.1` gives Contention `+3` attack **only against lightsaber wielders**, which is narrow for a class that mostly kills people who are not Jedi. **Flagged, not changed; the assignment came from `Contention` carrying `Penetration` and that still holds.**

# The question

> **⚠ `§4.1` — grant it to the Battlemaster and sell it to the Weaponmaster? Without something like it the two classes differ by three chains and one feat.**

**And `FINDINGS-51 §2.3`'s stance question is still open. `§3`'s tier 2 and 3 are written to work either way, but they are much weaker if there is no stance.**
