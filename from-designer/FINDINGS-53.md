# FINDINGS-53 — `Master of Forms` tier 1 granted, tiers 2 and 3 bought

**Owner: both classes, first tier free at level 1, the rest purchased.**

---

# 1 — Applied

| | Jedi Weaponmaster | Sith Battlemaster |
|---|---|---|
| **`Master of Forms`** tier 1 | **granted at 1** | **granted at 1** |
| › tier 2 · ›› tier 3 | **bought**, 2 feats | **bought**, 2 feats |
| **Forms held at 30** | 3 by level + 3 = **6** | 3 by level + 3 = **6** |
| **Feats left of 18** | **16** | **16** |

**⚠ Cleaner than what I proposed.** **A form-master gets the first one on arriving — which is what entering the class *means* — and the other two are a choice rather than a gift.**

**And it fixes something in my own version I had not noticed: under *granted at 1 / 4 / 8*, a Battlemaster entering at character level 10 would receive tier 1 immediately and tiers 2 and 3 at class levels 4 and 8.** **Bought tiers have no such schedule — they arrive when the player spends, which is the point of buying them.**

---

# 2 — ⚠ But this removes the thing that separated the two classes

**`FINDINGS-52 §4.1` proposed granted-versus-bought precisely because the pair is otherwise thin. With both on the same terms, here is everything that differs:**

    Jedi Weaponmaster    15 chains · 12 capstones · DEFLECT
    Sith Battlemaster    18 chains · 11 capstones · —

**Rate, hit die, Force die, primary ability, feats, saves, skill base, entry holdings and now the form chain are all identical.**

> **⚠ Three chains and one grant. `PT-83` split the tech pair at 89% overlap; this is higher.**

## 2.1 And it is asymmetric in the wrong direction

**`Deflect` is *"the only granted chain in either game that no other class receives"* — `FINDINGS-26 §3.1`. It is a whole defensive system against the galaxy's standard weapon.**

**The Battlemaster's distinction is that it **lacks** it.**

> **A class defined by what it does not have is not defined.** **The Sith should have something, not merely be missing something.**

## 2.2 The cheapest repair — one line, `PT-129`'s own shape

> **`Sith Battlemaster`: **+1 attack while in any lightsaber form.** Granted at 1. No condition, no action, no limit.**

**`PT-129` is the warrant and it is already ruled:** *a Jedi chain is conditional and answers a situation; a Sith chain is unconditional and raises a number.* **`Deflect` is conditional — it answers blaster fire. This answers nothing and adds a number.**

**The pair then reads:**

    Weaponmaster   one weapon, and blaster fire goes back where it came from
    Battlemaster   every form, and he is better in all of them

**⚠ One line, one number, no new machinery, and it makes the difference something the Sith *has*.**

**And it passes `PT-178`: *whichever form he is in, he is better at it.***
