# FINDINGS-54 — no stance layer. `FORMS-01 §6.1` is cut, and the Battlemaster needs a different hook.

**Owner: *"there is no stance switching anymore. The lightsaber forms are feats that unlock lightsaber attack trees."***

---

# 1 — What this deletes

**⚠ `FORMS-01 §6.1` gives all seven lightsaber forms a modifier line. Under this ruling none of it survives.**

    Determination   Attack +1; Defence +3; Defence vs current target −3
    Contention      Attack +3 vs lightsaber wielders; Damage +3; Deflection −5; Saves vs Force +2
    Resilience      Defence vs current target +2; Deflection +4; Threat Range −1
    Aggression      Defence −2; Defence vs current target +5; Deflection −4; Threat Range +1
    Perseverance    Attack +2; Defence vs current target −5; Deflection +2; Crit Multiplier +1
    Moderation      +1 across five stats; casting does not provoke
    Ferocity        Defence −4; Saves vs Force −4; ATTACKS PER ROUND +1; Crit Attack +4

> **⚠ A form is now a feat that unlocks two attack trees and does nothing else.**

**Three things worth knowing about what goes with it:**

**`Ferocity`'s *attacks per round +1* is deleted.** **⚠ Which is a relief — as a permanent feat it would have been the largest single grant in the game, and `ACTION-ECONOMY-01 §421` cut Velocity from five strikes to three specifically to stop that shape existing.**

**`Moderation`'s Force-integration clause goes.** **`FORMS-01` had already flagged it as *"depends on rules not yet written"* and it was the form's only distinctive line.**

**⚠ And `Moderation` was authored rather than ported** — `FORMS-01 §6.1` restored Niman because it is in the 2DA and documented nowhere, and authored `+1` across five stats to mean *"no strengths, no weaknesses."* **With the modifiers cut, the authored content is cut too and Moderation is a name attached to two trees.**

**⚠ That is not an objection. It is `PT-178` working — the version being deleted needed a paragraph and the version replacing it needs one line:**

> **A form is a feat. It unlocks that form's two lightsaber attack trees.**

---

# 2 — ⚠ Which means forms cannot distinguish the Battlemaster

**If a form is only an unlock, and both classes hold `Master of Forms` on identical terms, then form count is identical and form effects do not exist.**

**Everything separating the pair, restated:**

    Jedi Weaponmaster    15 chains · 12 capstones · Deflect
    Sith Battlemaster    18 chains · 11 capstones · —

**The Battlemaster needs a hook that is not forms. My `+1 attack in any form` is withdrawn** — **it was a flat number with no moment, and I have rejected exactly that shape twice in this project, once for the Sith Warrior.**

---

# 3 — The proposal: build on the one stat that is already his

**⚠ The Battlemaster has the widest chain count in the game — 18, against the Weaponmaster's 15 and the Commando's 14.** **`FINDINGS-35 §1.2` set it that way deliberately: *the feature is access, so the chain count should be too.***

**The access argument survives the ruling even though the feature did not. So the feature should reward breadth directly.**

**`Never the Same Twice` — Sith Battlemaster, granted at 1 / 4 / 8.**

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Never the Same Twice`** | 1 | **If the attack you declare is from a different chain than the one you declared last round, `+2` attack.** |
| › **`Unreadable`** | 4 | **`+4`.** |
| ›› **`No Pattern At All`** | 8 | **`+4`**, and the target gains no Defence bonus against you from any Defensive chain it has declared. |

**⚠ It is conditional, which is the Jedi shape rather than the Sith one under `PT-129`.** **I think that is right here and the reason is that `PT-129` describes *base* classes — `FINDINGS-26 §3.5` already recorded that the prestige tier does not follow it, since `Regenerate Force Points` and both alignment chains are unconditional on Jedi prestige classes.**

**Priced.** **`+4` attack every round a Battlemaster rotates chains, against a Weaponmaster's `Deflect` which is a whole defensive system. Roughly even, and it costs him nothing to satisfy — a class with 18 trees has something else to declare.**

**⚠ And it punishes the build that ignores its own chain count.** **A Battlemaster who found one good chain and declares it every round gets nothing, which is the correct incentive for the widest class in the game.**

**`PT-178`:** *He never attacks the same way twice, and you cannot get set for it.*

**The pair then reads:**

    Weaponmaster   one weapon, and blaster fire goes back where it came from
    Battlemaster   eighteen trees, and you never see the same attack twice

# The question

> **⚠ `§3` — `Never the Same Twice`, or something else? The Battlemaster currently has no feature at all.**

**And `§1` — `FORMS-01 §6.1`'s modifier table needs striking by whoever holds that document. It is live content that this ruling deletes.**
