**Sith Battlemaster. Granted at class levels 1, 4 and 8.**

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Never the Same Twice`** | 1 | If the attack you declare comes from a **different chain** than the one you declared last round, **`+2` attack**. |
| › **`Unreadable`** | 4 | **`+4` attack**, same condition. |
| ›› **`No Pattern At All`** | 8 | **`+4` attack**, and the target gains **no Defence bonus from any Defensive chain it has declared** against you. |

**One line:** *He never attacks the same way twice, and you cannot get set for it.*

# 2 — ⚠ The Brawler capstone triples when `Combination` exists, and it should not

**`PT-180` recorded that `Nothing In My Hands` does not multiply, and called it luck. `REPLY-46` is right that it stops being luck the moment an unarmed Velocity chain ships.**

**Priced against Korr — Defence 19, of which 7 is medium battle armour, attacked at `+12` with unarmed `8d4`:**

    no Velocity chain, one strike        14.0 -> 19.0     capstone worth +5.0
    with Combination, three strikes      42.0 -> 57.0     capstone worth +15.0

> **⚠ Three times the value, from a chain in a different document, with no line of either rule mentioning the other.**

## 2.1 Repricing tier 3 does not fix it

**I tried `−6` points instead of *all*. Against Korr it changes nothing** — **`ACTION-ECONOMY-01`'s hit ceiling is 95%, and both readings reach it.** **The multiplication is in the strike count, not the tier value.**

## 2.2 The fix is one word, and it decouples the two permanently

> **`Nothing In My Hands`, tier 3: **the first unarmed attack you make each round** ignores the target's armour bonus to Defence.**

    capstone on all strikes         57.0   (+15.0)
    capstone on one strike a round  47.0   (+5.0)
    today, single strike            19.0   (+5.0)

**⚠ Worth exactly the same whether or not an unarmed Velocity chain exists.** **Which is the property that matters — the Brawler was priced in `FINDINGS-23` against a world with no such chain, and this makes that pricing stay true.**

**And it keeps the moment intact. The plate still does not help; it just does not stop helping three times.**

# 3 — `Combination`, pushed

**`ATTACKS-07`, gated on `Unarmed Specialist IV`.**

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Combination`** | 1 | **Strike twice unarmed.** The second gains `+2` if the first hit. **Attack −3, Defence −2.** |
| › **`Chain Punch`** | 4 | **Three strikes**, each `+2` if the previous hit. **Attack −2, Defence −1.** |
| ›› **`Rain of Blows`** | 8 | **Three strikes**, each `+2` if the previous hit, and **if all three hit the target is `Slowed`** until the end of its next turn. |

**⚠ Deliberately not `Flurry` renamed.** **`Flurry` holds volume flat and buys back accuracy across its tiers. This starts at two strikes and escalates *within* the round — which is what a boxer does and a swordsman does not.**

**`PT-184` defers the damage comparison because weapon upgrades do not exist, so the objection that held this chain is deferred rather than resolved.** **⚠ Re-test when `AGENDA-CURRENT §2.4b` ships. If the gap inverts, `Combination` may be under-tuned rather than over.**

# 4 — Officer

**Owner: *"a natural move for a Soldier from `Combat` to `Middle`. Talking is part of it; leadership is the bigger part."***

    Middle · d10 · Charisma · 16 feats · saves 12/6/12 · skill base 4
    13 chains, 9 capstones · picks 27, T = 31
    Skills 7   Persuade · Intimidate · Alertness · Awareness · Athletics · Streetwise · Pilot

> **Entry: character level 10, **Soldier 6**, and `Persuade` 8 ranks.**

**⚠ `d10` is the Soldier's die kept across a rate drop, which nothing else in the roster does** — **every other prestige class matches its parent's die at its own rate. It is deliberate: an Officer is a Soldier who stopped advancing as a fighter, not one who got softer.**

## 4.1 Feature — `Give the Order`

**⚠ `FINDINGS-28 §4` found this space occupied three times: `Battle Meditation` is a Force power, `Squad Tactics` is Soldier-only, `Spotter` is the Scout's. All three are **bonuses**.**

> **Nothing in the game gives an ally an **action**.**

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Give the Order`** | 1 | **Once per encounter, spend your declaration.** One ally who can hear you **immediately makes one attack**. |
| › **`On My Mark`** | 4 | **Twice per encounter.** |
| ›› **`Command Presence`** | 8 | Twice, and the ally may instead **move its full speed without provoking**. |

**⚠ It is a transfer at a loss, which is what makes it safe.** **`ATTACKS-01 §2` gives the Officer one declaration, which may be a three-strike `Barrage`. He trades it for **one** ally attack — so it is a net loss in attacks and a gain only in *whose* attack and *when*.**

**Which is exactly what an order is: the Officer does not fight better, he decides who swings and at what moment.**

**One line:** *Spend your attack to let someone else attack now.*

**⚠ And it does not overlap `PT-145`'s henchmen.** **A henchman has its own turn; an ally with `Give the Order` is a player character acting out of sequence on the Officer's initiative.**
