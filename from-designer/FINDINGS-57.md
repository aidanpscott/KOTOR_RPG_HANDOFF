# FINDINGS-57 — ⚠ The form modifiers are a double-count. Owner ruled, and the roster proves it.

**Owner, deciding `FINDINGS-56 §1`:**

> *"lightsaber forms should not keep their stat effects. they are simply feats that unlock attack trees, with attacks that have benefits already."*

**⚠ The second half is the argument, and `ATTACKS-06` bears it out exactly.**

---

# 1 — The same stat, twice, from the same form

**`FORMS-01 §6.1` gives each form a modifier line. `ATTACKS-06` gives each form two attack chains. Set them side by side:**

## Resilience

    FORMS-01 §6.1     Defence vs current target +2 · Blaster Deflection +4 · Threat Range −1

    ATTACKS-06        Circle of Shelter    Defence +4 / +6 / +8, damage reduction 3 / 5 / 8
                      Deflecting Slash     Deflection +5 / +10 / +15

> **⚠ The form grants `+2` Defence and `+4` deflection. Its own two chains grant `+4` to `+8` Defence and `+5` to `+15` deflection.**

**Same form, same two stats, at overlapping magnitudes — and they stack.** **A Jedi declaring `Impenetrable Guard` would hold `+15` from the chain and `+4` from the form. **`+19` deflection**, four of it invisible on the entry he declared.**

## Perseverance

    FORMS-01 §6.1     Attack +2 · Deflection +2 · Critical Multiplier +1
    ATTACKS-06        Bolt return · Counterattack

**⚠ Same shape. The form pays `+2` deflection and the form's own theme is bolt return.**

## 1.1 What this means

> **The modifier line is a weaker, always-on copy of what the form's chains already do.**

**`ATTACKS-06 §21` says the form gate is the whole design — *"a Jedi in Resilience has six of these entries available, not forty-two."*** **The form's identity is delivered by its entries. The modifier line delivers it a second time, smaller, with no declaration and no cost.**

**⚠ So this is not a deletion of twenty-eight ported effects. It is the removal of a double-count that nothing in either document had noticed.**

**`REPLY-48` called it *"the largest unforced loss in this project."* It is not a loss and it is not unforced — it is the correction `ATTACKS-06` and `FORMS-01` needed against each other.**

---

# 2 — Applied

**`FORMS-01 §6.1`'s modifier column is **cut** for all seven lightsaber forms.**

    A lightsaber form is a feat. It unlocks that form's two attack chains.
    It has no other effect.

**⚠ `§6.2` — the four **Force** forms — is untouched.** **Those are a separate exclusion group, they have no attack chains behind them, and the owner has deferred what they become. Nothing here reaches them.**

## 2.1 Three things that go with the cut, and all three are gains

**`Ferocity`'s *attacks per round +1* is gone.** **⚠ As a permanent feat it would have been the largest single grant in the game, and `ACTION-ECONOMY-01 §421` cut Velocity from five strikes to three specifically to prevent that shape.**

**`Moderation`'s Force-integration clause is gone.** **`FORMS-01` had already flagged it as depending on rules not yet written.**

**⚠ And `Moderation` was the one authored form** — restored because it is in the 2DA and documented nowhere, with `+1` across five stats invented to mean *"no strengths, no weaknesses."* **The authored content goes with the cut, and Moderation becomes what the other six are: a name and two chains.**

## 2.2 ⚠ What is now unresolved instead

**With no stance, `ATTACKS-06 §5`'s central sentence is false: *"you hold one form at a time… a Jedi in Resilience has six of these entries available, not forty-two."***

**A Jedi holding three form feats has eighteen entries available, and a form-master six forms has thirty-six.**

**⚠ That sentence justified the roster's size and it needs striking or rewriting by whoever holds `ATTACKS-06`.** **The gate still exists — you cannot use a form's entries without its feat — it is just no longer *one*.**
