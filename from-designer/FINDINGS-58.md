# FINDINGS-58 — `Sneak Attack` subsumes, and the Scoundrel

**Owner:** *"higher sneak attacks override the previous. If one class already had the feat tree, they don't double — they just get subsumed so that the one tree stays the same."*

---

# 1 — The rule

> **A character holds **one** `Sneak Attack` tree. Where two classes grant it, the higher governs and the lower is subsumed. Dice never sum.**

**⚠ Consistent with `PT-159` — highest rate held, highest chain count held — and now the third quantity on the same principle. That is a pattern rather than three exceptions.**

## 1.1 ⚠ One wrinkle: *higher* is measured at your level, not at the cap

**The three ladders differ in **speed** and **cap**, and the caps are not the test.**

    level 12      Smuggler 6d6      Watchman 4d6
    level 30      Smuggler 10d6     Watchman 7d6

**And a split character reads each ladder from **its own class level**, so the comparison shifts as you advance:**

    Smuggler 10 / Scoundrel  4     Smuggler frozen at 5d6  ·  Scoundrel 4d6   -> Smuggler
    Smuggler 10 / Scoundrel  8     Smuggler frozen at 5d6  ·  Scoundrel 8d6   -> Scoundrel

> **⚠ A player's governing tree can change mid-career. At Scoundrel level 6 the new tree overtakes the frozen one and the old is subsumed from then on.**

**That is the ruling working as intended — the tree *stays the same*, one tree, and which one it is resolves to whichever is higher when you check. But it needs stating, because *"the higher"* reads as a fixed answer and is not one.**

# 2 — Scoundrel

**`PT-...` / `REPLY-28` ruled it the `Sneak Attack` specialist. `PT-73` took its pistol identity to the `Gunslinger`.**

    Specialist · d6 · Dexterity · 11 feats · saves 6/12/6 · skill base 6
    9 chains, 6 capstones · picks 18, T = 22
    Skills 7   Stealth · Sleight of Hand · Streetwise · Alertness · Awareness · Security · Persuade

> **Entry: character level 10, **Smuggler 6 or Agent 6**, and `Stealth` 8 ranks.**

**⚠ `Stealth` 8 is the same gate the `Watchman` carries and for the same reason** — **`Sneak Attack` requires `Stealth` 5 to hold at all and caps its dice at ranks ÷ 3. A Scoundrel entering without ranks would receive a tree that does nothing.**

## 2.1 The fourth speed

> **One die per Scoundrel level, capping at **12d6**.**

    Smuggler        every odd level          10d6 at 19
    Sith Assassin   every 2nd from 5          9d6 at 20
    Jedi Watchman   every 3rd                 7d6 at 19
    Scoundrel       every level              12d6 at class level 12

**⚠ It is the only ladder that gains every level, and that is the class.** **It is also a **prestige** ladder with at most twenty class levels behind it, so *every level* reaches 12 and stops — it cannot run away.**

**Priced:**

    pure Smuggler 30              10d6 = 35 average
    Smuggler 10 / Scoundrel 20    12d6 = 42 average     +7

**⚠ Two dice and it arrives sooner. That is the whole reward for committing, and it is deliberately small** — **`Sneak Attack` fires once per approach under `ACTION-ECONOMY-01 §19.5`, so seven average damage once a fight is a modest return on twenty class levels.**

**The Scoundrel's case is not that the dice are bigger. It is that nothing else about the class competes for them.**

## 2.2 Feature — `Nowhere To Stand`

**⚠ The chain has to avoid three neighbours: `Vanish` re-hides after the kill, `Nobody Saw Him Leave` moves after the kill, `No Firing Position` never reveals the position.**

**All three answer *what happens after you strike*. The Scoundrel's answers *whether the strike is available at all*.**

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Nowhere To Stand`** | 1 | **A target that has not yet acted this encounter is treated as unaware of you**, whether or not you are hidden. |
| › **`Off Balance`** | 4 | **Also a target that moved more than half its speed** on its last turn. |
| ›› **`No Safe Ground`** | 8 | **Also a target that is the only enemy within 4 metres of you**, on the reasoning that a lone target has nobody watching their back. |

**⚠ It buys `Sneak Attack` *conditions*, not dice, which is what a specialist in one mechanic should get.** **`ACTION-ECONOMY-01 §19.5` requires the target to be Hidden-from, stunned, or unable to see you; this adds three positional openings to that list and nothing else.**

**One line:** *People who are moving, distracted, or alone are open to you whether they can see you or not.*

**Not dominant:** **it grants no dice and no bonus.** **Against a target that has acted, is standing still, and has friends, the Scoundrel is an ordinary d6 class with 18 attack picks.**
