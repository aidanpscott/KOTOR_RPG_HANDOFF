# FINDINGS-61 — the shortfall stands, written as a rule rather than left as an accident

**Owner: leave it. `PT-...`.**

---

# 1 — The clause

> **A `Sneak Attack` tier is a ceiling you grow into.** **The tier states the dice it can reach; your `Stealth` ranks ÷ 3 states the dice you have. The lower governs.**

**⚠ Written this way round on purpose.** **The old wording gives a number and then quietly takes one back. This gives a ceiling and a current value, and a player reading it expects them to differ.**

**Suggested sheet line: *`Master Sneak Attack` — up to 6d6, or your `Stealth` ÷ 3 if lower.***

---

# 2 — How long it actually binds

**Derived. A class skill costs one point per rank — `SKILLS-01 §11.1` — so a Scoundrel with skill base 6 and Intelligence 12 buys `Stealth` every level and holds `L + 3` ranks.**

| Tier | Full dice need | Reached at character level |
|---|---|---|
| **Sneak Attack** 2d6 | Stealth 6 | **3** |
| › **Improved** 4d6 | Stealth 12 | **9** |
| ›› **Master** 6d6 | Stealth 18 | **15** |

**⚠ And the tiers themselves are available at `Stealth` 5, 10 and 15 — character levels 2, 7 and 12.**

    tier available    2 / 7 / 12
    tier full         3 / 9 / 15

> **One level, two levels, three levels. The gap widens as the tiers get bigger, which is the right direction — the harder technique takes longer to make good on.**

**⚠ That is now an argument for the design rather than a defence of an accident, which is what the ruling asked for.**

---

# 3 — What it does not touch

**Nothing else changes. `PT-192`'s `6d6` ceiling stands, the three speeds stand, `Killer's Instinct`'s worked example stands, and the `Scoundrel` in `FINDINGS-60 §2` is unaffected** — **its case was never the dice.**

**⚠ One consequence worth noting for whoever writes the sheet: a character who is granted the chain by class but has no `Stealth` ranks gets **nothing**.** **The chain is granted; the dice are earned. That is already true of the `Jedi Watchman`, which is why `FINDINGS-26 §3.3` put `Stealth` 8 on its entry.**
