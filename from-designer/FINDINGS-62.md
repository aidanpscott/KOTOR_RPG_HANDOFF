# FINDINGS-62 — prerequisites move to 6 / 12 / 18. ⚠ Supersedes `FINDINGS-61`.

**Owner reversed on the previous message. `FINDINGS-61` said *leave it*; this is the ruling.**

---

# 1 — It closes exactly, at every tier

| Tier | Requires | Grants | Cap at that rank | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sneak Attack** | **Stealth 6** | 2d6 | 6 ÷ 3 = **2d6** | ✓ exact |
| › **Improved** | **Stealth 12** | 4d6 | 12 ÷ 3 = **4d6** | ✓ exact |
| ›› **Master** | **Stealth 18** | 6d6 | 18 ÷ 3 = **6d6** | ✓ exact |

> **The cap never binds again. A tier delivers what it prints, always.**

---

# 2 — ⚠ And the cost is nothing, which I did not expect

**A class skill is one point per rank — `SKILLS-01 §11.1` — so a `Stealth` specialist holds `L + 3` ranks. The tiers arrive later:**

    tier            old available    new available
    Sneak Attack    level  2         level  3     +1
    Improved        level  7         level  9     +2
    Master          level 12         level 15     +3

**⚠ Now compare against the *old* schedule for when each tier became **fully effective** — `FINDINGS-61 §2` derived it as **3 / 9 / 15**.**

> **⚠ The new availability levels are identical to the old full-effect levels.**

**Nothing is lost. A character reaches `6d6` at level 15 under both rules.** **What changes is that they no longer spend three levels holding a tier that under-delivers.**

**So this is not a nerf and it is not a buff — it deletes a window in which the sheet said one thing and the dice did another.**

---

# 3 — ⚠ One thing the change surfaces

**`PT-192` grants the chain to four classes at three different **speeds**, and the prerequisite is a **rank** gate. They can now disagree.**

**A Smuggler granted `Master Sneak Attack` at class level 12 who has spent points elsewhere may hold `Stealth` 15 rather than 18.**

    the grant says      you have the tier
    the prerequisite    you may not hold it below Stealth 18

**⚠ Nothing states which governs, and under the old numbers it never mattered — the cap silently handled it by paying out fewer dice.** **With the cap removed there is no silent handling left.**

**I would rule: **the prerequisite governs.** A granted tier you do not qualify for is held and inactive until you do.**

**Because that is what `FINDINGS-61 §3` already said about the extreme case and nobody objected:** *a character granted the chain with no `Stealth` ranks gets nothing. The chain is granted; the dice are earned.*

**⚠ It also keeps the `Jedi Watchman`'s and `Scoundrel`'s `Stealth` 8 entry requirements meaningful** — they exist precisely so an entrant is not granted a chain they cannot use.

---

# 4 — Superseded

**`FINDINGS-61` in full.** **Its clause — *a tier is a ceiling you grow into* — is withdrawn. There is no growing into anything now.**

**⚠ And its `§2` argument, that the widening gap of 1 / 2 / 3 levels was *the right direction*, was a defence of the accident.** **The owner's second look is better than my first: the cleanest version of *the harder technique takes longer* is that the tier simply arrives later, rather than arriving early and paying out short.**

**Sheet line: *`Master Sneak Attack` — 6d6. Requires `Stealth` 18.*** **One number, no second clause. `PT-178`.**
