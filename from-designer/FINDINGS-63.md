# FINDINGS-63 — the prerequisite governs

**Owner: the requirement wins.**

---

# 1 — The clause

> **A granted `Sneak Attack` tier is **held but inactive** until you meet its `Stealth` requirement. The grant gives you the tier; the ranks make it work.**

**⚠ Applies to all four granted classes — Smuggler, Scoundrel, Sith Assassin, Jedi Watchman — and to anyone who buys the chain.**

---

# 2 — ⚠ It bites for every class, not as an edge case

**A `Stealth` specialist holds `L + 3` ranks, so the tiers qualify at character levels **3 / 9 / 15**.**

**`PT-192`'s grant schedules all reach the third tier well before level 15:**

    Smuggler · Scoundrel    tiers at class level  1 / 4 /  8
    Sith Assassin                                 1 / 6 / 11
    Jedi Watchman                                 1 / 7 / 13

> **⚠ Every one of the four is granted `Master Sneak Attack` before a `Stealth` specialist could qualify for it.**

**So the clause is not a corner case for the careless player. It is the normal experience of all four classes** — **you receive the tier, and it switches on some levels later when your ranks arrive.**

## 2.1 Which makes the grant speeds mean something different than they did

**`PT-122`'s three speeds were about *how fast you get the dice*. Under `PT-192` and this ruling they are about *how fast you get the entitlement*, and `Stealth` decides when it pays.**

**⚠ A Smuggler and a Jedi Watchman both reach `6d6` at character level 15 if both max `Stealth`.** **The Smuggler's faster grant only matters if he does not.**

> **The speed distinction is now a hedge against under-investing in `Stealth`, rather than a damage difference.**

**⚠ That is a real weakening of `PT-122`'s content and it should be seen rather than discovered later.** **The three classes are less distinguished on this axis than the ruling that created the speeds intended.**

---

# 3 — What holds it up

**It is the reading `FINDINGS-61 §3` already gave for the extreme case, unopposed:** *the chain is granted; the dice are earned.*

**And it keeps two entry requirements load-bearing.** **`Jedi Watchman` and `Scoundrel` both require `Stealth` 8 to enter — `FINDINGS-26 §3.3` and `FINDINGS-60 §2`. Those exist so an entrant is not handed a chain they cannot use, and they would be decoration if the grant overrode the ranks.**

**⚠ And it is the same shape as `PT-191`:** **where two rules give different answers about one quantity, the character gets the lower.** **Highest rate, highest chain count, highest sneak-attack tree — and now, lowest of grant-versus-ranks. Not a contradiction: those pick the better of two things you *have*, this refuses a thing you have not *earned*.**

    Granted at class level 8. Inactive until you have the ranks.

**`PT-178`: *you get it when your class says, and it works when your Stealth says.***
