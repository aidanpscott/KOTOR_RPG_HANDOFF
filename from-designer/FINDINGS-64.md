# 1 — ⚠ Rider or declaration: `ATTACKS-01` and `ATTACKS-05` say opposite things, right now

**`ATTACKS-01 §223`, on the stealth trees:**

> *"The dice attach to the first attack of whatever you declare. **They are not a declaration you choose instead of attacking.**"*

**`ATTACKS-05 §75`, on the same chain:**

> *"That is a feat rider granted to three classes; **this competes for the declaration**."*

**⚠ Same mechanic, two documents, flatly opposite. And I told the owner the second one an exchange ago, having read only that page.**

## 1.1 It is not a wording quibble

    as a rider          declare Barrage, sneak-attack dice land on the first strike
                        -> 3 strikes AND +6d6.  Costs you a tree and nothing else.

    as a declaration    declare Sneak Attack INSTEAD of Barrage
                        -> 1 strike at +6d6, or 3 strikes without.  A real trade.

**⚠ Roughly a 21-damage swing per round at the top tier, and it decides whether the chain is a bonus or a choice.**

## 1.2 Which is right

**`ATTACKS-01` governs — it is the framework document and `§223` is where the move was ruled.** **`ATTACKS-05 §75` is a line written about the chain's relationship to `Killer's Instinct` that overreached into a claim about declarations.**

**⚠ And the rider reading is the one the corpus supports elsewhere:** **`ACTION-ECONOMY-01 §19.5` says *"attacking reveals you, so it fires once per approach"*, which describes something that happens *when you attack*, not something you attack *with*.**

**⚠ Sixth instance of the same failure this session** — a mechanic stated twice with different content, in documents that cite each other. **`FINDINGS-59 §2`'s method found it in one grep and it should be run against every chain that moved between documents, not just this one.**
