# FINDINGS-66 — trees open to all. ⚠ Two corrections to the reasoning, one of which matters.

**Owner: accept the pricing, `Sneak Attack` and `Stealthy Shot` open to every class, `Killer's Instinct` is what makes them worth taking.**

---

# 1 — ⚠ The stealth trees do not stun

**Owner: *"it has the bonus of a) surprise and b) stunning a target (or I think it does at least… don't remember)."***

**Derived, `ATTACKS-05` — the full tree:**

    Sneak Attack     lvl 1    +2d6 vs unaware. Attacking reveals you.
    › Improved       lvl 5    +4d6, and you may attempt to re-hide in the same declaration
    ›› Master        lvl 10   +6d6, and critical threat multiplier +1 vs an unaware target

> **⚠ No stun at any tier.**

**The stun belongs to `Critical Strike` and `Precise Shot`:** *"a first-attack hit stuns the target for one round unless it saves — Fortitude, DC 10 + your level + Strength."*

**⚠ And *surprise* is not a bonus of the chain either — it is the **condition** that lets it fire.** **`ACTION-ECONOMY-01 §19.5`: unaware means Hidden from, Stunned, or unable to see. You need surprise to use `Sneak Attack`; you do not get surprise from it.**

## 1.1 What the tree actually gives beyond damage

**Two things, and both are real:**

**The re-hide at tier 2** — *"you may attempt to re-hide as part of the same declaration."* **⚠ That is the chain paying its own condition back. It is the only entry in either roster that restores the state it consumed, and it is what makes the chain repeatable rather than once-per-fight.**

**The threat multiplier at tier 3** — `+1` against an unaware target, on top of `PT-176`'s no-compounding rule.

**⚠ So the acceptance still holds, but on the re-hide rather than on a stun.** **The chain is not *one big hit*; it is *a hit you can set up again*, which is a different and better argument for the same conclusion.**

# 2 — ⚠ The Scoundrel is not granted `Killer's Instinct`

**`FEATS-LIBRARY-01 §248`:** *"Granted to the three classes that carried `Sneak Attack` in the source."* **Named in `PT-122`'s working: Smuggler, Sith Assassin, Jedi Watchman.**

**The `Scoundrel` is a prestige class written afterwards and is not among them.**

> **⚠ So the class I built as the sneak-attack specialist does not get the feat the owner has just identified as *the thing that makes the trees worth taking*.**

**Priced, at the top tier:**

    Smuggler       Sneak Attack + Killer's Instinct     31.1 a round
    Scoundrel      Sneak Attack alone                   23.8
    anyone         Barrage                              27.3

**⚠ A Scoundrel would be worse at sneak attacking than the Smuggler he prestiged from, and worse than an ordinary melee character who never bothered.**

**Fix is one word: add the `Scoundrel` to the granted list.** **It continues the Smuggler, it is the class built on the mechanic, and the list's own wording — *"the three classes that carried `Sneak Attack` in the source"* — is a statement about the source, not a design principle. The Scoundrel did not exist in the source to carry anything.**

---

# 3 — Applied

**`Sneak Attack` and `Stealthy Shot` are open to every class with the matching roster.** **No class restriction, no `Stealth` prerequisite, gated `1 / 5 / 10`.**

**⚠ And the design reads as the owner describes it: the trees are a **gate**, not a reward.** **They cost a whole declaration and a whole chain slot, they do less than an unconditional chain, and they only pay for a character who has invested in being unseen. Someone who wants to swing three times takes Barrage and never looks at them.**

**That is the chain doing its job. Recorded as intent rather than as a balance concession, so nobody later reads 23.8-against-27.3 as a defect and "fixes" it.**
