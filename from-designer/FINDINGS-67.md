# 1 — Applied

    Killer's Instinct — granted to    Smuggler · Sith Assassin · Jedi Watchman · Scoundrel

**⚠ And the entry's own wording can no longer stand.** **`FEATS-LIBRARY-01 §248` reads *"granted to the three classes that carried `Sneak Attack` in the source."***

**That is now false twice — the count is four, and the fourth did not exist in the source.**

**Proposed replacement: *granted to the four classes built on striking an unaware target.*** **⚠ A design statement rather than a provenance one, which is what the list has become.**

## 1.1 The Scoundrel now prices correctly

    Scoundrel, Sneak Attack + Killer's Instinct    31.1 a round
    Barrage, unconditional                         27.3
    Scoundrel without it                           23.8

> **The specialist is now better at the thing than an ordinary character with an ordinary chain, which is the least a specialist should be.**

**⚠ And it is still conditional.** **31.1 fires only against an unaware target, once per approach unless the tier-2 re-hide lands. Barrage's 27.3 fires every round against anyone.**

# 2 — ⚠ What this makes the Scoundrel, now that everything is settled

**The class has been rebuilt three times by rulings landing underneath it, so it is worth stating what survived.**

    Specialist · d6 · Dexterity · 11 feats · saves 6/12/6 · skill base 6
    9 chains, 6 capstones · picks 18, T = 22
    Entry: character level 10, Smuggler 6 or Agent 6, Stealth 8 ranks
    Granted: Killer's Instinct

**Feature — `Nowhere To Stand`:** targets who have not acted, who moved more than half their speed, or who stand alone are **unaware of you** whether or not you are hidden.

> **⚠ Every other class buys the stealth trees and then has to *get* unaware. The Scoundrel is the class that finds unaware targets where nobody else can see any.**

**And that reads better after `PT-...`'s declaration ruling than it did before it.** **A wasted declaration costs a whole turn, so the class whose feature is *there is always an opening* is worth more the more expensive the opening is to miss.**

**⚠ Its dice are identical to the Smuggler's. Its openings are not. That is the whole class and it took four rulings to arrive at a shape that was drafted before any of them.**
