# 1 — ⚠ The obvious tank feature is taken, and the corpus says what is not

**`Hold the Line` is the Soldier's: *take the hit meant for the ally beside you*. A Juggernaut that absorbs damage for allies is the Soldier with a bigger die.**

**And `ACTION-ECONOMY-01 §105`–`111` states the gap directly:**

> *"Opportunity attacks trigger on an enemy **leaving** melee reach. **Nothing triggers on an enemy closing.**"*
>
> *"That is deliberate and it is D&D's own line… **melee gets zone control and ranged gets range**."*

**⚠ The corpus names *zone control* as melee's compensation and then implements almost none of it.** **One feat — `Guarded Step`, which fires when an enemy moves to flank.**

> **So the Juggernaut is not the class that takes hits. It is the class you cannot get past.**

**The pair reads cleanly: the Soldier takes the blow meant for the ally; the Juggernaut stops the enemy reaching the ally at all.**

# 3 — Feature — `Immovable Object`

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Immovable Object`** | 1 | **You cannot be knocked back, knocked prone, or `Slowed`.** |
| › **`No Way Through`** | 4 | **An enemy that begins its turn in your melee reach cannot leave that reach** except by `Disengage`, and `Disengage` against you costs its whole movement. |
| ›› **`The Line Holds`** | 8 | As above, and **an enemy that closes into your reach provokes an opportunity attack** from you. |

**⚠ The capstone is the exception `§107` names and declines:** *"entering reach provokes nothing without a specific feat — 5e's Polearm Master, and 2024 removed even that."*

**Granting it to one prestige class, at level 8, on a `Combat` chassis with a `Heavy Armour` gate, is the narrowest possible version of the thing the corpus deliberately did not give everyone.**

**Priced.** **Tier 1 answers `Power Attack`, which *"knocks the target back 3 squares and knocks it prone."* — the most common melee opener in the game. **A Juggernaut ignores it.****

**⚠ And tier 2 is the real one: it does not stop enemies **reaching** you, it stops them **leaving**.** **A Juggernaut who closes on the enemy line pins it, and the party's ranged half shoots into a fight that cannot spread.**

**Not dominant:** **it grants no attack bonus, no damage, and no extra Defence.** **Against a ranged enemy that never entered reach it does nothing at all, and `Disengage` still works — it just costs everything.**

**One line, `PT-178`:** *Nothing moves him, and nothing gets past him.*
