# 1 — ⚠ Every utility the owner described already exists as a species trait

**`SPECIES-CHAPTER-v2` carries four droid chassis as full species records. I was about to author *reach hard places, fly, slice, hack* and all four are already written:**

| Chassis | What it already does |
|---|---|
| **Astromech** | **`Portable Workbench` — *"any character may perform item construction or upgrade work anywhere the droid is, without needing a facility."*** `Spike Fabrication` once per session. **+2 Slicing, +2 Repair** — the only entry in the chapter with two racial skills |
| **Remote** | **`Repulsorlift Frame` — hovers, ignores difficult terrain, makes no footfalls, +4 Stealth.** 12 m speed. `Shield Projector` gives an adjacent ally damage reduction 5. **Tiny or Medium** |
| **Assassin** | **The entire ranged suite — `ATTACKS-01 §242`.** Ships with a vocabulator and speaks all five languages |
| **Battle** | **`Mass-Produced` — repair takes half the normal time and cost.** Always speaks Basic and Binary |

> **⚠ *Flies* is the Remote. *Slices and hacks* is the Astromech. *Reaches hard places* is the Remote at Tiny with `+4 Stealth`. *Upgrades* is `Portable Workbench`, verbatim.**

**`AGENDA-CURRENT §215` already said so and I had not read it:** *"the Droid Master is not blocked — its droids are **species** and the list already exists."*

**So the class does not need droid rules. It needs to say how many you get and what they may do.**

# 2 — `Command Protocol`, rebuilt

| Tier | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **`Command Protocol`** | 1 | **You hold a primary droid** — any chassis, built at your level, upgradeable, **it acts on your turn and may attack.** **And one support droid**, any chassis, built at half your level, **which may not attack.** |
| › **`Squad Doctrine`** | 4 | **Two support droids.** |
| ›› **`Master and Servants`** | 8 | **Three support droids.** |

**⚠ Support droids bring their chassis traits and nothing else.** **A Remote scouts, hovers and shields. An Astromech slices, fabricates spikes and is a mobile workbench. They do not attack, so four droids is not four attacks.**

## 2.1 What it fixes

    damage         4 attacks -> 1        only the primary fights
    turns          5 -> 1                all act on your turn, FINDINGS-69
    clauses        4 -> 1                nothing to persist through
    the strong one  ✓                    the primary is the one he starts with

**⚠ And `PT-151`'s unanswerable question is now answered.** **It said the class's *"power is measured in other people's time."*** **A primary droid that attacks is one character's worth of damage; three supports that cannot attack are worth nothing on the damage axis and everything off it. That is measurable.**

**One line:** *You command a droid that fights and up to three that do things nobody else can.*

## 2.2 On upgrades

**`EQUIPMENT-01` has the upgrade *curve* — three points on the melee track — and states plainly that the full item list is *"a data-extraction job, not a research job"* and not done.**

**⚠ So *upgradeable* is real for weapons and armour and undefined for droids specifically.** **`Portable Workbench` removes the facility requirement, which is the access half; the item half waits on the same extraction the Machinist's chain waits on.**

**Recorded, not blocked — the class works today with a primary droid that levels.**
