## 1.1 The Sith Assassin keeps its feature and loses what made it *the* Sith striker

**`Vanish` triggers off *"an attack of yours that deals `Sneak Attack` damage."*** **The chain still exists, so the trigger still fires.**

**⚠ But its differentiation was arithmetic.** **`PT-122` gave it `9d6` against the Smuggler's `10d6` and the Watchman's `7d6`. `PT-193` caps everyone at `6d6` and opens the chain to every class.**

> **A Sith Assassin's stealth damage is now identical to a Duelist's who spent one tree on it.**

**What is left is `Vanish` plus `Killer's Instinct` — a real distinction, and a smaller one than the class was built with.**
