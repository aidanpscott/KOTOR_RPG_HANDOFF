**`REPLY-59`:** *"**not droids** survives the move and cuts harder now — a Droid Master's four henchmen gain nothing from their own party's Officer."*

**⚠ `FINDINGS-76 §2.2` ruled the opposite, deliberately, and gave the reason:**

> **`PT-210`: share what is *training*, withhold what is *instinct* or *anatomy*.** **An order is training. A droid follows orders better than anyone.**

**The source clause is a **Force-morale** artefact — `Inspire Followers` was a Light-side power raising *morale*, and a droid has none. An Officer's version is not morale; it is instruction.**

**⚠ And the consequence `REPLY-59` names is the argument against its own reading:** **a Droid Master fielding four henchmen would gain nothing at all from the one class in the roster whose entire feature is making allies better.**

**Not settled by me. Flagged as a live disagreement with a stated warrant on my side.**
