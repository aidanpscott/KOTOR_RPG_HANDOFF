# FINDINGS-79 — `Beast Handling` to the Explorer, and orders reach droids without commanding them

**Two owner rulings, one of them delegated to me and decided on data.**

---

# 1 — ⚠ `Beast Handling` was the only single-class skill in the game

**Owner left the call to me. Derived across all sixteen parsed class lists before deciding:**

    Beast Handling      1    Scout
    Archaeology         2    Explorer · Jedi Consular
    Swim                2    Soldier · Scout
    Botany              3    Doctor · Explorer · Scout
    Sleight of Hand     3    Machinist · Smuggler · Marksman

> **⚠ Every other skill in the game is held by at least two classes. `Beast Handling` was held by one.**

**Not a tight design choice — an outlier of one, and the only one.**

## 1.1 Decided: it goes to the Explorer

**Three reasons beyond the anomaly.**

**`SKILLS-01 §3` created the skill so *"non-Force characters get parity with a Force option"* — `Beast Trick`, `Beast Confusion` and `Dominate Beast` already do this job for Jedi.** **⚠ One class in thirty-six is not parity.**

**The Explorer already holds `Botany`.** **He is the other naturalist, and `Botany` without `Beast Handling` is a strange half of that — he knows the plants and not the animals.**

**And it opens `Beast Master` to two parents**, which `PT-217`'s model wants and could not have while the skill was locked to one class.

## 1.2 Checked before deciding, not after

    Explorer  9 skills   Archaeology · Xenology · Appraise · Science · Botany ·
                         Alertness · Awareness · Pilot · Mysticism
    Scout    11 skills

    shared now       4 of 9   = 44%
    shared after     5 of 10  = 50%

**⚠ `PT-83` split the tech pair at 89% and kept Scout/Soldier at 86%. Fifty percent is not close to either.**

**And the two remain different classes: the Scout's list is fieldcraft — `Acrobatics`, `Demolitions`, `Repair`, `Scavenging`, `Swim`. The Explorer's is knowledge — `Archaeology`, `Xenology`, `Science`, `Mysticism`.**

## 1.3 The consequence

    Beast Master   entry: level 10, Scout 6 or Explorer 6, Beast Handling 8

**⚠ And it now passes `PT-217`'s own test, which it failed in `FINDINGS-78 §1.2`.** **Both admitted parents hold the skill as a class skill.**

# 2 — Orders reach droids; command does not transfer

**Owner: *"the Officer's orders reach droids under his command like anyone else, but he does not control the droids."***

**⚠ That is cleaner than either reading in `FINDINGS-78 §2` and it resolves the tier-2 clause, which neither of us had noticed was the actual question.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **The buff** — `+1/+2/+3` attack, damage and Will | **applies to any ally who can hear him, droid or not** |
| **The immediate attack** at tiers 2 and 3 | **granted, not directed** — if the ally is a henchman, **its controller decides what it does with the opening** |

> **The Officer creates the moment. He does not take the controls.**

**⚠ So a Droid Master's four droids all fight better under an Officer and still belong entirely to the Droid Master.** **`PT-145` makes a henchman a character, and a character can be given an opening by an ally without changing whose character it is.**

**And it settles the *"not droids"* clause without either side of the disagreement winning:** **the source's exclusion was about morale, which droids lack; the Officer's version is instruction, which they follow — and neither of those touches who is holding the leash.**

# 3 — Standing

**⚠ Every class in the roster now has a rate, a stat line, an entry requirement that passes `PT-217`'s test, and a feature.**

**Outstanding from `FINDINGS-78`, unanswered:**

**`Droid Master` — `Repair` 8 taxes the Engineer, who does not hold `Repair` as a class skill.** **The Machinist does. `PT-217` adopted the entry in the same document that wrote the test that catches it.**

**⚠ And unlike `Beast Handling`, this one has no skill-list fix available** — **`Repair` is already held by four classes, so the defect is in the entry rather than in the skill.**

**Then the owner's items: starting attacks for every standard base class, and first-level multiclass benefits.**
