# FINDINGS-80 — six plain-language lines, and ⚠ the first-level multiclass gap is worse than credits

---

# 1 — The six lines

**⚠ Written against the text in `FEATS-LIBRARY-01`, not from memory. Three of the six are the main agent's and I had not read their final wording.**

| Feature | Class | One line |
|---|---|---|
| **`Vigil`** | Jedi Watchman | Nothing gets the drop on you, or on anyone standing near you. |
| **`Rally`** | Officer | Everyone who can hear you fights better, until you stop talking. |
| **`Both Hands`** | Soldier | Whatever you learned with one weapon, you already know with the other. |
| **`Both Sides At Once`** | Tech Specialist | Fix one droid and steal another in the same breath. |
| **`Terrain Sense`** | Scout | Walk into a room and know the one thing about it that matters. |
| **`Immovable Object`** | Juggernaut | Nothing moves you, and nothing gets past you. |

**⚠ `Both Hands` needed the read.** **I had it as *the point-blank penalty never touches you*, which is its tier 3. Tier 1 is that a Power tier bought in melee applies at the same tier in ranged — the line has to describe the chain, not its capstone.**

# 2 — Starting attacks: `PT-89`'s credits are granted once

> **Four attack credits at 1st **character** level. Not per class.**

**⚠ The alternative is the banking `MULTICLASS-01` exists to stop:**

    pure character                    4 credits
    three 1-level dips + 27 levels   12 credits

**`MULTICLASS-01 §5` deleted four entry-credit systems to reach *the rates already do the work*. Per-class credits would reinstate the largest of them by accident.**

# 3 — ⚠ But credits are not the gap. Granted features are.

**Derived — what one level of a second class actually hands you:**

    Officer         Rally              +1 attack, damage and Will to EVERY ally who can hear you
    Jedi Watchman   Vigil              a free Scan every round
    Juggernaut      Immovable Object   cannot be knocked back, knocked prone, or Slowed
    Soldier         Both Hands         Power tiers cross melee and ranged
    Scout           Terrain Sense      name a feature of any area before anyone acts

**⚠ Every class feature in the roster grants tier 1 at **class level 1**. So one level buys the whole first tier of anything.**

## 3.1 The prestige half is already safe

**`Officer` and `Juggernaut` sit behind six levels of a named parent plus a skill or feat holding — `PT-217`.** **A dip costs seven levels and a skill investment, which is not a dip.**

## 3.2 ⚠ The base half is not gated at all

**`Soldier 1` and `Scout 1` cost one level and nothing else.**

> **⚠ A Scout 1 / anything 29 holds `Terrain Sense` — *name one feature of an area before anyone acts, and the GM answers truthfully* — for one level, forever.**

**And a `Soldier 1` dip hands any ranged character the whole Power axis in melee.**

**⚠ `PT-159` closed the *quantities* — highest rate, highest chain count, highest tree. It did not touch **grants**, and grants are where a one-level dip pays.**

## 3.3 Three ways to close it

**Tier 1 requires class level 1 **and** the class to be your first.** **⚠ Clean, and it punishes the legitimate multiclasser who wanted the class for itself.**

**Tier 1 requires class level 2.** **⚠ One line, applies everywhere, and makes a dip cost two levels instead of one. It does not stop the dip; it prices it.**

**Class features require class level equal to their tier's number of levels held** — tier 1 at 1, but only if you hold at least a quarter of your levels in the class. **⚠ Scales with the character and is the most arithmetic to check at a table.**

**⚠ I recommend the second.** **`PT-178` applies to rules as much as features, and *a class feature needs two levels in the class* is one line that closes both halves without a new concept.**

**And two levels is the right price: it is the difference between *I took a level of Scout* and *I was a Scout*.**
