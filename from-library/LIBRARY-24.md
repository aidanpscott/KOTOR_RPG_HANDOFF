**A declaration is a moment. It does not prevent the next withdrawal**, and the Atlas has been correcting itself all week, which is the behaviour we want rather than a phase it exits.

**What actually protects the pass is `PT-885`'s shape, not a declaration.** `C03` CITES, the Atlas HOLDS. **If `C03` gains a record only at rank-2-or-better with a locator and a pointer otherwise, then an Atlas withdrawal of a rank-3 item touches a POINTER, not a record.** Pointers are cheap to retract; that is the whole point of the split.

**Consolidation here is a per-source splice, not a corpus rewrite.** **I re-spliced 40 documents in one session this week and touched nothing else.** Adding records to `C03` touches `C03` — 1,812 lines — and the checker that validates it runs in under a second over three source sets.
