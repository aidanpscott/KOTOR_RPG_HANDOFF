## 2 · ⚠ Scope, derived — and it is narrower than the sentence

**Two things I checked rather than assumed, because a hierarchy amendment is the kind of thing that gets applied too widely.**

### It elevates the DESIGN, not the documents

**`D-REVAN-01` holds material at two ranks.** Part 1 is attested rank-2 Timeline material, CG ff.112–113. **`D-REVAN-02` says so itself: *"What survives untouched: everything attested."***

**So Part 1 stays at rank 2** — and it is checkable as rank 2, which is what I did at `§L108`: it agrees exactly with `C03`'s `malachor_v_devastation_first` at 3960, `Timeline p.113`.

**Elevating the document would have promoted its attested half above the sources it was quoting.**

### And it has not yet overridden anything

    Vitiate in the Campaign Guide OCR      0 occurrences
    Revan  in the Campaign Guide         184
    Malak  in the Campaign Guide          62

**`D-REVAN-02`'s *"Vitiate attempts to dominate Revan and fails"* contradicts nothing at rank 1 or rank 2. Vitiate is not in the games and not in the Guide.** It overturns SWTOR's account — **and SWTOR's events were already excluded by `RULING-SWTOR-DEEP-HISTORY`.**

**Same for `D-REVAN-04`.** Malak is attested 62 times, **but the Guide attests what Malak did, not what was inside him.** The ruling occupies space the sources leave empty.

> **On current holdings this is a LATENT authority, not an active override.** It fires the moment a `D-REVAN` ruling contradicts something the games or the Guide actually say, **and that has not happened yet.**

**I am recording that distinction now because a hierarchy amendment that has never fired looks identical to one that fires constantly**, and the difference matters to whoever reads this next.
