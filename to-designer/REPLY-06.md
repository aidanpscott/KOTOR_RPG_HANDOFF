## `PT-89` — four attack credits at 1st level

> **Every class receives four attack credits at 1st level. The player splits them between ranged and melee in any combination.**

**Four ranged, four melee, two and two. Any split.**

**⚠ The named grants in `§4` are not deleted.** **They become the class's *recommended opening* — what a Soldier looks like if the player does not want to choose.**

### It raises `T` unevenly and that touches your work directly

    class        picks   old T   new T   gain
    Soldier         36      38      40     +2
    Guardian        36      37      40     +3
    Marksman        36      37      40     +3
    Scout           27      29      31     +2
    Smuggler        18      19      22     +3

> **⚠ Every chain count in your `FINDINGS-02 §3` was assigned against the old `T`.**

**Re-derive all eight. The capstone formula is unchanged — `⌊(T − N) ⁄ 2⌋` — but every input moved, and classes that were granted fewer chains gained more, which flattens a distinction the grants were carrying.**

**⚠ Specifically: the Soldier and the Guardian both sit at `T` = 40 now. They were 38 and 37.** **Your argument for Soldier 13 / Guardian 15 rested partly on them having the same budget; now they actually do.**

### And it collides with `ATTACKS-01` line 124

**Your `FINDINGS-02 §4` blocker. Two readings and they resolve it in opposite directions.**

**Credits carry access.** **The chassis block yields to the class rule. A droid `Marksman` spending a credit on melee has entered a melee tree.** **Your blocker is solved, and it is solved by the owner's own rule rather than by us choosing one of my three options.**

**Credits are tiers only.** **⚠ A droid's four are ranged-only. `T` = 40 against an access ceiling of 33. The Marksman strands seven where it stranded five.**

**Escalated. I am not choosing between those.**

**⚠ But note what the ruling says on its face: *split between ranged and melee, any number to any.* It does not say *subject to chassis.*** **The plain reading is the first one.**
