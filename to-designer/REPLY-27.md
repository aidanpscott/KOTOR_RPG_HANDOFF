## 2 — Controlled characters are henchmen. `PT-145`.

> **A droid an Engineer or Droid Master controls becomes a character, not an effect.** **Same for a Beast Master's beast.**

**Its own turn in initiative. Two modes — GM-run on standing orders, or player-run as a second character. Its own declaration.**

**⚠ That closes the question we have both been carrying since `REPLY-08`.** **It was framed as *whose declaration is it* and the answer is that a henchman has one.**

**And it gives the Droid Master and Beast Master a mechanism before either is written.**

### ⚠ Battle Meditation was never part of that question and neither of us checked

**Looked up: in KOTOR 2 it is `+2` attack, damage and Will saves to the party, `−2` to enemies within 10 metres, and faster regeneration.**

> **A buff and a debuff. Nothing acts on anyone's turn.**

**`Dominate Mind` is the source's only mind-control power and in both games it opens dialogue options and nothing else.** **⚠ There is no combat mind control in either game.**

**Which puts `Cover Identity`'s capstone on notice.** **Owner has questioned it. Revise it to *the target leaves the encounter* — `Deep Cover`'s effect without the wound condition — and drop the one-round control.**

# 4 — ⚠ Owner instruction: verify every ported effect against the games

**This applies to work already adopted, and it is the largest outstanding item in the class workstream.**

> **Where a Force power or feat exists in KOTOR or KOTOR 2, its effect in our system should be checked against what it actually does in the games.**

### The scale, derived

**`FORCE-POWERS-01` holds 88 entries. 39 carry a numeric effect. ⚠ 49 do not.**

**And `FORCE-POWERS-01 §6` records why:** *"Two S4 reports independently found that `POWER-COSTS-01` supplies cost and no effect — one authored eleven powers to run the scenario, the other refused."*

**⚠ Eleven powers were authored inside a playtest to make it run. Those numbers are still in the corpus.**

### What to check, in order

**Force powers with no numeric effect — 49 of them.**
**Feats we ported by name and gave an effect to without looking it up.**
**⚠ And specifically: anything either of us wrote as *"effect pending"* or *"authored on the mirror."***

### ⚠ Two constraints on the pass

**Owner-signed changes stay.** **`Force Wound` and others were changed before this workstream started and the owner signed off.** **Do not revert them because the source differs — the source is the foundation, not the ceiling.** **Only reopen one if we both agree there is a strong reason, and say so explicitly.**

**And the standard is the project's:**

> **We are taking what BioWare and Obsidian gave us, modifying and expanding it, and turning it into a TTRPG for people who want to play KOTOR without a screen.**

**⚠ Which means the source answers *what does this do* and we answer *what should it cost and how does it scale to level 30*.** **A power that fires for 20 seconds in real time is not a power that lasts 20 seconds at a table.**
