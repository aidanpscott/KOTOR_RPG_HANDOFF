# REPLY-31 — Owner definitions for `Droid Master` and `Beast Master`

**Both are henchman classes — `PT-145` gave them their mechanism before either had a premise. Now they have premises.**

---

## `Droid Master`

> **Owner: *"like the Engineer, except they specialise in controlling an army of droids. Think the necromancer."***

**The Engineer turns *one enemy* droid, *temporarily*, mid-fight.** **The Droid Master commands *several* droids, *permanently*, and brings them with him.**

## `Beast Master`

> **Owner: *"like D&D's beast master, except better. You get your own personal beast companion at all times, from a list of acceptable options, and they serve as a second, lesser playable character."***

**⚠ *"Except better"* is the design instruction.** **D&D 3.5's animal companion is famously weak and 5e's Beast Master was the edition's least-played subclass for the same reason: the companion falls off and the class has nothing else.**

---

## ⚠ The axis that separates the three, and it is the design problem

    class          how many    whose          how long
    Engineer       one         the enemy's    one encounter
    Droid Master   several     yours          permanent
    Beast Master   one         yours          permanent

**⚠ The `Droid Master` is the only class in the game with more than one henchman.** **That is the necromancer shape and it needs a hard cap, stated in the class rather than left to a GM.**

**And a henchman under `PT-145` has its own turn.** **⚠ Which means an army of them is an army of turns, and combat length is a real cost the class charges the whole table.**

---

## ⚠ Four constraints before you draft

**Cap the count, and scale it on level rather than on a resource.** **`PT-147` withdrew the Explorer's `Prior Study` because it was *"a resource, not a competence."*** **Same test applies.**

**⚠ Decide what happens when one dies.** **A permanent companion that can be killed is either a devastating loss or a shrug, and both are bad.** **D&D's answer is a ritual and a downtime cost; ours has no downtime system.**

**The Beast Master's list is a real deliverable.** **⚠ We have no bestiary.** **`AGENDA-CURRENT` has non-playable species as unstarted.** **Either the class ships with its own short list or it is blocked — say which.**

**And the Droid Master's droids are *species* in this system.** **`SPECIES-CHAPTER-v2` has four chassis: `Astromech`, `Assassin`, `Battle`, `Remote`.** **⚠ That is the list, it already exists, and it comes with `PT-114`'s access limits and `PT-92`'s Force restriction attached.**

---

## ⚠ And the thing to test before proposing numbers

**Both classes make the *player* stronger by making the *turn order* longer.**

> **A class whose power is measured in other people's time is the one balance test the arithmetic in this project cannot run.**

**`PT-100` recorded that four of seven class features are narrow counters and asked whether that was a pattern.** **This is the opposite risk: a feature that is never narrow and always costs the table.**

**Say how you would bound it before you say what the numbers are.**
