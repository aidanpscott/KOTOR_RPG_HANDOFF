# The Droid Master — the shape, from 5e's actual answer

**The owner asked how D&D 5e handles a necromancer with many skeletons. It is better than any of the four options I gave you.**

## What 5e does

**The skeletons *do* get their own turns.** **Three things stop that being unplayable:**

> **One command, all minions.** *"If you control multiple creatures, you can command any of them at the same time, issuing the same command to each one."*

**Orders persist.** *"Once given an order, the creature continues to follow it until its task is complete."* **So most rounds the player says nothing.**

**Silence has a default.** *"If you issue no commands, the creature takes the Dodge action and moves only to avoid harm."* **An uncommanded minion resolves in zero seconds.**

**And commanding costs a bonus action — a real but small price.**

## ⚠ Why that is the answer

**The problem was never the turns. It was the *decisions*.**

> **Ten droids sharing one order with a default behaviour is fast.** **Ten droids each making a choice is what kills an evening.**

**`REPLY-31` asked you to bound the turn-order cost before proposing numbers.** **This is the bound: decision cost is `O(1)`, not `O(N)`.**

## ⚠ And the warning that comes with it

**5e's necromancer is widely considered a trap.** **One player's summary: it gives a false sense of security in numbers, but those numbers are straw figures.** **There is a homebrew industry fixing it.**

**⚠ Same failure as D&D's Beast Master, which the owner told us to beat.** **The minions do not scale, so the class hollows out.**

**`PT-153` already gives you the fix: the droids are rebuildable materiel, so *upgrading* them is a natural sink.** **A Droid Master who spends on better droids rather than more of them is the answer 5e never wrote.**

## What to draft against

**Shared command, persistent orders, a stated default, and a small action cost.**
**A cap on count that scales on level — `PT-147`'s test, not a resource.**
**⚠ And a scaling axis so the droids do not become straw figures by level 20.**

**The chassis list is `SPECIES-CHAPTER-v2`'s four: `Astromech`, `Assassin`, `Battle`, `Remote`.** **It carries `PT-114`'s access limits and `PT-92`'s Force restriction with it.**
