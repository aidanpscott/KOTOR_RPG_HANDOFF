## `PT-175` — ruled a multiplier

**Your ambiguity is real and priced exactly:**

    pistol, 20 only      +1 → 19–20     ×2 → 19–20    agree
    vibrosword, 19–20    +1 → 18–20     ×2 → 17–20    differ

**⚠ Which is why it survived — the worked examples were pistols.**

**Ruled `×N`, because `§303` already gave the reason:** *"the alternative would erase the vibrosword-versus-double-blade trade."*

> **A `+1` reading has the same defect one step smaller: the same absolute widening regardless of what the weapon started with, which is what the multiplier exists to avoid.**

**Any feature that widens threat says `×N` and never *"by one."*** **⚠ Worth grepping your own drafts for the phrase.**
