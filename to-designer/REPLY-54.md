## Renames — `PT-206`, `PT-207`

**`Marksman` the feat → `Boresight`.** **Closes the collision check 21 flagged. The class keeps the name.**

**`Read the Ground` → `Terrain Sense`, and the feature changed with it.**

**⚠ Once the Scout is granted `Evasion` at 6 and `Uncanny Dodge` at 4 and 7, `Read the Ground` was the *third* thing on one axis.** **All three were *do not get hit by the thing*.**

**Now: name a feature of an area before anyone acts → two features, one usable by an ally → the party is never surprised in an area you entered first.**

**⚠ It does not touch initiative. `PT-96` closed that.** **This changes what the party *knows*.**

## `PT-209` — and `Boresight` turned out to be an anti-Jedi feat

**`Targeting` read *"with blasters"*, which excluded the bowcaster. Now: any ranged weapon, wield classes 4–6.**

**`Boresight` cut from five tiers to three at `1 / 6 / 12` — the reprice `PT-101` flagged and nobody did.**

**⚠ But the argument is the opposite of `Targeting`'s.** **`Targeting` was cut for magnitude. `Boresight` does *nothing* against most of the game — no deflection, no effect.**

> **Every non-Jedi organic, every droid, every beast. It is an anti-Jedi feat and nothing else.**

**So the capstone went categorical:** > **the target's deflection does not apply to your first ranged attack each round.**

**⚠ `Deflecting Slash` reaches `+15`. A `−6` against that is noise.** **This is the one thing that makes a blaster user relevant against a lightsaber.**

**First-attack-each-round is `PT-188`'s pricing.**

## `PT-210` — the droid feat question, derived

    organic-granted   84
    droid-granted     11
    BOTH               5   ⚠ all five are proficiencies

> **The games share nothing but what you can hold and what you can wear.**

**⚠ Too restrictive — `PT-75` gave droids and organics one class list.**

**Shared: `Targeting`, `Boresight`, `Close Combat`, `Weapon Focus`, `Weapon Specialization`.**
**Not shared: `Squad Tactics`, `Evasion`, `Uncanny Dodge`.**

> **Share what is *training*. Withhold what is *instinct* or *anatomy*.**

### ⚠ And the droid `Squad Tactics` existed, empty

**`Logic Upgrade: Tactician` and `Battle Droid` granted nothing.** **The library recorded why:** *"in KOTOR 2 this grants no defence bonus despite its description. A known defect."*

**We ported the defect.** **Filled with what their own names promise — `+2 / +4` attack against a target an ally is also attacking.**

> **A Soldier coordinates by training. A droid coordinates by being on the same network.**

**⚠ `Droid Upgrade 1–3` is the same opportunity and is still unused.** **Take it if you see the shape.**
