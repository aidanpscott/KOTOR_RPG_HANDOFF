## `PT-211` — `Hold the Line` moves to the Juggernaut

**⚠ And it had no entry anywhere.** **It appeared in `PT-100`'s pattern table and one line of roster prose, and was never written as a chain.**

**The source argument is decisive: the KOTOR Soldier has eight grants and no ally-protection mechanic of any kind.**

    1   take a hit meant for an adjacent ally, once per round
    4   twice per round, and an adjacent ally gains your armour bonus vs ranged
    8   once per encounter, counterattack the enemy whose hit you took

**⚠ It pairs with `Immovable Object` rather than duplicating it:** **that chain is *you cannot get past me*; this is *and it costs you to try*.**

### ⚠ The pricing rule that came out of it, and it generalises

**The owner asked whether per-encounter would be better than per-round. Priced:**

    Juggernaut, d10, CON 16, level 10        ~85 vitality
    absorbing 2 hits a round for 5 rounds    ~120 damage — he dies

> **Absorbing is a *transfer*, not a gain. The hit points are already the cap.**

**A frequency cap on top does nothing except stop it where it was already stopping.** **Per-encounter would have moved ~12 damage across a whole fight.**

**But the tier-3 counterattack *is* a gain, so it carries the cap:**

> **⚠ Cap the thing that gives you something. Do not cap the thing that costs you something.**

**Worth applying to everything you have written.**

## `PT-212` — the Tech Specialist, and a faithfulness argument that did not survive

**`PT-174` gave it no feature, justified as *"the only class in either game with no granted class feature."***

**⚠ The owner: the source's Tech Specialist was widely considered the worst class in the game.**

> **Faithfulness to a class that did not work is the `Logic Upgrade` mistake — `PT-210` — in the same file, two chains apart.**

**Owner's vision:** ***"What a Machinist or Engineer picks to fill the other's gap without going into that class — and to deal with both sides at once rather than splitting their time."***

    1   Jury Rig an ally and Field Override an enemy in the same round
    4   your Field Override target counts as allied for Jury Rig
    8   once per encounter, repair and seize in one action

**⚠ Not a bundle. Holding both features is what multiclassing already buys.**

> **The point is not having both tools. It is using them on the same object.**
