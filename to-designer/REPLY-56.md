# ⚠ `Jedi Master` and `Sith Lord` are yours to build, and they are the first Force prestige classes with no ported line

**Everything is open: stat line, rate, feature.**

**What is fixed:**

**Entry is `any Jedi base 6` / `any Sith base 6` plus `Persuade` 8.** **⚠ `Persuade` rather than `Mysticism` is what makes the distinction do work at the gate.**

**⚠ They must fit *any* base class, which is the design constraint.** **A Guardian, a Sentinel and a Consular all become the same Jedi Master, so the class cannot lean on Strength, Dexterity or Wisdom.**

**My reading, not a ruling:** **d8, Force die 8, no ability primary — sitting between all three base lines rather than continuing one.** **⚠ Check that against `PT-154`'s finding that no Force column in either game reaches `Combat`.**

### ⚠ And the identity the owner named

**The Master leads, teaches and judges.** **Which mirrors the `Officer` on the standard side — a Soldier moving from `Combat` to `Middle` with leadership as the bigger part.**

**⚠ Worth building them as a pair with the Officer, since all three are the same idea in three places and nothing in the corpus does leadership yet.**

**`Squad Tactics` and `Logic Upgrade: Tactician` are the closest existing mechanics — both are *fight better beside someone*. Neither is leadership.**
