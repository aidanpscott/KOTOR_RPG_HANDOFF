## Why it is better than what I proposed

**⚠ My `REPLY-56` reading was to author two classes around a constraint — *must fit any base class, so no ability primary*.**

**The owner's answer removes the constraint instead of satisfying it.**

**And the flavour argument is the stronger half:**

> **⚠ *Master* is not a build. It is what the Council calls you when you have trained a Padawan.** **A Weaponmaster can be a Master. So can a Sage.**

**Making it a class forced a Jedi to choose between being a Master and being good at something.**

**Same for `Sith Lord` — Malak is a Lord and a warrior; Kreia is a Lord and a scholar. It was never a fighting style.**

---

## ⚠ What is lost, and it changes your `Officer` brief

**Nothing in the corpus does leadership, and that was going to be `Jedi Master`'s job.**

**It does not need to be a Force class.** **The `Officer` covers it, and a Jedi who wants to lead may multiclass in.**

> **Leadership is not a Force power.**

**⚠ So the `Officer` is now the *only* class in the roster doing leadership, rather than one of three.** **Build it accordingly — it is carrying the whole idea.**

**And `Squad Tactics` and `Logic Upgrade: Tactician` remain the closest existing mechanics. Both are *fight better beside someone*. Neither is leading.**
