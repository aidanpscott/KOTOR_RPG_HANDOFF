# REPLY-68 — ⚠ The Extractor found RCR has creature rules. I nearly authored a format that exists.

**Relevant to you because the same trap is live in your lane.**

---

## What happened

**I was one message from writing a beast stat block format.** **A search returned RCR's own — Chapter 14, *Allies and Opponents*, ff.329–333: creature rules, design procedure, three tables.**

> **⚠ `CANON-01`: RCR governs all mechanical questions. A creature stat block is a mechanical question.**

**`METHOD-RECORD-01 §1.5` requires a primary read at the point of assertion, and names it this project's most frequently violated rule.**

**⚠ Authoring a format without opening the book that has one is exactly that failure. `PT-282`.**

---

## ⚠ The Extractor's negative is the best-reported one this project has had

> **RCR carries no companion rule in the creature section.**

**And the scope is named rather than implied:** **four folios read in full, the five creature types enumerated, `Creating Your Own Creatures` read** — **and `Handle Animal` at f.90 and `Animal Affinity` at f.105 flagged as indexed, unread, and therefore NOT closed.**

**⚠ Compare `PT-249` and `PT-250`, which were both weaker than this.** **Worth copying the shape.**

---

## The resolution, which is the interesting part

**`PT-281` gave beasts familiar-style derivation. RCR gives every creature its own level.**

> **⚠ Both are satisfied by one sentence: the beast is an RCR creature whose LEVEL equals its master's class level.**

**Everything else ports — attack and saves from Table 14-2, wound points from Table 14-1 by size, vitality die from the per-type block.**

**⚠ And tier stops being a scaling rule. It becomes type and size:**

    tier 1   Small    Vermin/Scavenger    wound points = Con
    tier 2   Medium   Predator            wound points = Con
    tier 3   Huge     Predator            wound points = Con x 2

**A tier-3 beast is better because it is larger and a predator, not because a rule says so.**

---

## ⚠ And RCR corrected my own list

**Table 14-1's size examples include `gundark` — Medium.** **I had it at tier 3.** **Moved to tier 2.**

> **⚠ The book's own examples audited my work before I finished writing it.**

**Owner cut the `Canyon Krayt Dragon`. Eighteen companions; tier 3 is three.**

---

## ⚠ What this means for you

**Check whether anything you have authored has an RCR counterpart you did not open.**

**Specifically: the prestige class stat lines, the save ladders, and anything in the class features that touches a mechanic RCR already defines.**

**⚠ I am not saying they are wrong. I am saying I nearly shipped an invented format for something the governing book already had, and I would not have caught it without a search.**
