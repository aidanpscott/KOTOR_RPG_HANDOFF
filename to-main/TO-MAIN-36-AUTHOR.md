# TO MAIN — from AUTHOR. Chapter five, Armour, for review — a different kind of source than the last three.

## Why this one didn't need the same cross-check

`ITEMS-02` isn't a secondary-sourced summary the way `EQUIPMENT-01`'s weapon tables
were — it's the primary-source catalogue itself, converted from the game files under
the same seven rulings cited throughout this book. The verification pass that caught
real errors in Chapters Two through Four doesn't apply the same way here; `ITEMS-02`
largely *is* the more-primary source those chapters needed to check against.

## What the chapter does

Points back to Chapter One for the defence formula, the sum-of-nine rule, robes, and
droid plating — all already taught there. This chapter is the item catalogue: six
categories (Clothing 16, Disguise 2, Light 37, Medium 41, Heavy 34, Robes 42, totaling
173), five representative entries rather than a full transcription, and one priced pair
(`Padawan Robe`/`Dark Padawan Robe`) worth knowing about as a deliberate owner ruling
(`PT-980`) rather than an oversight — two mechanically identical robes now priced the
same.

## One thing flagged rather than resolved

Several Light-armour entries carry a `DecreaseAC` property with a negative value
instead of the `Armor N` flat bonus most entries use. Couldn't confidently determine
from the property list alone whether this means the item is genuinely below its
category's usual protection, or whether `DecreaseAC` is a separate mechanic layered on
a base value not shown in the same row. Stated as an open question, not a guess either
way.

## Scope, stated plainly

Five representative items out of 173, not a full audit — the right grain for a
browsable reference built on top of a catalogue that already exists in full, same as
how Chapters Two through Four sampled rather than transcribed their own weapon lists.
