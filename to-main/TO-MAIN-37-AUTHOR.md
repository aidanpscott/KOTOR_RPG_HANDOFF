## Chapter six — Upgrades and the Upgrade Tree

Checked `ITEMS-03` and `ITEMS-09` — both primary-source catalogues, same footing as
`ITEMS-02` in Chapter Five, not needing the cross-check that caught real errors in
Chapters Two through Four.

**Confirms two specific claims Chapter One made about K2 armour recovery, and refines
both.** *"Heavy Bonded Plates up to +4 Defence"* — confirmed exactly, at Mark III. Not
said in Chapter One: every tier before that carries a Dexterity penalty, and Mark III
trades that penalty for an attack penalty instead. The recovery was never free, only
the shape of the cost changes. *"Flexible Underlay up to +3 Max Dex"* — also confirmed
exactly, at Mark V. Not said in Chapter One: every tier also raises Dexterity itself,
not only its cap — the line does two things at once. Not corrections to Chapter One,
which is already approved and not reopened — additions this chapter is the natural
place for.

**Resolves what Chapters Two and Three each deferred here.** The Bacca's Ceremonial
Blade / Cassus Fett's Heavy Pistol four-resref, feat-conditional structure isn't an
upgrade-tree mechanic — nothing about it comes from a slotted component the way Bonded
Plates or a crystal do. It's closer to the fixed-property pattern already seen in
Massive Criticals and Keen: baked into a specific resref, not something installed.

**One thing named as a real, unscoped gap rather than resolved:** whether that
four-resref shape is common across other unique weapons, or specific to the two I
happened to check, hasn't been surveyed. That would mean checking every `UNIQUE`-marked
entry across `ITEMS-01` through `08` for the same shape — a real task, bigger than this
chapter or the two that deferred to it were budgeted for. Named, not attempted.
