# TO MAIN — from AUTHOR. A scoping question before four more chapters, plus a third instance of the corrupted-Name pattern.

## The scoping question

The outline's next row bundled `ITEMS-04` through `08` — droid equipment, worn gear,
usable items, and quest/miscellaneous items — into one chapter. Looked at all five
before drafting: they aren't one category the way Armour's six sub-categories were.

**Proposing four chapters instead of one, same reasoning as the Melee/Ranged/
Lightsabers split:**

- **Droid equipment** (`ITEMS-04`, 135) — drafted below, its own coherent set.
- **Worn gear** (`ITEMS-05`, 241 — belts, forearms, gauntlets, implants, masks) — the
  largest catalogue after weapons, mechanically its own thing.
- **Usable items** (`ITEMS-06`, 58 — adrenals, medical, trap kits) — active consumables.
- **Quest and miscellaneous items** (`ITEMS-07` + `ITEMS-08`, roughly 60) — both small
  enough to share a chapter, both genuinely miscellaneous.

Drafted the first (Droid Equipment) below and held the other three until this split is
confirmed, same as holding the ranged/lightsaber chapters until the three-way weapon
split was ruled on.

## Chapter seven — Droid Equipment

Eight categories, 135 items. Two things worth your attention:

**Every device-category item and most plating carry a `Use Limitation Feat (Droid
Upgrade N)` — the "bay gate" `FEATS-LIBRARY-01` already names as the deliberate
exception to how a feat normally reaches a character.** Cross-referenced rather than
re-derived.

**A third instance of the corrupted-Name-field pattern**, after `Baragwin Assault
Blade` and the Weapon Master item from Chapter Two's research. `spike-mount`'s first
row carries *"Prerequisites: Stealth is a Cross-Class Skill..."* — class-feature text —
instead of an item name. Left that category uncharacterized in the chapter rather than
guess at what it actually is from a name that isn't one. Same resolution path as
before would apply if someone wants to run it down — the raw `.uti` and a GFF parser,
not this markdown copy.
