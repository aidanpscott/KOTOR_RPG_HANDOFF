# TO MAIN — from AUTHOR. Split confirmed and drafted — Worn Gear, Usable Items, Quest and Miscellaneous. One deliberate-departure clarification, two authored surprises, one arithmetic gap left open.

## Worn Gear (`ITEMS-05`)

241 items, five categories. Two of the five (Gauntlets, Implant) mix `⚠ AUTHORED`
content in with extracted items — flagged plainly rather than presenting authored and
extracted items side by side without saying which is which. The provenance discipline
from much earlier in this project working as intended, at the item level this time.

## Usable Items (`ITEMS-06`)

Found something that looked like a contradiction and turned out not to be one.
`Medpac`'s own item text describes healing as 10 + Wisdom + Treat Injury skill (and
20/30 with the same shape for Advanced Medpac and Life Support Pack). `PT-1`'s ruling,
already cited in Chapters One and Three, gives flat dice instead — 2d8/4d8/6d8. Not
routing this as a conflict: the ruling deliberately drops the ability/skill dependency
while keeping the source's own ×1/×2/×3 scaling shape in average-damage terms. Framed
as a deliberate departure in the chapter, not an open question.

## Quest and Miscellaneous Items (`ITEMS-07` + `ITEMS-08`)

Two more authored categories, and one is bigger than a flag. `Shock-arm` (`PT-713`) is
droid chassis hardware, and `PT-714` rules it isn't a melee weapon at all — the same
category as a beast's claws, worth knowing for whoever drafts droid material in the
Bestiary or Threat Database later. **`Boots` (`PT-690`) invents an entire equipment
slot KOTOR never had** — the games have no foot slot at all, confirmed against
`baseitems.2da`'s own slot list, and the resref pattern is confirmed unused in either
game. Framed clearly as new equipment, not a port, since it's the most direct example
in this book of the project adding to KOTOR's design rather than translating it.

**One thing left open rather than reconciled:** `ITEMS-08`'s header says 42 items; the
five categories I found sum to 69. Might be the same stale-header shape `ITEMS-07`'s
own corrected history already shows this catalogue is prone to, might be a category
overlap I'm not seeing. Stated honestly rather than forced to match.
