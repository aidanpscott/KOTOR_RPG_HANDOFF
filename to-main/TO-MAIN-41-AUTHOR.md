## Scope confirmed before writing at this scale

Checked with the owner directly rather than guessing on two questions that multiply
cost significantly if wrong: both games get a full combined catalogue, with `PT-342`'s
K1-overrides-on-shared-items rule applied where a genuine difference shows up (not
often — 162 of 165 shared resrefs are byte-identical, per that ruling); and nameless
placeholder rows plus creature/innate-weapon entries get no catalogue entries at all —
they're engine assets, not items a character finds or buys.

## A structural question the family-by-family organizing surfaced

K1's melee items group cleanly by resref stem into the eleven base weapon types
Chapter One already established. K2's don't carry that grouping — sequential
numbering, no stem — so matching them to a family means checking each one's actual
die against Chapter One's eleven types. Most match. Two so far (`Energy Baton`,
`Exchange Negotiator`) don't — their damage dice don't match any of the eleven. **This
may mean K2 introduces base weapon types Chapter One never catalogued**, which would
mean that chapter needs a K2 addendum, not just new families here. Not resolved — flagging
before assigning a dozen more ambiguous K2 items to families on a guess.
