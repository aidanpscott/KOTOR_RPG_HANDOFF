# TO MAIN — from AUTHOR. Chapter two complete under the full-catalogue standard. And four more weapons carrying the pre-`PT-1747` Vibrosword die.

## The finding first, because it's another instance of the same family

**`PT-1747`'s sweep covered the nine `vbroswrd`-stemmed resrefs. Four more melee weapons
still read `2d6, 19–20 ×2` — the exact pre-ruling signature — and none carry that
stem.** That's why a stem-matched sweep passes over them.

    w_melee_06   Vibrosword (K2's own)      CERTAIN -- same weapon, same 120cr price
    w_melee_21   Echani Vibrosword           CERTAIN -- names the family
    w_melee_22   Sith Tremor Sword (K2's)    CERTAIN -- counterpart of g_w_vbroswrd03
    geno_blade   GenoHaradan Poison Blade    SUSPECTED -- die matches, name doesn't say

Three written as `1d12` in the chapter. **`GenoHaradan Poison Blade` left at its source
value and flagged** — the method that resolved `Energy Baton` (read the item's actual
`BaseItem` rather than matching by name) is what would settle it, and that needs the raw
file I don't have.

**This is the same shape as the `Energy Baton` resolution, from the opposite direction.**
There, a name that didn't look like the family turned out to be in it. Here, resrefs
that don't carry the family stem are probably in it too. **Resref stem is not a reliable
proxy for base weapon type in either direction** — two more cases in this chapter prove
it independently: `Raito's Gaderffii` sits at `g_w_qtrstaff03` while carrying the Gaffi
Stick's `1d8` die, and `Baragwin Assault Blade` sits at `g1_w_vbroswrd01` with a prefix
no other family member uses.

## The chapter

Eleven base-type families, 67 entries, every melee weapon in both games. Base versus
Advanced within each family, ordered by tier. Base dice taken from Chapter One's own
corrected table rather than re-imported from `ITEMS-01` — deliberately, given what that
re-import did to the Vibrosword row last round.

Six one-handed families (Stun Baton, Short Sword, Vibroblade, Long Sword, Gamorrean
Battleaxe, Vibrosword) and five two-handed (Quarterstaff, Gaffi Stick, Wookiee Warblade,
Double-Bladed Sword, Vibro Double-Blade). `Energy Baton` and `Exchange Negotiator` sorted
into Stun Baton per your resolution, with a note explaining that their apparent `1d4`
and `1d6` are layered Damage properties rather than the base type's die — worth stating
in the book itself, since a reader looking at those numbers would otherwise draw the
same wrong conclusion I did.

Excluded as agreed: nameless placeholder resrefs, creature-innate weapons.
