# F-ORD-CRUSADE — the Ords are crusade infrastructure

**`F-ORD-NETWORK` asked: *who was here first, and where did they go?* The Ordnance/Regional Depot article answers a bigger question than that, and it re-frames all forty-three worlds of Tier 7 before I research one of them.**

---

## What an Ord actually was

> *"An **Ordnance/Regional Depot**, abbreviated **Ord**, was a planet that served as a **military outpost** during the height of the Galactic Republic. These depots were **set up as the Core Worlds began to colonise rimward worlds from around 13,000 BBY**."*

> **⚠ *"During the **Pius Dea Era**, the Ordnance/Regional Depot worlds served as a network of **forward military and scout bases for Republic Navy units and Cathedral ships led by missionaries** expanding into the Frontier. **These worlds were used to deploy Pius Dea forces during Contispex's anti-alien crusades.**"***

> *"Many of the planets settled as military bases were designed to **protect colonists, specifically Human colonists**\u2026 It was often done **out of a reaction of speciesism to protect Humans from indigenous aliens**."*

---

## ⭐ So the network is not a supply chain. It is a crusade's road system.

**`Alsakan` already gave me the other half:** the **Seventh Alsakan Conflict was the Pius Dea Civil War**, the **Caamasi convinced the Jedi to end the Recusal**, and **Contispex XIX was imprisoned for life on Caamas.**

> ⟡ **Tier 7 is forty-three forward bases of a religious anti-alien crusade, and the war that ended it is already in my records on two other worlds.**

**That is the spine. Every Ord in Tier 7 can now be asked three questions instead of one:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **① Who was here first?** | *The depots existed **to protect Human colonists from indigenous aliens**. On many worlds there were indigenous aliens* |
| **② What was launched from here?** | *Cathedral ships and Navy units, deploying **Contispex's crusades** into the Frontier* |
| **③ What is left at 3956?** | **Eight to nine thousand years after founding, and long after the crusades collapsed** |

## The two closed here

**`Ord Biniir`** — *"colonised by the Galactic Republic as an Ordnance/Regional Depot **circa 12,000 BBY**."* **Relgim sector, K-5.** ✅ **My note was right and needed no correction** — the only one in this batch.

**`Ord Cantrell`** — ⭐ *"settled by a colony of **Duros** that **lost contact with the galaxy and reverted to simple technology** \u2014 **wooden sailing ships** \u2014 **before dying out**."*

> ⟡ **A species that crossed the stars was stranded here long enough to go back to sail, and then ran out. At 3956 they are already gone.**

**`Swim` rests on those sailing ships** \u2014 *you do not build them without water. **That is an inference and I made it deliberately after refusing four looser ones**: a sailing ship names its own medium, where a former shipyard does not name a surviving ruin.* ⚠ **`Archaeology` refused a fifth time even here: a colony that died out must have left buildings, but nothing attests one, and "must have" is the phrase I have spent this batch removing.**

# ⭐⭐ UPDATE — the era is dated, and the depots were OUTSIDE the Republic

**Two worlds state it independently, in almost the same words.**

> **`Ord Tiddell`** — *"served as an Ordnance/Regional Depot outpost for the Galactic Republic **during the Pius Dea Era, a period of time that lasted between approximately 12,000 BBY and 11,000 BBY**. **During that time, the world was located just outside the Republic's borders.**"*

> **`Ord Sabaok`** — *"served as an Ordnance/Regional Depot outpost… during the Pius Dea Era… **despite it being located outside the borders of Republic space**."*

## What this changes

**① The crusade window is fixed: c. 12,000 – 11,000 BBY** — *roughly **eight thousand years** before the campaign.* **The depots begin c.13,000 BBY and are repurposed within the next thousand years.**

**② The Ords were forward bases *beyond* the frontier, not garrisons *on* it.** ⟡ **That is exactly what a crusade needs: staging posts in territory you do not yet hold.** *The network was not defending a border. It was standing outside one.*

**③ ⚠ And "Core Worlds" is not a synonym for "Republic."** *`Ord Sabaok` is a **Core Worlds planet in the Southern Core** that lay **outside Republic space** during the crusade era.* **I had been reading the two as interchangeable without noticing** — the same assumption that failed on **`Skako`**, **`Farrfin`** and **`Salliche`**, none of which has attested Republic membership at 3956.

**④ `Ord Sabaok` had a hyperlane to the Giju system** — **the Giju Run**, which `Fondor` recorded as blazed by **Herglic** scouts. ⟡ *A crusade outpost with a road to Herglic space.*

## ⚠ And a note withdrawn on the strength of it

**`Ord Sabaok`'s old note read *"a Core Worlds depot, one of the original **inward** garrisons and proud of the fact."*** **It was exactly backwards, and `Archaeology` — which rested entirely on it — has been withdrawn. Floor 2 → 4.**

## Three consequences

**① ⭐ One of the invaded worlds is in this selection.** *The Fourth Crusade marched **through Klatooine** — Tier 8, already recorded as bound to the Hutts by the **Treaty of Vontor**.* **Eight thousand years before our campaign, a Human religious army crossed it.**

**② ⭐ `Ord Ortag` was eaten by the enemy it was built to attack.** *"Republic forces moved from Ord Ortag to the Hollastin system... **Eventually, Ord Ortag itself became incorporated into that region**."* ⟡ **The staging post for an anti-Hutt crusade ended up inside Hutt Space** — which is exactly what my note already said, without knowing why.

**③ "Outside the borders of the Republic" is now stated by five worlds** — `Ord Sabaok`, `Ord Tiddell`, `Ord Ortag`, `Ord Lonesome`, `Ord Marsax` — **in near-identical words each time.** ⟡ *That is how a reference book describes **a policy**, not a coincidence.*

## ⚠ And two notes of mine were confirmed rather than corrected

**`Ord Ortag`** — *"a Republic depot sitting inside Hutt Space."* **True, and now with a mechanism.**
**`Ord Lonesome`** — *"Albanin, and the name is the report. Isolation is the training."* ⟡ **It was lonesome because it was a Republic outpost standing outside the Republic.**

> **⭐⭐ Six crusades in, and the Twelfth is the first to name who was on the other end.** *Every earlier entry named a **world** — Toydaria, Circumtore, Cyborrea, Delacrix, Old Barag.* ⟡ **This one names two peoples**, which is what the network was for: *"a reaction of **speciesism** to protect Humans from **indigenous aliens**."* **Here are two of the aliens.**

## ⭐ And the machine is described in one sentence

> *"Republic forces moved from the **Mon Gazza system in the Republic's territory** to the **Teirasa system via Ord Zynthar**."*

⟡ **Gather inside the border. Stage beyond it. Strike.** **That is what an Ord was for, stated as a route.**

## `Ord Gimmel`'s two wars are now both dated

**The Twelfth is 11,660. The Fifteenth is 11,591.** ⟡ **Sixty-nine years apart — a classified world founded for one war, kept for the next, and abandoned when they stopped.**

## ⚠ And a different shape of war

**`Ord Vaug` was *"centred on nearby Old Barag."*** *Every other crusade **moved from** an Ord **toward** something.* ⟡ **Ord Vaug was not a springboard but a base beside the fighting.**

## ⚠ One absence recorded as an absence

**Eight Ords state they lay outside Republic space. `Ord Zat` does not.** **That is a gap in the source, not evidence of inclusion**, and it is recorded as a gap.

# ⚠⚠ FIFTH UPDATE — "Ord" is not a date-stamp, and one of these worlds is from the Clone Wars

> **`Ord Antalaha`** — *"a planet located along the **Shwuy Exchange**, in the **Colonies** region, that was **colonised and established as an Ordnance/Regional Depot by the Galactic Republic during the Clone Wars**."*

## What this breaks

**I had been treating "Ord" as a reliable date-stamp** — a name that meant **c.13,000 BBY** and a Pius Dea foundation. ⟡ **It does not. The Republic revived the designation eleven thousand years later, in a different war, having forgotten why the word existed.**

> ### **Any Ord I have not individually checked could in principle be a late foundation.**
>
> **That is now on the record rather than assumed away.** *Two worlds are already carried on the class-wide rule alone — **`Ord Simres`** and **`Ord Bueri`**, neither of whose own articles confirms what it is — and both are marked accordingly.*

**`Ord Antalaha` reclassified to `place`: uncolonised at 3956.** *Third world in the project reclassified for that reason, after `Taanab` and `Kalarba`.*

## And a class-wide statement worth having, marked as below rank

*A French fan encyclopedia summarising **The Essential Guide to Warfare** puts it plainly:* **"Virtually every Ord served, at one time or another, as a departure point for an assault in the context of a crusade."**

⚠ **Below rank, and recorded rather than relied on** — *but it is consistent with every individually-attested case: **Ord Wylan, Ord Thabl, Ord Namurt, Ord Marsax, Ord Ortag, Ord Lonesome, Ord Klina, Ord Zynthar, Ord Vaug** and `Ord Gimmel`, ten worlds, each with a named or numbered crusade attached.*

## ⚠ And one I could have taken and did not

**`Ord Canfre`** *"**millennia later** hosted a facility of the **Jedi Order's Exploration Corps**."*

> **"Millennia later" from 11,000 BBY could land anywhere across seven thousand years — including our own era.** ⟡ **But *could* is not *does*, and after twenty-six wrong notes I am not putting a Jedi installation on a world because the date is vague enough to permit it.** **Flagged as worth a targeted check if the owner wants Jedi outposts mapped.**
