# F-SITH-ROADS — the road map of Sith space, and why sixteen worlds should stay blocked

**Written instead of sixteen individual entries. The probe said not to grind them.**

---

## ⚠ First, the finding that stopped the grind

**Tiers 4 and 5 looked like 41 unresearched worlds. They are *five block notes*, and the character counts prove it:**

| Block | Worlds | Note length |
|---|---|---|
| **Sith Worlds proper** | Bhargebba · Bosthirda · Nfolgai · Savek · Svolten | **all exactly 150** |
| **Esstran approach** | Antared · Buamlon Central · Critoki · Daluba · Haffrin · Kursid · Lapez · Marranis · Melldia · Saarn | **all exactly 165** |
| **Tion / Cronese** | Argoon · Barancar · Caluula · Kanaver · Kaon · Spinax | **all exactly 159** |
| **Gree Enclave (outer)** | ten worlds | **all exactly 240** |
| **Gree Enclave (inner)** | five worlds | **all exactly 246** |

⟡ **Identical lengths are not a coincidence. They are one note written once and applied to a set.**

## ⭐ And unlike the Mandalore eight, the block is *correct*

**I probed the Sith Worlds block before committing to research, because `Gargon`, `Breshig`, `Harswee` and `Vorpa'ya` all turned out to be distinct when I dissolved a block of eight. This one does not.**

| | What the sources actually hold |
|---|---|
| **`Bosthirda`** | *"A terrestrial astronomical object… **It had a brown and black surface.**"* ⟡ **That is the whole article.** |
| **`Nfolgai`** | ⚠ **Only a *system* article exists** — *"contained the planet of Nfolgai."* Nothing more |
| **`Bhargebba`** | ⚠ **The world is *Bhargebba Six*** — and its only event is **3643 BBY**, SWTOR, **excluded** |
| **`Svolten`, `Savek`** | ⚠ **Appear only inside route lists** |

> **The block note is true of these worlds. Dissolving it would produce five entries saying "brown and black" and "nothing more offered."**

# ⭐⭐⭐ What the probe found instead: six named roads

> *"Along the **Kamat Krote** route were **Bosthirda**, **Dromund Kaas**, and **Jaguada**. Jaguada was also included in the **Kamat Aegit** route along with **Begeren**, **Kalsunor**, **Ashas Ree**, and **Khar Delba**. **Nicht Ka** was along the **Nache Bhelfia** route that connected **Korriban to Ziost**, and the **Zorfe Trete** route connected **Ch'hodos to Ashas Ree**. The **Descri Wris** route connected **Korriz**, **Athiss**, **Svolten**, **Bhargebba**, and **Nfolgai**. **Krayiss II and Rhelg** were on the far side of the Nache Bhelfia route across from Nicht Ka. **Thule, the Iliabath system, and Corbos were *outside* the Stygian Caldera**."*

| Route | Worlds on it |
|---|---|
| **Kamat Krote** | Bosthirda · **Dromund Kaas** · Jaguada |
| **Kamat Aegit** | Jaguada · Begeren · Kalsunor · Ashas Ree · **Khar Delba** |
| **Nache Bhelfia** | ⭐ **Korriban ↔ Ziost**, with **Nicht Ka** on it and **Krayiss II** and **Rhelg** opposite |
| **Zorfe Trete** | Ch'hodos ↔ Ashas Ree |
| **Descri Wris** | Korriz · **Athiss** · Svolten · Bhargebba · Nfolgai |
| **Daragon Trail** | *named for the sector, worlds not listed in this passage* |

**⭐ The spine is *Nache Bhelfia*: it connects the two capitals.** ⟡ *`Korriban` is the tomb world and `Ziost` the seat — and **one road runs between them, with `Nicht Ka` sitting on it.***

**⚠ And three worlds are *outside* the nebula:** ***Thule***, the **Iliabath system**, and ***Corbos***. ⟡ *`Thule` and `Corbos` are both in this selection, and this is the first thing that tells me **they are not inside the Caldera's shelter**.*

**⭐ And where the survivors went:** *"When the Sith Empire fell, **refugees fled back to the worlds of Ambria, Kesh, Thule, Tund, Vjun, Yavin 4**, and into the Unknown Regions."*

## ⚠ Two corrections that fell out of the probe

**① `Bhargebba` → `Bhargebba Six`.** *Thirteenth naming discrepancy. **The register holds the system name, not the world's.***

**② `Nicht Ka` is a world, not a region.** *It is listed among the Sith Worlds and sits on the Nache Bhelfia route.* ⚠ **It currently has no menu, and I had it filed with the regions. That is wrong and it needs one.**

---

## What I recommend

**Leave the sixteen block worlds blocked, and point every one of them at this file.** ⟡ *A GM asking "what is Svolten" is better served by **"a stop on the Descri Wris, between Athiss and Bhargebba, inside the Caldera"** than by five words about a brown surface.*

⚠ **The Gree Enclave blocks are a separate question and I have not probed them.** *Their notes say the Gree **\"do not explain themselves\"** and the space is **sealed** — which may be the same situation or may be a real gap. **One probe would tell us, and I would rather ask than assume.**
