## What this changes about Batch E

**It does not mean fifty-four searches.** It means the tier has a **known bias with a known signature**, so the research has a specific question to carry:

> **⚠ For any Core world whose menu is `Persuade · Appraise · X`: what does this world actually *make, hold, or refuse*?**

**Tier 5 answered that question thirty-one times out of thirty-six and finished at floor 2.** *Rafa's crystal orchards, Belsavis's rifts, Upekzar's caves and Rhelg's fields were all sitting in the sources the whole time.*

⟡ **And where the answer is genuinely nothing, the world gets `openness: open` and says so — not a third generic skill.**
