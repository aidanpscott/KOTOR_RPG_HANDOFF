# F-WONDERS — the Twenty Wonders of the Galaxy, and three of them are already mine

**Surfaced across three separate reads in Batch E. It is a canonical, closed list of twenty, and it makes a ready-made spine of destinations.**

---

## The catalogue exists and has an author

> **Compiled by the historian *Vicendi* in his ***Arturum Galactinum***, published *10,000 BBY*.**

**Twenty entries. Every one is a real, visitable place with a name.**

---

## Three found so far, all inside this selection

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **Alsakan Mosaics** | **Alsakan** #— | *"An ancient Wonder of the Galaxy that stretched in **five lanes of tiles** to each of five equidistant mountains"*, at **Rucapar** |
| **Celebratus Archive** | **Obroa-skai** | Founded **16,782 BBY** to gather *"the complete data records of over **three hundred thousand species**"* |
| **Cathedral of Winds** | **Vortex** | ⭐ **A colossal crystal structure that plays itself in the wind** — and the Vors fly through it once a year opening and closing its windows **like an organ** |

---

## ⭐ And the Cathedral has the best date in the tier

> *"The structure became one of the Twenty Wonders of the Galaxy but was **generally considered a myth** until the Vors joined the Old Republic."*
> *"**Only when Vortex joined the Galactic Republic in 3,977 BBY did their symbol and their species enter the conception of reality of the average galactic citizen.**"*

**Twenty-one years before the campaign.**

> ⟡ **A Wonder that was a legend for seven thousand years and became a fact within living memory.** *Anyone over thirty at 3956 remembers when the Cathedral of Winds was a story people repeated and did not believe.* **Vortex is the newest real place in the galaxy.**

*During the **Pius Dea crusades**, the scout **Hicco** became the first outsider to see it. **Nobody believed him either.***

---

## Why this is worth chasing

**Seventeen remain unidentified.** ⟡ *If even half of them sit on worlds already in the 292, the Wonders become **a curated tour the setting supplies for free** — a reason to visit a world that is not a battle, and a list a GM can hand to players as a goal.*

**And it is a cheap check.** *One search on the list itself should name most of them, and each name maps to a world I can test against the selection in seconds.*
